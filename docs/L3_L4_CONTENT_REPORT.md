Those were judged by a reviewer reading the machine's list of compounds, which is partly judgement. Words that were judged not guessable by a learner were glossed in the English cell of the row, as "(词 cí: meaning)", or the sentence was changed. ## 4. Project decisions the owner should know

- **拉 là** ("leave behind", L3-1 vocabulary; used in L3-1 and L3-6, for example 丢三拉四 and 拉在): this is the book's spelling and the book's reading. The standard character is 落 (luò, or là). Kept as the book prints it; a reviewer's suggestion to replace it was overruled.
- **Neutral tones follow the book's vocabulary list** even where dictionaries differ: hǎochu, dìdao, zhúyi, wèidao, xīnxian, lìhai, mǎhu, tàidu, rènao, zǎochen.
- **想像** in L4-2 vocabulary 11 (line 45, also lines 173, 224, 244) is kept as the book prints it; standard is 想象.
- **人材** in L4-9 vocabulary 5 (line 40) is tagged `v` as printed; it looks like a book error for `n`. Kept.
- **Numbering:** the files `level-4/L1.md` to `L10.md` are the book's Lessons 11 to 20 (`lesson: 1` to `10`).
- **Pinyin for two-syllable direction complements:** neutral tone with a space, for example xiǎng qilai, hēi xialai. Potential forms keep their tones (for example tán de xiàlái, qù de liǎo). 看起来 and 听起来 follow the book's list: kàn qǐlai, tīng qǐlai (so these keep qǐ).
- **Quotes** inside Chinese text are Chinese quotation marks “…”, as in Level 2.
- Pinyin spacing: 了, 着, 过 after a verb are joined (mǎile); sentence-final 了 and 的/得/地 stand alone.

## 6. What was not verified

- **Nobody listened to the audio.** Pinyin and Chinese were checked by reading only, by the pinyin library and by reviewing models.
- **Level 4 vocabulary has no second source.** Its pinyin and meanings were checked only by the pinyin library and the reviewers' reading. Level 3 vocabulary was also compared with an Anki deck (it agreed; the deck's own typos aside).
- **The facts files were transcribed from page images by a model** and lint-checked by machine; they were not proofread against the book by a person.
- **The cumulative-vocabulary check is partly judgement** (section 3).
- **Interruptions.** The run was cut off by usage limits. The Level 4 L1 to L5 writers and the Level 3 reviewers ran in the earlier session. One session ended in the middle of reviews (the L4-10 round-1 review and the round-2 checks of L4-5 to L4-8); those were rerun from scratch, so a small style seam may remain between early and late files.
- One fixer ran a read-only `git status` once by mistake; nothing was written.
- No test of the app build was possible: `npm run build:content` fails until the parser is adapted (see `docs/L3_L4_PARSER_NOTES.md`).
