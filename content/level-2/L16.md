# Lesson 16 · 约会 · Dating

## Objectives
- After this lesson you can invite someone to a film or a concert and settle the day and meeting place.
- After this lesson you can turn down an invitation politely, give a reason, and suggest something else.
- After this lesson you can say whether a result can or cannot be reached (看得懂, 买不到, 想不起来).
- After this lesson you can describe the state an action leaves someone in with 得 plus a longer phrase.
- After this lesson you can use 就 for "only" and describe movement with two-part directional complements.

## Grammar

### G1 · Descriptive complements (II)
**Pattern:** `Subject + Verb / Adjective + 得 + phrase describing the resulting state`

You already use 得 plus a short adjective to say how well something is done (他说得很好). The part after 得 can also be a longer phrase, even a small sentence, that paints the state the subject ends up in because of the action or feeling: "so tired that…", "laughed until…". The description normally refers back to the subject, although it may bring its own subject (肚子 in the third example). If the verb has an object, say the verb twice and attach 得 to the second one, which is the step learners most often skip.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 他累得午饭都不想吃了。 | Tā lèi de wǔfàn dōu bù xiǎng chī le. | He was so tired that he didn't even want lunch. |
| 我们聊得忘了时间。 | Wǒmen liáo de wàngle shíjiān. | We chatted so much that we lost track of the time. |
| 弟弟笑得肚子都疼了。 | Dìdi xiào de dùzi dōu téng le. | My little brother laughed until his stomach hurt. |
| 她打扫房间打扫得没力气说话了。 | Tā dǎsǎo fángjiān dǎsǎo de méi lìqi shuōhuà le. | She cleaned her room until she had no strength left to talk. |

**Watch out:** 得 must follow a verb or adjective, never the object: ✗ 她打扫房间得很累 → 她打扫房间打扫得很累.

### G2 · Potential complements (I)
**Pattern:** `Verb + 得 / 不 + result or direction complement`

Slip 得 or 不 between a verb and its complement to say whether the result can be reached: 看懂 (understand by reading) becomes 看得懂 (able to understand) or 看不懂 (unable to understand). The negative form is by far the more common; the positive one appears mainly in questions and in replies to them. Ask with 吗, or by putting both forms side by side (看得懂看不懂). When the meaning is "I try but the result does not come", Chinese wants this form and not 不能, which sounds more like "not allowed to". Potential complements are not used in 把 sentences.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这个电影的票现在买不到了。 | Zhège diànyǐng de piào xiànzài mǎi bu dào le. | You can't get tickets for this film any more. |
| 老师说的话你听得懂吗？ | Lǎoshī shuō de huà nǐ tīng de dǒng ma? | Can you understand what the teacher says? |
| 功课太多了，我今天做不完。 | Gōngkè tài duō le, wǒ jīntiān zuò bu wán. | There is too much homework; I can't finish it today. |
| 他叫什么名字？我想不起来了。 | Tā jiào shénme míngzi? Wǒ xiǎng bu qǐlái le. | What is his name? I can't recall it. |

**Watch out:** ✗ 我不能听懂 → 我听不懂.

### G3 · 就 (only, just)
**Pattern:** `就 + (Verb +) Number + Measure word + Noun` · `就 + Noun / Pronoun + …`

Besides "then" and "sooner than expected", 就 can mean "only" and stress how small a number or group is. It goes before the verb, or, when no verb is there, directly before the number, noun or pronoun: 就两张, 就我一个人. It is the relaxed, spoken counterpart of 只, with one difference worth remembering: 只 has to be followed by a verb, while 就 can stand right in front of a noun or pronoun. In this meaning 就 is said with a little stress.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我们班就五个学生。 | Wǒmen bān jiù wǔ ge xuésheng. | Our class has only five students. |
| 我就有两张票，不能请大家都去。 | Wǒ jiù yǒu liǎng zhāng piào, bù néng qǐng dàjiā dōu qù. | I only have two tickets, so I can't invite everybody. |
| 大家都去旅行了，就我一个人在宿舍。 | Dàjiā dōu qù lǚxíng le, jiù wǒ yí ge rén zài sùshè. | Everyone has gone travelling; I'm the only one in the dorm. |
| 今天的功课很少，就写十个汉字。 | Jīntiān de gōngkè hěn shǎo, jiù xiě shí ge Hànzì. | There is very little homework today: just writing ten characters. |

**Watch out:** ✗ 只我一个人去 → 就我一个人去 (or 只有我一个人去).

### G4 · Directional complements (II)
**Pattern:** `Verb + 上 / 下 / 进 / 出 / 回 / 过 / 起 + 来 / 去`

A simple directional complement is just 来 or 去 after a verb (进来, 回去). The compound kind has two parts: the first gives the path (up, down, in, out, back, across, up from rest) and 来 or 去 adds whether the movement is towards the speaker or away. The position of the object is the tricky part. A place word must go before 来 or 去 (走进教室来); an ordinary thing can sit before 来 or 去 or after the whole complement (买回三本书来 or 买回来三本书), and with 把 it moves in front of the verb. A few combinations have meanings beyond movement, for example 想起来 (to recall) and 记下来 (to note down). These complement syllables are spoken lightly, so the pinyin here leaves them without tone marks.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 老师走进教室来了。 | Lǎoshī zǒu jin jiàoshì lai le. | The teacher walked into the classroom. |
| 请你把那本书拿下来。 | Qǐng nǐ bǎ nà běn shū ná xialai. | Please take that book down. |
| 他从书店买回来三本书。 | Tā cóng shūdiàn mǎi huilai sān běn shū. | He bought three books at the bookshop and brought them back. |
| 我们把桌子搬上楼去吧。 | Wǒmen bǎ zhuōzi bān shang lóu qu ba. | Let's carry the table upstairs. |

**Watch out:** a place never follows 来 or 去: ✗ 老师走进来教室了 → 老师走进教室来了.

## Dialogues

### Dialogue 1 · Two tickets
*Setting: After class, 马克 finally works up the courage to ask 林月 to see a film with him.*

| Speaker | Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 马克 | 林月，学校旁边的电影院后天晚上演一个新电影，你听说了吗？ | Lín Yuè, xuéxiào pángbiān de diànyǐngyuàn hòutiān wǎnshang yǎn yí ge xīn diànyǐng, nǐ tīngshuō le ma? | Lin Yue, the cinema next to campus is showing a new film the evening after next. Have you heard? |
| 林月 | 听说了。可是大家都说票已经卖完了，买不到了。 | Tīngshuō le. Kěshì dàjiā dōu shuō piào yǐjīng màiwán le, mǎi bu dào le. | I have. But everyone says the tickets are already sold out and can't be had. |
| 马克 | 我买到了！我早上六点就去了，等了两个钟头，累得午饭都不想吃了。 | Wǒ mǎidào le! Wǒ zǎoshang liù diǎn jiù qù le, děngle liǎng ge zhōngtóu, lèi de wǔfàn dōu bù xiǎng chī le. | I got some! I went at six this morning and waited two hours. I was so worn out I didn't even want lunch. |
| 林月 | 买电影票费了那么大的力气！你买了几张？ | Mǎi diànyǐng piào fèile nàme dà de lìqi! Nǐ mǎile jǐ zhāng? | All that effort for film tickets! How many did you buy? |
| 马克 | 就两张。一张是我的，一张是你的。就我们俩去，好吗？ | Jiù liǎng zhāng. Yì zhāng shì wǒ de, yì zhāng shì nǐ de. Jiù wǒmen liǎ qù, hǎo ma? | Only two. One is mine and one is yours. Just the two of us, OK? |
| 林月 | 就我们俩？中国电影你看得懂吗？ | Jiù wǒmen liǎ? Zhōngguó diànyǐng nǐ kàn de dǒng ma? | Just the two of us? Can you actually follow a Chinese film? |
| 马克 | 看得懂一点儿。看不懂的地方，你可以告诉我。 | Kàn de dǒng yìdiǎnr. Kàn bu dǒng de dìfang, nǐ kěyǐ gàosu wǒ. | A little. You can explain the bits I can't follow. |
| 林月 | 我对你的第一印象是不爱说话，没想到你现在也会请人看电影了。 | Wǒ duì nǐ de dì yī yìnxiàng shì bú ài shuōhuà, méi xiǎngdào nǐ xiànzài yě huì qǐng rén kàn diànyǐng le. | My first impression of you was that you didn't like talking. I never thought you'd be asking people out to films. |
| 马克 | 我们在同一个班一年了，已经成了好朋友了。那你去不去？ | Wǒmen zài tóng yí ge bān yì nián le, yǐjīng chéngle hǎo péngyou le. Nà nǐ qù bu qù? | We've been in the same class for a year and have become good friends. So, are you coming or not? |
| 林月 | 去！后天晚上几点？ | Qù! Hòutiān wǎnshang jǐ diǎn? | I'm coming! What time, the evening after next? |
| 马克 | 七点半。我七点在你宿舍楼下等你，我们一起走过去。 | Qī diǎn bàn. Wǒ qī diǎn zài nǐ sùshè lóu xià děng nǐ, wǒmen yìqǐ zǒu guoqu. | Half past seven. I'll wait for you downstairs at your dorm at seven and we'll walk over together. |
| 林月 | 好，一言为定！ | Hǎo, yì yán wéi dìng! | Great, it's a deal! |

### Dialogue 2 · A busy weekend
*Setting: 陈乐, who met 安娜 once at a party, phones to invite her to a concert, and 安娜 lets him down gently.*

| Speaker | Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 陈乐 | 喂，安娜吗？我是陈乐。你还记得我吗？ | Wéi, Ānnà ma? Wǒ shì Chén Lè. Nǐ hái jìde wǒ ma? | Hello, is that Anna? This is Chen Le. Do you still remember me? |
| 安娜 | 对不起，我想不起来了。我们在哪儿见过？ | Duìbuqǐ, wǒ xiǎng bu qǐlái le. Wǒmen zài nǎr jiànguo? | Sorry, I can't place you. Where did we meet? |
| 陈乐 | 上个月林月的生日晚会上，我们一起唱过歌，你还把电话号码告诉我了。 | Shàng ge yuè Lín Yuè de shēngrì wǎnhuì shang, wǒmen yìqǐ chàngguo gē, nǐ hái bǎ diànhuà hàomǎ gàosu wǒ le. | At Lin Yue's birthday party last month. We sang together, and you even gave me your phone number. |
| 安娜 | 哦，我想起来了！那天你唱得大家都不想回家了。 | Ò, wǒ xiǎng qilai le! Nàtiān nǐ chàng de dàjiā dōu bù xiǎng huí jiā le. | Oh, now I remember! You sang so well that day that nobody wanted to go home. |
| 陈乐 | 对，就是我！这个周末有一个音乐会，我想请你一起去听，你有空吗？ | Duì, jiù shì wǒ! Zhège zhōumò yǒu yí ge yīnyuèhuì, wǒ xiǎng qǐng nǐ yìqǐ qù tīng, nǐ yǒu kòng ma? | Yes, that was me! There is a concert this weekend and I'd like to take you. Are you free? |
| 安娜 | 谢谢你，可是这个周末不行。我要搬家，东西还没整理好，新房间也要打扫。 | Xièxie nǐ, kěshì zhège zhōumò bù xíng. Wǒ yào bānjiā, dōngxi hái méi zhěnglǐ hǎo, xīn fángjiān yě yào dǎsǎo. | Thank you, but this weekend won't work. I'm moving, my things aren't sorted yet, and the new room needs cleaning too. |
| 陈乐 | 我可以帮你搬！我力气很大，重的东西我都搬得动。 | Wǒ kěyǐ bāng nǐ bān! Wǒ lìqi hěn dà, zhòng de dōngxi wǒ dōu bān de dòng. | I can help you move! I'm strong, I can lift all the heavy stuff. |
| 安娜 | 不用了，谢谢。马克和林月会来帮我把东西搬上楼去。 | Búyòng le, xièxie. Mǎkè hé Lín Yuè huì lái bāng wǒ bǎ dōngxi bān shang lóu qu. | No need, thanks. Mark and Lin Yue are coming to help me carry everything upstairs. |
| 陈乐 | 那下个周末呢？ | Nà xià ge zhōumò ne? | What about next weekend, then? |
| 安娜 | 下个周末我要去旅行。等我旅行回来，我们跟林月、马克一起吃饭，怎么样？ | Xià ge zhōumò wǒ yào qù lǚxíng. Děng wǒ lǚxíng huílai, wǒmen gēn Lín Yuè, Mǎkè yìqǐ chīfàn, zěnmeyàng? | Next weekend I'm going on a trip. When I'm back, how about we have a meal together with Lin Yue and Mark? |
| 陈乐 | 好吧，那就一起吃饭。糟糕，我的手机快没电了，听不清楚了！ | Hǎo ba, nà jiù yìqǐ chīfàn. Zāogāo, wǒ de shǒujī kuài méi diàn le, tīng bu qīngchu le! | All right, a meal together it is. Oh no, my phone is about to die, I can't hear you properly! |
| 安娜 | 没关系，你的号码我记下来了，旅行回来再给你打电话。再见！ | Méi guānxi, nǐ de hàomǎ wǒ jì xialai le, lǚxíng huílai zài gěi nǐ dǎ diànhuà. Zàijiàn! | Never mind, I've saved your number. I'll call you when I'm back from my trip. Bye! |

## Exercises

### E1 · Choose the right word {type=choice}
1. 这个字太难了，我写___好。 [不 | 没 | 别] → 不
2. 我们班___三个男学生。 [就 | 再 | 更] → 就
3. 他忙___没有时间吃饭。 [得 | 的 | 了] → 得
4. 你的电话号码是多少？我想不___了。 [起来 | 回来 | 下去] → 起来
5. 老师走___教室来了。 [进 | 去 | 完] → 进

### E2 · Put the words in order {type=order}
1. 今天的 / 我做不完 / 太多了 / 功课 → 今天的功课太多了，我做不完。
2. 学生 / 我们班 / 五个 / 就 → 我们班就五个学生。
3. 累得 / 吃饭 / 他 / 不想 → 他累得不想吃饭。
4. 把 / 拿下来 / 请你 / 那本书 → 请你把那本书拿下来。
5. 来了 / 走进 / 教室 / 老师 → 老师走进教室来了。

### E3 · Translate into Chinese {type=translate}
1. I can't recall his phone number. → 我想不起来他的电话号码了。
2. Only the two of us are going to see the film. → 就我们俩去看电影。
3. She was so busy that she had no time to sleep. → 她忙得没有时间睡觉。
4. Can you understand Chinese films? → 你看得懂中国电影吗？
5. He walked back to the dorm. → 他走回宿舍去了。

### E4 · Match the word {type=match}
1. 印象 → impression
2. 力气 → strength, effort
3. 后天 → the day after tomorrow
4. 号码 → number
5. 打扫 → to clean up
