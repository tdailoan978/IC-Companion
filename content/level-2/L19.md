# Lesson 19 · 旅行 · Travel

## Objectives
- After this lesson you can talk about what you plan to do in a vacation: an internship, a temporary job or a trip.

- After this lesson you can book a flight: one way or round trip, direct or with a transfer, window or aisle seat, special meal.
- After this lesson you can read and say prices and other numbers above one thousand.
- After this lesson you can compare two options and say how big the difference is.

## Grammar

### G1 · 不得了 (extremely)
**Pattern:** `Adj / feeling verb + 得 + 不得了`

不得了 after 得 is a degree complement: it pushes an adjective, or a verb of feeling such as 想 or 喜欢, to the top of the scale, like "incredibly" or "unbelievably" in English. It is colloquial and a little dramatic, so it suits complaints, excitement and exaggeration better than neutral description. The order is fixed: the adjective first, then 得, then 不得了. Because 不得了 already gives the degree, the adjective takes no other degree word in front of it.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 香港的夏天热得不得了。 | Xiānggǎng de xiàtiān rè de bùdéliǎo. | Summer in Hong Kong is unbelievably hot. |
| 放假以前，机票贵得不得了。 | Fàngjià yǐqián, jīpiào guì de bùdéliǎo. | Just before the holidays, plane tickets are outrageously expensive. |
| 我走了一天的路，累得不得了。 | Wǒ zǒu le yì tiān de lù, lèi de bùdéliǎo. | I walked all day and I am completely worn out. |
| 听说可以去北京，安娜高兴得不得了。 | Tīngshuō kěyǐ qù Běijīng, Ānnà gāoxìng de bùdéliǎo. | When she heard she could go to Beijing, Anna was over the moon. |

**Watch out:** do not stack 很 or 非常 on the adjective: ✗ 很热得不得了 → 热得不得了.

### G2 · Question pronouns as indefinite references (whoever, whatever…)
**Pattern:** `clause with question word，(subject) + 就 + clause with the same question word`

When the same question word (谁, 什么, 哪儿, 哪 + measure word, 怎么) appears in two linked clauses, neither one is asking anything. The first sets an open condition and the second points back to it: "whoever …, that person …", "wherever …, there …". 就 normally sits in the second clause, after its subject if there is one. English uses a different word in each half ("whatever you say, I'll do it"), but Chinese repeats the very same question word, and that is where learners slip.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你想去哪儿，我们就去哪儿。 | Nǐ xiǎng qù nǎr, wǒmen jiù qù nǎr. | We will go wherever you want to go. |
| 谁有时间，谁就去订机票。 | Shéi yǒu shíjiān, shéi jiù qù dìng jīpiào. | Whoever has time goes and books the tickets. |
| 哪家旅馆便宜，我就住哪家。 | Nǎ jiā lǚguǎn piányi, wǒ jiù zhù nǎ jiā. | I will stay at whichever hotel is cheap. |
| 导游说什么，我们就做什么。 | Dǎoyóu shuō shénme, wǒmen jiù zuò shénme. | We do whatever the tour guide says. |

**Watch out:** keep the question word in the second clause instead of swapping in 那个 or 那儿: ✗ 你想去哪儿，我们就去那儿。

### G3 · Numbers over one thousand
**Pattern:** `千 qiān = 1,000 · 万 wàn = 10,000 · 十万 = 100,000 · 百万 = 1,000,000`

English counts big numbers in groups of three digits (thousand, million); Chinese counts in groups of four, and the key unit is 万, ten thousand. So 10,000 is 一万, 25,000 is 两万五千 ("two ten-thousands and five thousand"), and a million is 一百万. To read a long number, cut it into blocks of four from the right, read the left block followed by 万, then the rest. One or more zeros in the middle are read as a single 零. Before 千 and 万 most speakers say 两 rather than 二.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这张机票一千五百块。 | Zhè zhāng jīpiào yìqiān wǔbǎi kuài. | This plane ticket is 1,500 yuan. |
| 去台北的往返票要三千零八十块。 | Qù Táiběi de wǎngfǎn piào yào sānqiān líng bāshí kuài. | A round-trip ticket to Taipei costs 3,080 yuan. |
| 我们学校有两万五千个学生。 | Wǒmen xuéxiào yǒu liǎngwàn wǔqiān ge xuésheng. | Our school has 25,000 students. |
| 这个城市有一百二十万人。 | Zhège chéngshì yǒu yìbǎi èrshí wàn rén. | This city has 1,200,000 people. |

**Watch out:** there is no "ten thousand" built from 千: ✗ 十千 → 一万, and 100,000 is 十万, not ✗ 一百千.

### G4 · Comparative sentences with 比 (II)
**Pattern:** `A + 比 + B + Adj + 一点儿 / 得多 / 多了 / number + measure word`

In Lesson 11 you learned the bare comparison A 比 B + adjective. To say how large the gap is, put the amount after the adjective, never before it: 一点儿 for a small difference, 得多 or 多了 for a big one, or an exact figure such as 两百块 or 三岁. The classic mistake is to translate "much cheaper" with 很 in front of the adjective.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这家旅馆比那家便宜一点儿。 | Zhè jiā lǚguǎn bǐ nà jiā piányi yìdiǎnr. | This hotel is a little cheaper than that one. |
| 直飞的航班比转机的贵得多。 | Zhífēi de hángbān bǐ zhuǎnjī de guì de duō. | The direct flight is much more expensive than the one with a transfer. |
| 往返票比单程票贵八百块。 | Wǎngfǎn piào bǐ dānchéng piào guì bābǎi kuài. | The round-trip ticket costs 800 yuan more than the one-way ticket. |
| 坐飞机比开车快多了。 | Zuò fēijī bǐ kāichē kuài duō le. | Flying is far faster than driving. |

**Watch out:** 很, 非常 and 太 cannot be used in a 比 sentence: ✗ 这家旅馆比那家很便宜 → 这家旅馆比那家便宜得多.

## Dialogues

### Dialogue 1 · Plans for the summer
*Setting: At the end of term, Lin Yue asks Mark what he will do over the summer and ends up inviting him to Beijing.*

| Speaker | Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 林月 | 马上就要放假了，你暑假有什么计划？ | Mǎshàng jiù yào fàngjià le, nǐ shǔjià yǒu shénme jìhuà? | The vacation starts any day now. What are your plans for the summer? |
| 马克 | 我想去一家公司实习，可是还没找到。你呢？ | Wǒ xiǎng qù yì jiā gōngsī shíxí, kěshì hái méi zhǎodào. Nǐ ne? | I would like to intern at a company, but I have not found one yet. And you? |
| 林月 | 我不打工，也不实习。我打算回北京看父母。 | Wǒ bù dǎgōng, yě bù shíxí. Wǒ dǎsuàn huí Běijīng kàn fùmǔ. | No job and no internship for me. I plan to go back to Beijing to see my parents. |
| 马克 | 北京！我去过香港和台北，可是还没去过北京。听说那儿的名胜古迹多得不得了。 | Běijīng! Wǒ qùguo Xiānggǎng hé Táiběi, kěshì hái méi qùguo Běijīng. Tīngshuō nàr de míngshèng gǔjì duō de bùdéliǎo. | Beijing! I have been to Hong Kong and Taipei, but never to Beijing. I hear it has an incredible number of famous sights. |
| 林月 | 那你跟我一起去吧，我给你当导游。你想看什么，我就带你看什么。 | Nà nǐ gēn wǒ yìqǐ qù ba, wǒ gěi nǐ dāng dǎoyóu. Nǐ xiǎng kàn shénme, wǒ jiù dài nǐ kàn shénme. | Then come with me and I will be your tour guide. I will take you to see whatever you want to see. |
| 马克 | 真的吗？我最想去长城！可是我只有护照，还没有签证。 | Zhēn de ma? Wǒ zuì xiǎng qù Chángchéng! Kěshì wǒ zhǐ yǒu hùzhào, hái méiyǒu qiānzhèng. | Really? The Great Wall is what I want to see most! But I only have a passport, no visa yet. |
| 林月 | 没关系，明天就去办。机票呢？找旅行社订，还是自己在网上订？ | Méi guānxi, míngtiān jiù qù bàn. Jīpiào ne? Zhǎo lǚxíngshè dìng, háishi zìjǐ zài wǎngshàng dìng? | No problem, go and apply tomorrow. What about the tickets? Shall we book through a travel agency or online ourselves? |
| 马克 | 哪儿便宜，就在哪儿订。我的钱比你的少得多。 | Nǎr piányi, jiù zài nǎr dìng. Wǒ de qián bǐ nǐ de shǎo de duō. | We book wherever it is cheap. I have a lot less money than you do. |
| 林月 | 别担心，在北京你可以住我家，不用花钱。 | Bié dānxīn, zài Běijīng nǐ kěyǐ zhù wǒ jiā, búyòng huā qián. | Do not worry. In Beijing you can stay at my place, so you will not have to spend anything. |
| 马克 | 那太麻烦你父母了。我给他们带什么礼物好？ | Nà tài máfan nǐ fùmǔ le. Wǒ gěi tāmen dài shénme lǐwù hǎo? | That is a lot of trouble for your parents. What gift should I bring them? |
| 林月 | 什么都不用带。你多说中文，他们就高兴得不得了。 | Shénme dōu búyòng dài. Nǐ duō shuō Zhōngwén, tāmen jiù gāoxìng de bùdéliǎo. | You do not need to bring anything. Just speak lots of Chinese and they will be thrilled. |

## Exercises

### E1 · Choose the right word {type=choice}
1. 放假以前的机票贵得___。 [不得了 | 得多 | 一点儿] → 不得了
2. 你想住哪儿，我们___住哪儿。 [就 | 才 | 还] → 就
3. 这张往返票三___五百块。 [千 | 份 | 初] → 千
4. 直飞的航班比转机的贵___。 [得多 | 非常 | 很] → 得多
5. 我想订一___素餐。 [份 | 张 | 本] → 份

### E2 · Put the words in order {type=order}
1. 就 / 谁 / 当导游 / 谁 / 去过北京 → 谁去过北京，谁就当导游。
2. 不得了 / 香港 / 得 / 的东西 / 贵 → 香港的东西贵得不得了。
3. 便宜 / 往返票 / 比 / 两百块 / 这张 / 那张 → 这张往返票比那张便宜两百块。
4. 来 / 我父母 / 了 / 马上 / 就要 → 我父母马上就要来了。
5. 靠 / 位子 / 我 / 窗户的 / 想要 → 我想要靠窗户的位子。

### E3 · Translate into Chinese {type=translate}
1. Summer vacation is about to start. → 马上就要放暑假了。
2. We will order whatever you want to eat. → 你想吃什么，我们就点什么。
3. That company has thirty thousand people. → 那家公司有三万人。
4. Hotels in Hong Kong are three hundred yuan more expensive than hotels in Taipei. → 香港的旅馆比台北的贵三百块。
5. My parents are extremely happy. → 我父母高兴得不得了。

### E4 · Match the word {type=match}
1. 护照 → passport
2. 签证 → visa
3. 导游 → tour guide
4. 首都 → capital city
5. 往返 → to make a round trip
6. 转机 → to change planes
7. 打折 → to give a discount
8. 旅馆 → hotel
