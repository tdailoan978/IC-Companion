# Lesson 18 · 运动 · Sports

## Objectives
- After this lesson you can talk about the sports you play or watch and how good you are at them.
- After this lesson you can say how long you have not done something, and how long you did do something when the verb has an object.
- After this lesson you can call things easy, hard, pleasant or unpleasant with 好/难 + verb, and urge someone to keep going with 下去.
- After this lesson you can describe what someone is wearing, holding or how they are sitting with 着.
- After this lesson you can say what happened to someone or something with a 被, 叫 or 让 passive sentence.

## Grammar

### G1 · Duration of inactivity
**Pattern:** `Subject + time span + 没 + Verb (+ Object) + 了`

To say how long something has not happened, put the length of time before 没 and the verb. This is the reverse of a positive sentence, where the duration comes after the verb (我跑了半个小时). The 了 at the end shows that the gap is still going on as you speak: "it has been two years since I last…". The mistake to avoid is copying the positive word order and putting the time span after the verb.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 她半年没游泳了。 | Tā bàn nián méi yóuyǒng le. | She hasn't swum for half a year. |
| 我三天没睡好觉了，真难受。 | Wǒ sān tiān méi shuì hǎo jiào le, zhēn nánshòu. | I haven't slept well for three days; I feel awful. |
| 马克一个多月没打篮球了。 | Mǎkè yí ge duō yuè méi dǎ lánqiú le. | Mark hasn't played basketball for over a month. |
| 我们好几年没见面了。 | Wǒmen hǎo jǐ nián méi jiànmiàn le. | We haven't seen each other for quite a few years. |

**Watch out:** ✗ 我没运动两年了。 The time span goes first: 我两年没运动了。

### G2 · 好/难 + verb
**Pattern:** `好/难 + Verb`

好 or 难 in front of a verb makes a new adjective, with one of two meanings. With verbs of the senses, such as 吃, 喝, 看, 听 and 受, it says whether the experience is pleasant: 好吃 (tasty), 好听 (nice to listen to), 难看 (ugly), 难受 (hard to bear). With most other verbs it says whether the action is easy or hard to carry out: 好找 (easy to find), 难写 (hard to write), 好学 (easy to learn). Because the result is an adjective, it takes 很, 不, 太 and so on, and the thing being judged comes first as the topic rather than after the verb.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这个公园很好找，就在学校旁边。 | Zhège gōngyuán hěn hǎo zhǎo, jiù zài xuéxiào pángbiān. | This park is easy to find; it's right next to the school. |
| 游泳不难学，可是我怕水。 | Yóuyǒng bù nán xué, kěshì wǒ pà shuǐ. | Swimming isn't hard to learn, but I'm afraid of water. |
| 她唱的歌真好听。 | Tā chàng de gē zhēn hǎotīng. | The songs she sings are really lovely. |
| 跑了半天，我的脚难受死了。 | Pǎole bàntiān, wǒ de jiǎo nánshòu sǐ le. | After running for ages, my feet feel terrible. |

**Watch out:** ✗ 很难写这个字。 Put the thing first: 这个字很难写。

### G3 · 下去 for continuing an action
**Pattern:** `Verb + 下去`

下去 literally means "go down", but after a verb it can also mean "carry on": an action that has already started keeps going into the future. You will hear it most with verbs like 说, 学, 跑, 住 and 做. Nothing may follow 下去, so an object has to be moved to the front of the sentence as a topic. Insert 不 to say you cannot go on: 跑不下去了 is "I can't run any further". The usual slip is putting the object after 下去.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 请你说下去，我们都想听。 | Qǐng nǐ shuō xiàqu, wǒmen dōu xiǎng tīng. | Please go on; we all want to hear it. |
| 太累了，我跑不下去了。 | Tài lèi le, wǒ pǎo bu xiàqù le. | I'm too tired; I can't keep running. |
| 中文很有意思，我一定要学下去。 | Zhōngwén hěn yǒu yìsi, wǒ yídìng yào xué xiàqu. | Chinese is really interesting; I'm definitely going to keep learning it. |
| 这套公寓又便宜又安静，我想住下去。 | Zhè tào gōngyù yòu piányi yòu ānjìng, wǒ xiǎng zhù xiàqu. | This apartment is both cheap and quiet; I'd like to stay on. |

**Watch out:** ✗ 我要学下去中文。 Move the object to the front: 中文我要学下去。

### G4 · Duration of activity (II)
**Pattern:** `Verb + (了) + time span + (的) + Object`

In Lesson 14 you put a length of time straight after the verb. When the verb also has an object there are two ways to fit everything in. You can say the verb twice (打球打了两个小时), or you can slide the time span in between the verb and its object, with an optional 的: 打了两个小时(的)球. The second way also works for verb-object words such as 游泳, 跑步 and 睡觉, which split in two: 游了四十分钟泳. What you cannot do is leave the verb and object together and tack the duration on behind them.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 他们打了两个小时的篮球。 | Tāmen dǎle liǎng ge xiǎoshí de lánqiú. | They played basketball for two hours. |
| 我每天早上跑半个小时步。 | Wǒ měi tiān zǎoshang pǎo bàn ge xiǎoshí bù. | I jog for half an hour every morning. |
| 安娜游了四十分钟泳，一点儿也不累。 | Ānnà yóule sìshí fēnzhōng yǒng, yìdiǎnr yě bú lèi. | Anna swam for forty minutes and isn't tired at all. |
| 昨天晚上我看了一个半小时的足球比赛。 | Zuótiān wǎnshang wǒ kànle yí ge bàn xiǎoshí de zúqiú bǐsài. | Last night I watched a soccer match for an hour and a half. |

**Watch out:** ✗ 我跑步了半个小时。 Split the verb and its object: 我跑了半个小时步。

### G5 · The particle 着
**Pattern:** `Verb + 着 (+ Object)` or `Verb 1 + 着 + Verb 2`

着 (zhe, neutral tone) after a verb shows a state that lasts: a posture (坐着, 躺着), something being worn or held (穿着, 拿着, 抱着), or the way something has been left. It is not the same as 在 or 正在, which describe an activity in progress: 她在穿运动服 means she is putting the sportswear on, while 她穿着运动服 means she has it on. A second very common use is Verb 1 + 着 + Verb 2, where the first verb gives the manner or posture in which the second action is done: 躺着看书 is "read lying down". The negative is 没 + Verb + 着.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 马克穿着一件新运动服。 | Mǎkè chuānzhe yí jiàn xīn yùndòngfú. | Mark is wearing a new tracksuit. |
| 她手里拿着两个网球拍。 | Tā shǒu li názhe liǎng ge wǎngqiúpāi. | She is holding two tennis rackets in her hand. |
| 别躺着看书，对眼睛不好。 | Bié tǎngzhe kàn shū, duì yǎnjing bù hǎo. | Don't read lying down; it's bad for your eyes. |
| 林月笑着说：“你跑得太慢了！” | Lín Yuè xiàozhe shuō: "Nǐ pǎo de tài màn le!" | Lin Yue said with a smile, "You run too slowly!" |

**Watch out:** 着 marks a lasting state, not a finished action, so do not add 了 after it: ✗ 她穿着了运动服。

### G6 · Passive-voice sentences with 被/叫/让
**Pattern:** `Receiver + 被/叫/让 + Doer + Verb + result or 了`

A passive sentence starts with the person or thing that the action lands on, then 被, 叫 or 让, then whoever did it, then the verb. The verb can never stand bare: it needs a result, a direction or at least 了 after it. Chinese passives are used far less than English ones and usually report something unwelcome or unexpected, such as a thing being taken, eaten, broken or lost. 被 is the neutral choice and lets you leave the doer out (球被拿走了); 叫 and 让 are more colloquial and always need a doer. Two mistakes are common: a bare verb at the end, and dropping the doer after 叫 or 让.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我的网球拍被弟弟拿走了。 | Wǒ de wǎngqiúpāi bèi dìdi názǒu le. | My tennis racket was taken away by my little brother. |
| 蛋糕叫马克吃完了。 | Dàngāo jiào Mǎkè chīwán le. | The cake got eaten up by Mark. |
| 我的运动服让妹妹穿坏了。 | Wǒ de yùndòngfú ràng mèimei chuānhuài le. | My sportswear was worn out by my little sister. |
| 那个球被踢到公园外边去了。 | Nàge qiú bèi tī dào gōngyuán wàibian qù le. | That ball was kicked out of the park. |

**Watch out:** ✗ 蛋糕被马克吃。 Add what happened to it: 蛋糕被马克吃完了。

## Dialogues

### Dialogue 1 · Ten minutes in
*Setting: 林月 has dragged 马克 out for a Saturday-morning run in the park, and he is already struggling.*

| Speaker | Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 马克 | 等一下！我跑不下去了，肚子难受死了。 | Děng yíxià! Wǒ pǎo bu xiàqù le, dùzi nánshòu sǐ le. | Hang on! I can't keep going; my stomach is killing me. |
| 林月 | 我们才跑了十分钟！你多久没运动了？ | Wǒmen cái pǎole shí fēnzhōng! Nǐ duō jiǔ méi yùndòng le? | We've only been running for ten minutes! How long has it been since you exercised? |
| 马克 | 半年多没运动了。你看，我越来越胖了。 | Bàn nián duō méi yùndòng le. Nǐ kàn, wǒ yuè lái yuè pàng le. | More than half a year. Look, I'm getting fatter and fatter. |
| 林月 | 那当然！你每天坐着上网，连路都不愿意走。 | Nà dāngrán! Nǐ měi tiān zuòzhe shàngwǎng, lián lù dōu bú yuànyì zǒu. | Of course you are! You sit online every day and aren't even willing to walk. |
| 马克 | 我不是懒，是跑步太没意思了。打网球、打篮球都比跑步好玩。 | Wǒ bú shì lǎn, shì pǎobù tài méi yìsi le. Dǎ wǎngqiú, dǎ lánqiú dōu bǐ pǎobù hǎowán. | I'm not lazy; jogging is just too boring. Tennis and basketball are both more fun than jogging. |
| 林月 | 可是跑步最简单，不用买网球拍，一双鞋就够了。 | Kěshì pǎobù zuì jiǎndān, búyòng mǎi wǎngqiúpāi, yì shuāng xié jiù gòu le. | But jogging is the simplest. You don't need to buy a racket; a pair of shoes is enough. |
| 马克 | 那我们明天去游泳吧。游泳很舒服，我愿意天天游。 | Nà wǒmen míngtiān qù yóuyǒng ba. Yóuyǒng hěn shūfu, wǒ yuànyì tiāntiān yóu. | Then let's go swimming tomorrow. Swimming feels great; I'd happily do it every day. |
| 林月 | 游泳？不行，太危险了。我从小就怕水，觉得一下水就会淹死。 | Yóuyǒng? Bùxíng, tài wēixiǎn le. Wǒ cóng xiǎo jiù pà shuǐ, juéde yí xià shuǐ jiù huì yānsǐ. | Swimming? No way, too dangerous. I've been afraid of water since I was little; I feel I'd drown the moment I got in. |
| 马克 | 你跑得那么快，可是连游泳都不会？ | Nǐ pǎo de nàme kuài, kěshì lián yóuyǒng dōu bú huì? | You run that fast, yet you can't even swim? |
| 林月 | 别笑！我学过好几次，都没学会。 | Bié xiào! Wǒ xuéguo hǎo jǐ cì, dōu méi xuéhuì. | Don't laugh! I've tried to learn quite a few times and never managed. |
| 马克 | 那我教你游泳，你教我跑步，怎么样？ | Nà wǒ jiāo nǐ yóuyǒng, nǐ jiāo wǒ pǎobù, zěnmeyàng? | Then I'll teach you to swim and you teach me to run. How about it? |
| 林月 | 一言为定！可是今天我们还要再跑十分钟。 | Yì yán wéi dìng! Kěshì jīntiān wǒmen hái yào zài pǎo shí fēnzhōng. | It's a deal! But today we still have another ten minutes to run. |

### Dialogue 2 · Whose football?
*Setting: 陈乐 drops by and finds 安娜, still in her kit after a soccer match, watching American football on TV.*

| Speaker | Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 陈乐 | 安娜，你怎么穿着运动服看电视？ | Ānnà, nǐ zěnme chuānzhe yùndòngfú kàn diànshì? | Anna, how come you're watching TV in your sports kit? |
| 安娜 | 我刚踢完足球。为了提高水平，我上大学以后每个星期都踢三次。 | Wǒ gāng tīwán zúqiú. Wèile tígāo shuǐpíng, wǒ shàng dàxué yǐhòu měi ge xīngqī dōu tī sān cì. | I've just finished playing soccer. To get better, I've played three times a week ever since I started university. |
| 陈乐 | 真棒！电视上是什么比赛？他们怎么抱着球跑？ | Zhēn bàng! Diànshì shang shì shénme bǐsài? Tāmen zěnme bàozhe qiú pǎo? | Impressive! What match is that on TV? Why are they running with the ball in their arms? |
| 安娜 | 这是美式足球，不是国际足球，可以用手抱着球跑。 | Zhè shì měishì zúqiú, bú shì guójì zúqiú, kěyǐ yòng shǒu bàozhe qiú pǎo. | This is American football, not international football; you're allowed to run holding the ball in your hands. |
| 陈乐 | 用手？那应该叫“手球”吧！用脚踢的才是足球。 | Yòng shǒu? Nà yīnggāi jiào "shǒuqiú" ba! Yòng jiǎo tī de cái shì zúqiú. | With their hands? Then it ought to be called "handball"! Only the kind you kick with your feet is football. |
| 安娜 | 你说得对，可是美国人都这么叫。 | Nǐ shuō de duì, kěshì Měiguó rén dōu zhème jiào. | You have a point, but that's what all Americans call it. |
| 陈乐 | 你看，那个人被三个人抱住了！不危险吗？ | Nǐ kàn, nàge rén bèi sān ge rén bàozhù le! Bù wēixiǎn ma? | Look, that player just got grabbed by three people! Isn't that dangerous? |
| 安娜 | 别担心，他们身体都很棒。我看了半天了，谁都没事。 | Bié dānxīn, tāmen shēntǐ dōu hěn bàng. Wǒ kànle bàntiān le, shéi dōu méi shì. | Don't worry, they're all in great shape. I've been watching for ages and nobody has been hurt. |
| 陈乐 | 那你们今天的比赛怎么样？ | Nà nǐmen jīntiān de bǐsài zěnmeyàng? | So how did your own match go today? |
| 安娜 | 别问了！我们踢了两个小时的球，一个球都没踢进去，我的脚还让人踢疼了。 | Bié wèn le! Wǒmen tīle liǎng ge xiǎoshí de qiú, yí ge qiú dōu méi tī jìnqu, wǒ de jiǎo hái ràng rén tīténg le. | Don't ask! We played for two hours and didn't get a single ball in, and somebody kicked my foot so hard it hurts. |
| 陈乐 | 没关系。你踢下去，水平一定会越来越高。 | Méi guānxi. Nǐ tī xiàqu, shuǐpíng yídìng huì yuè lái yuè gāo. | Never mind. Keep playing and you're bound to get better and better. |
| 安娜 | 谢谢！下次比赛你来看吧，可是别抱着球跑！ | Xièxie! Xià cì bǐsài nǐ lái kàn ba, kěshì bié bàozhe qiú pǎo! | Thanks! Come and watch the next match, but don't run off with the ball in your arms! |

## Exercises

### E1 · Choose the right word {type=choice}
1. 我三个月___打网球了。 [没 | 很 | 别] → 没
2. 这个字很___写，我写了五遍还写错了。 [难 | 好 | 快] → 难
3. 中文很有意思，我想一直学___。 [下去 | 上去 | 回去] → 下去
4. 安娜穿___一件红色的运动服。 [着 | 得 | 的] → 着
5. 我的蛋糕___弟弟吃完了。 [被 | 把 | 对] → 被

### E2 · Put the words in order {type=order}
1. 没 / 我 / 了 / 半年 / 游泳 → 我半年没游泳了。
2. 的 / 打了 / 他们 / 篮球 / 两个小时 → 他们打了两个小时的篮球。
3. 抱着 / 她 / 小狗 / 一条 → 她抱着一条小狗。
4. 被 / 网球拍 / 拿走了 / 我的 / 马克 → 我的网球拍被马克拿走了。
5. 跑 / 我 / 下去 / 不 / 了 → 我跑不下去了。

### E3 · Translate into Chinese {type=translate}
1. I haven't jogged for two weeks. → 我两个星期没跑步了。
2. This tennis racket is easy to use. → 这个网球拍很好用。
3. In order to improve his Chinese, he speaks Chinese every day. → 为了提高中文水平，他每天都说中文。
4. She is wearing sportswear. → 她穿着运动服。
5. My ball was kicked away by my little brother. → 我的球被弟弟踢走了。

### E4 · Match the word {type=match}
1. 胖 → fat
2. 简单 → simple
3. 危险 → dangerous
4. 愿意 → to be willing
5. 比赛 → game, match
6. 提高 → to improve
7. 担心 → to worry
8. 运动服 → sportswear
