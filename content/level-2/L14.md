# Lesson 14 · 生日晚会 · Birthday Party

## Objectives
- After this lesson you can say what someone is in the middle of doing by ending the sentence with 呢.
- After this lesson you can describe a noun with a whole phrase or clause placed before it and linked by 的.
- After this lesson you can say how long an action lasts or lasted.
- After this lesson you can ask and tell when, where or how a past event happened with 是…的.
- After this lesson you can talk about gifts, party food and what a person or a pet looks like, using 还 and 又…又….

## Grammar

### G1 · 呢 for an action in progress
**Pattern:** `Subject + (正在 / 在) + Verb + (Object) + 呢`

You already know 呢 as the "and you?" question particle. At the end of a statement it does a different job: it signals that the action is going on right at this moment, like the English "-ing". It often teams up with 正在 or 在 before the verb, but in relaxed speech 呢 alone is enough. Because the action is still under way, the sentence takes no 了. It is typical for answering "what are you doing?" or for explaining why someone is not free just now.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我看书呢。 | Wǒ kàn shū ne. | I'm reading. |
| 林月正在打电话呢。 | Lín Yuè zhèngzài dǎ diànhuà ne. | Lin Yue is on the phone right now. |
| 你找什么呢？ | Nǐ zhǎo shénme ne? | What are you looking for? |
| 他们在楼下等你呢。 | Tāmen zài lóuxià děng nǐ ne. | They are waiting for you downstairs. |

**Watch out:** an action in progress is not finished, so leave out 了: ✗ 我看了书呢.

### G2 · Verb phrases and subject-predicate phrases as attributives (…的 + noun)
**Pattern:** `Verb phrase / Subject + Verb + 的 + Noun`

In Chinese everything that describes a noun stands in front of it, however long the description is, and 的 joins the two. Where English says "the book that I bought", Chinese says 我买的书, literally "I-bought 的 book". There is no word for "that", "who" or "which"; 的 does all the work. To build one, find the noun, say the whole describing phrase first, add 的, then the noun. Words like 这个 or 那个 usually come after the 的 phrase and directly before the noun.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这是我给安娜买的礼物。 | Zhè shì wǒ gěi Ānnà mǎi de lǐwù. | This is the present I bought for Anna. |
| 昨天来的那个人是我表姐。 | Zuótiān lái de nàge rén shì wǒ biǎojiě. | The person who came yesterday is my cousin. |
| 我最爱吃妈妈做的饺子。 | Wǒ zuì ài chī māma zuò de jiǎozi. | I love the dumplings my mom makes best of all. |
| 住在五楼的学生都认识她。 | Zhù zài wǔ lóu de xuésheng dōu rènshi tā. | The students who live on the fifth floor all know her. |

**Watch out:** the description cannot follow the noun as in English: ✗ 礼物我买的很贵 should be 我买的礼物很贵.

### G3 · Time duration (I)
**Pattern:** `Subject + Verb + (了) + duration + (的) + Object`

A point in time (今天, 八点) goes before the verb, but a length of time (两个钟头, 一年, 二十分钟) goes after it. If the verb has no object, simply add the duration: 住了一年. If there is an object, you have two choices. Either slip the duration between verb and object, with an optional 的: 跳了三个钟头的舞. Or say the verb twice, once with the object and once with the duration: 跳舞跳了三个钟头. When the object is a pronoun, the repeated-verb form is the safe one.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我们跳了三个钟头的舞。 | Wǒmen tiào le sān ge zhōngtóu de wǔ. | We danced for three hours. |
| 他在北京住了一年。 | Tā zài Běijīng zhù le yì nián. | He lived in Beijing for a year. |
| 我每天学一个半钟头中文。 | Wǒ měi tiān xué yí ge bàn zhōngtóu Zhōngwén. | I study Chinese for an hour and a half every day. |
| 她等你等了二十分钟。 | Tā děng nǐ děng le èrshí fēnzhōng. | She waited for you for twenty minutes. |

**Watch out:** a duration never goes before the verb: ✗ 我两个钟头跳舞了 should be 我跳了两个钟头的舞.

### G4 · 是…的 sentences (I)
**Pattern:** `Subject + (是) + time / place / manner + Verb + 的`

When both people already know that something happened and the real question is when, where, how or with whom, Chinese wraps that detail in 是…的 instead of using 了. 是 stands before the detail being highlighted and 的 closes the sentence or follows the verb. In positive sentences 是 is often dropped, but 的 has to stay. The negative is 不是…的, and there 是 cannot be left out. Think of it as the pattern for filling in the details of a known past event.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你是什么时候到的？ | Nǐ shì shénme shíhou dào de? | When did you arrive? |
| 我是坐地铁来的。 | Wǒ shì zuò dìtiě lái de. | I came by subway. |
| 这个蛋糕是在哪儿买的？ | Zhège dàngāo shì zài nǎr mǎi de? | Where was this cake bought? |
| 我们不是在中学认识的，是在暑期班认识的。 | Wǒmen bú shì zài zhōngxué rènshi de, shì zài shǔqībān rènshi de. | We didn't meet in secondary school, we met in a summer class. |

**Watch out:** do not use 了 to ask for these details: ✗ 你怎么来了的 should be 你是怎么来的.

### G5 · 还 (still)
**Pattern:** `Subject + 还 + Verb / Adj`

You first met 还 meaning "also, in addition". Here it means "still": a situation that began earlier is going on without change, often longer than expected. It is an adverb, so it comes after the subject and before the verb, or before 在, 没 or 不. The combination 还没(有) means "not yet" and frequently ends with 呢. Do not mix it up with 还是 ("or" in questions), and do not move it to the front of the sentence the way English can do with "still".

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 十二点了，他还在做功课。 | Shí'èr diǎn le, tā hái zài zuò gōngkè. | It's twelve o'clock and he is still doing homework. |
| 我表姐还住在纽约。 | Wǒ biǎojiě hái zhù zài Niǔyuē. | My cousin still lives in New York. |
| 舞会还没开始呢。 | Wǔhuì hái méi kāishǐ ne. | The dance hasn't started yet. |
| 他长大了，可是还像个孩子。 | Tā zhǎngdà le, kěshì hái xiàng ge háizi. | He has grown up, but he is still like a child. |

**Watch out:** 还 stays after the subject: ✗ 还他在睡觉 should be 他还在睡觉.

### G6 · 又…又… (both… and…)
**Pattern:** `Subject + 又 + Adj / Verb + 又 + Adj / Verb`

Use 又…又… to give two qualities, or two activities, that hold at the same time. Each 又 goes directly before its adjective or verb, and the adjectives stay bare, without 很. The two halves should point the same way: both pleasant or both unpleasant. If one is good and the other bad, the contrast needs 可是 or 但是 instead. Short adjectives sound best, which is why the pattern is so common for describing food, people and things.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这个西瓜又大又甜。 | Zhège xīgua yòu dà yòu tián. | This watermelon is both big and sweet. |
| 她的狗又聪明又可爱。 | Tā de gǒu yòu cōngming yòu kě'ài. | Her dog is both smart and cute. |
| 那家饭馆的菜又贵又不好吃。 | Nà jiā fànguǎn de cài yòu guì yòu bù hǎochī. | The food at that restaurant is expensive and doesn't taste good either. |
| 晚会上大家又唱歌又跳舞。 | Wǎnhuì shang dàjiā yòu chànggē yòu tiàowǔ. | At the party everyone sang and danced. |

**Watch out:** no 很 after 又: ✗ 又很大又很甜 should be 又大又甜.

## Dialogues

### Dialogue 1 · A gift and a watermelon
*Setting: On Saturday afternoon 林月 phones 马克 about 安娜's birthday dance party that evening.*

| Speaker | Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 林月 | 喂，马克，你做什么呢？ | Wéi, Mǎkè, nǐ zuò shénme ne? | Hello, Mark, what are you up to? |
| 马克 | 我在书店给安娜买生日礼物呢。你说，她会喜欢什么书？ | Wǒ zài shūdiàn gěi Ānnà mǎi shēngrì lǐwù ne. Nǐ shuō, tā huì xǐhuan shénme shū? | I'm at the bookstore buying Anna a birthday present. Tell me, what kind of book would she like? |
| 林月 | 她爱看书，你买的书她都会喜欢。我给她买了一把花。 | Tā ài kàn shū, nǐ mǎi de shū tā dōu huì xǐhuan. Wǒ gěi tā mǎi le yì bǎ huā. | She loves reading, so she'll like any book you buy. I got her a bunch of flowers. |
| 马克 | 那我买这本吧。舞会几点开始？安娜现在在家吗？ | Nà wǒ mǎi zhè běn ba. Wǔhuì jǐ diǎn kāishǐ? Ānnà xiànzài zài jiā ma? | Then I'll take this one. What time does the dance start? Is Anna at home now? |
| 林月 | 七点开始。她不在家，她到机场去接她表姐了。 | Qī diǎn kāishǐ. Tā bú zài jiā, tā dào jīchǎng qù jiē tā biǎojiě le. | It starts at seven. She's not home, she went to the airport to pick up her cousin. |
| 马克 | 她表姐？是住在纽约的那个表姐吗？ | Tā biǎojiě? Shì zhù zài Niǔyuē de nàge biǎojiě ma? | Her cousin? The one who lives in New York? |
| 林月 | 对，就是那个中学老师。还有，你能带一些饮料来吗？ | Duì, jiù shì nàge zhōngxué lǎoshī. Hái yǒu, nǐ néng dài yìxiē yǐnliào lái ma? | Right, the secondary school teacher. One more thing, can you bring some drinks? |
| 马克 | 没问题。要不要带水果？我宿舍里还有苹果和梨。 | Méi wèntí. Yào bu yào dài shuǐguǒ? Wǒ sùshè li hái yǒu píngguǒ hé lí. | No problem. Should I bring fruit? I still have apples and pears in my dorm. |
| 林月 | 不用了，水果我买好了，有一个又大又甜的西瓜。 | Bú yòng le, shuǐguǒ wǒ mǎihǎo le, yǒu yí ge yòu dà yòu tián de xīgua. | No need, I've already bought the fruit. There's a big, sweet watermelon. |
| 马克 | 大西瓜？一定很重吧！你怎么拿？ | Dà xīgua? Yídìng hěn zhòng ba! Nǐ zěnme ná? | A big watermelon? It must be heavy! How are you going to carry it? |
| 林月 | 所以我想请你帮我拿。对了，安娜住在五楼。 | Suǒyǐ wǒ xiǎng qǐng nǐ bāng wǒ ná. Duì le, Ānnà zhù zài wǔ lóu. | That's why I'd like you to carry it for me. Oh, by the way, Anna lives on the fifth floor. |
| 马克 | 五楼？好吧……你等我半个小时，我一买好礼物就到你宿舍去。 | Wǔ lóu? Hǎo ba… Nǐ děng wǒ bàn ge xiǎoshí, wǒ yì mǎihǎo lǐwù jiù dào nǐ sùshè qù. | The fifth floor? All right… Give me half an hour. I'll come to your dorm as soon as I've bought the present. |

### Dialogue 2 · The cleverest guest
*Setting: At the party, 马克 arrives late and meets the dog that 安娜's cousin brought along from New York.*

| Speaker | Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 安娜 | 马克，你来了！我们已经跳了一个钟头的舞了。 | Mǎkè, nǐ lái le! Wǒmen yǐjīng tiào le yí ge zhōngtóu de wǔ le. | Mark, you're here! We've already been dancing for an hour. |
| 马克 | 对不起，我来晚了。生日快乐！这是送给你的书。……那是谁的狗？ | Duìbuqǐ, wǒ lái wǎn le. Shēngrì kuàilè! Zhè shì sòng gěi nǐ de shū. … Nà shì shéi de gǒu? | Sorry I'm late. Happy birthday! Here's a book for you. … Whose dog is that? |
| 安娜 | 谢谢！是我表姐的，叫小白。它是去年生的，今年一岁。 | Xièxie! Shì wǒ biǎojiě de, jiào Xiǎobái. Tā shì qùnián shēng de, jīnnián yí suì. | Thanks! It's my cousin's. His name is Xiaobai. He was born last year, so he's one. |
| 林月 | 你们看，它的眼睛又大又圆，鼻子黑黑的，真可爱！ | Nǐmen kàn, tā de yǎnjing yòu dà yòu yuán, bízi hēihēi de, zhēn kě'ài! | Look, his eyes are big and round and his nose is so black. He's really cute! |
| 马克 | 它的脸长得有一点儿像你表姐。 | Tā de liǎn zhǎng de yǒu yìdiǎnr xiàng nǐ biǎojiě. | His face looks a little like your cousin's. |
| 林月 | 马克！她表姐还在这儿呢！ | Mǎkè! Tā biǎojiě hái zài zhèr ne! | Mark! Her cousin is still right here! |
| 马克 | 我是说，它跟她一样漂亮。 | Wǒ shì shuō, tā gēn tā yíyàng piàoliang. | What I mean is, he's just as good-looking as she is. |
| 安娜 | 没关系，我表姐自己也这么说。小白很聪明，你一说“坐”，它就坐。 | Méi guānxi, wǒ biǎojiě zìjǐ yě zhème shuō. Xiǎobái hěn cōngming, nǐ yì shuō "zuò", tā jiù zuò. | It's fine, my cousin says so herself. Xiaobai is very smart. The moment you say "sit", he sits. |
| 马克 | 是吗？它是在哪儿学的？ | Shì ma? Tā shì zài nǎr xué de? | Really? Where did he learn that? |
| 安娜 | 我表姐带它上了两个月的暑期班。 | Wǒ biǎojiě dài tā shàng le liǎng ge yuè de shǔqībān. | My cousin took him to a summer class for two months. |
| 马克 | 狗也上暑期班？我以为只有学生上暑期班。它比我用功！ | Gǒu yě shàng shǔqībān? Wǒ yǐwéi zhǐ yǒu xuésheng shàng shǔqībān. Tā bǐ wǒ yònggōng! | Dogs go to summer class too? I thought only students did. He works harder than I do! |
| 林月 | 安娜属狗，所以她最爱狗。好了，我们吃蛋糕吧！ | Ānnà shǔ gǒu, suǒyǐ tā zuì ài gǒu. Hǎo le, wǒmen chī dàngāo ba! | Anna was born in the Year of the Dog, so she loves dogs most of all. OK, let's have cake! |

## Exercises

### E1 · Choose the right word {type=choice}
1. 别进去，弟弟睡觉___。 [呢 | 吗 | 的] → 呢
2. 这是我妈妈做___蛋糕。 [的 | 得 | 了] → 的
3. 我是坐飞机来___。 [的 | 了 | 呢] → 的
4. 八点了，她___没起床。 [还 | 最 | 更] → 还
5. 这个苹果___大又甜。 [又 | 还 | 也] → 又

### E2 · Put the words in order {type=order}
1. 呢 / 做功课 / 正在 / 马克 → 马克正在做功课呢。
2. 礼物 / 这是 / 买的 / 我给你 → 这是我给你买的礼物。
3. 两个钟头 / 我们 / 的舞 / 跳了 → 我们跳了两个钟头的舞。
4. 的 / 是 / 你 / 怎么来 → 你是怎么来的？
5. 还 / 我表姐 / 纽约 / 住在 → 我表姐还住在纽约。

### E3 · Translate into Chinese {type=translate}
1. She is on the phone right now. → 她正在打电话呢。
2. The fruit you bought is really sweet. → 你买的水果真甜。
3. Last night I danced for two hours. → 我昨天晚上跳了两个钟头的舞。
4. We met in secondary school. → 我们是在中学认识的。
5. This watermelon is both big and heavy. → 这个西瓜又大又重。

### E4 · Match the word {type=match}
1. 礼物 → gift, present
2. 饮料 → beverage
3. 水果 → fruit
4. 西瓜 → watermelon
5. 钟头 → hour
6. 聪明 → smart
7. 眼睛 → eye
8. 蛋糕 → cake
