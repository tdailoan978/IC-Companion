# Lesson 15 · 看病 · Seeing a Doctor

## Objectives
- After this lesson you can tell a doctor where it hurts, since when, and how bad it is.
- After this lesson you can understand simple instructions for taking medicine (how many times a day, how many tablets).
- After this lesson you can say what you did with a specific thing by using a 把 sentence.
- After this lesson you can talk about allergies, colds and what is good or bad for your health with 对.
- After this lesson you can urge a friend to get help, and stack up reasons with 再说.

## Grammar

### G1 · 死 for an extreme degree
**Pattern:** `Adjective + 死 + 了`

Nobody is actually dying here. Putting 死 straight after an adjective pushes it to the top of the scale, the way English says "starving to death" or "dead tired", and a 了 almost always closes the phrase. It is casual spoken Chinese and turns up mostly with unpleasant feelings (疼, 饿, 累, 热, 忙), though you will also hear 高兴死了. Because 死了 already marks the extreme, learners go wrong by adding a second degree word in front.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我肚子疼死了。 | Wǒ dùzi téngsǐ le. | My stomach is killing me. |
| 今天热死了，我不想去打球。 | Jīntiān rèsǐ le, wǒ bù xiǎng qù dǎqiú. | It is unbearably hot today; I don't want to go and play ball. |
| 这个星期有三个考试，我忙死了。 | Zhège xīngqī yǒu sān ge kǎoshì, wǒ mángsǐ le. | I have three exams this week; I'm insanely busy. |
| 快点儿上菜吧，我们饿死了。 | Kuài diǎnr shàngcài ba, wǒmen èsǐ le. | Please bring the food quickly, we are starving. |

**Watch out:** no 很 or 非常 in front: ✗ 我很饿死了 → 我饿死了.

### G2 · 起来 for the beginning of an action
**Pattern:** `Verb / Adjective + 起来` · with an object: `Verb + 起 + Object + 来`

Added after a verb or adjective, 起来 says that something starts and then keeps going: 笑起来 is "burst out laughing", 疼起来 is "start to hurt". It often describes a change nobody planned, and 了 usually follows. If the verb has an object, the object splits the pair and sits between 起 and 来. In speech the two syllables are light, so the pinyin below writes them without tone marks.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 听了他的话，大家都笑起来了。 | Tīngle tā de huà, dàjiā dōu xiào qilai le. | Hearing what he said, everybody started laughing. |
| 我刚躺下，肚子又疼起来了。 | Wǒ gāng tǎng xia, dùzi yòu téng qilai le. | I had just lain down when my stomach started hurting again. |
| 外边下起雨来了，我们回去吧。 | Wàibian xià qi yǔ lai le, wǒmen huíqu ba. | It has started raining outside; let's go back. |
| 他们一见面就聊起天来了。 | Tāmen yí jiànmiàn jiù liáo qi tiān lai le. | As soon as they met they started chatting. |

**Watch out:** the object never goes after 起来: ✗ 下起来雨了 → 下起雨来了.

### G3 · 次 for frequency
**Pattern:** `Verb + (了 / 过) + Number + 次 (+ Object)`

To say how many times something happens, put number + 次 after the verb, not before it as in English "twice went". An ordinary object follows 次 (上了三次厕所). If the object is a person or a place it may stand on either side of the count (去过一次医院 or 去过医院一次), and a pronoun always comes first (见过他两次). The related word 遍 also counts occasions, but only for doing something the whole way through from start to finish, such as reading a text or repeating a sentence.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我夜里上了好几次厕所。 | Wǒ yèli shàngle hǎo jǐ cì cèsuǒ. | I went to the toilet quite a few times during the night. |
| 这种药一天吃三次，一次两片。 | Zhè zhǒng yào yì tiān chī sān cì, yí cì liǎng piàn. | Take this medicine three times a day, two tablets each time. |
| 我去过那家医院一次。 | Wǒ qùguo nà jiā yīyuàn yí cì. | I have been to that hospital once. |
| 我以前见过他两次。 | Wǒ yǐqián jiànguo tā liǎng cì. | I have met him twice before. |

**Watch out:** the count follows the verb: ✗ 我三次去过医院 → 我去过三次医院.

### G4 · The 把 construction (I)
**Pattern:** `Subject + 把 + Object + Verb + something more (了 / complement / place)`

A 把 sentence moves the object in front of the verb and then reports what was done to it: where it ended up, or that it got finished, drunk, broken. Use it when both speakers know which thing is meant (this glass of water, the cake in the fridge), never for "a" something unspecified. The verb cannot stand bare at the end; it needs a 了, a result such as 完 or 好, or a place phrase like 放在桌子上. Negatives and modal verbs (没, 别, 想, 要) belong before 把, not before the verb.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你把这杯水喝了吧。 | Nǐ bǎ zhè bēi shuǐ hē le ba. | Drink up this glass of water. |
| 我把药放在桌子上了。 | Wǒ bǎ yào fàng zài zhuōzi shang le. | I put the medicine on the table. |
| 他把冰箱里的蛋糕都吃完了。 | Tā bǎ bīngxiāng li de dàngāo dōu chīwán le. | He ate up all the cake that was in the fridge. |
| 你还没把功课做完吗？ | Nǐ hái méi bǎ gōngkè zuòwán ma? | Haven't you finished your homework yet? |

**Watch out:** two classic slips are the bare verb (✗ 我把药吃 → 我把药吃了) and the misplaced negative (✗ 我把药没吃完 → 我没把药吃完).

### G5 · The preposition 对 (to, for)
**Pattern:** `A + 对 + B + Adjective / Verb phrase`

对 introduces the person or thing that an attitude, an effect or a reaction is directed at, where English uses "to", "for" or "towards": good for your body, allergic to flowers, kind to patients. The whole 对 phrase goes before the adjective or verb. English speakers tend to put it at the end of the sentence, which Chinese does not allow.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 多喝水对身体好。 | Duō hē shuǐ duì shēntǐ hǎo. | Drinking more water is good for your body. |
| 我对这种药过敏。 | Wǒ duì zhè zhǒng yào guòmǐn. | I am allergic to this kind of medicine. |
| 那位医生对病人特别好。 | Nà wèi yīshēng duì bìngrén tèbié hǎo. | That doctor is especially kind to patients. |
| 晚上睡得太晚对健康不好。 | Wǎnshang shuì de tài wǎn duì jiànkāng bù hǎo. | Going to sleep too late at night is bad for your health. |

**Watch out:** the 对 phrase comes first: ✗ 我过敏对这种药 → 我对这种药过敏.

### G6 · 越来越… (more and more)
**Pattern:** `Subject + 越来越 + Adjective / feeling verb (+ 了)`

越来越 shows that a quality keeps increasing as time passes: colder and colder, better and better, more and more fond of something. It stands directly before an adjective or a verb of feeling such as 喜欢 or 想, and a 了 at the end underlines that things have been changing. Because it already expresses degree, it cannot be combined with 很, 非常 or 更. To use it with an action verb, attach it to the description after 得 (说得越来越好).

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 天气越来越冷了。 | Tiānqì yuè lái yuè lěng le. | The weather is getting colder and colder. |
| 我的肚子越来越疼。 | Wǒ de dùzi yuè lái yuè téng. | My stomach hurts more and more. |
| 他的中文说得越来越好了。 | Tā de Zhōngwén shuō de yuè lái yuè hǎo le. | He speaks Chinese better and better. |
| 我越来越喜欢这个城市了。 | Wǒ yuè lái yuè xǐhuan zhège chéngshì le. | I like this city more and more. |

**Watch out:** ✗ 天气越来越很冷 → 天气越来越冷了.

### G7 · The conjunction 再说 (moreover)
**Pattern:** `Statement, reason 1, 再说 + reason 2`

When you have already given one reason for a decision and want to throw in another, start the extra reason with 再说: "besides", "and on top of that". It opens its clause, and 也 often appears later in the same clause. Unlike 而且, which can add any kind of information, 再说 only adds a further reason or excuse, so it needs an earlier reason to lean on.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我不想去医院，太远了，再说我也没有保险。 | Wǒ bù xiǎng qù yīyuàn, tài yuǎn le, zàishuō wǒ yě méiyǒu bǎoxiǎn. | I don't want to go to the hospital. It is too far, and besides, I have no insurance. |
| 今天别去打球了，你感冒了，再说外边也很冷。 | Jīntiān bié qù dǎqiú le, nǐ gǎnmào le, zàishuō wàibian yě hěn lěng. | Don't go and play ball today. You have a cold, and what's more, it is cold outside. |
| 我们在这家饭馆吃吧，菜很好吃，再说也不贵。 | Wǒmen zài zhè jiā fànguǎn chī ba, cài hěn hǎochī, zàishuō yě bú guì. | Let's eat at this restaurant. The food is tasty, and besides, it isn't expensive. |

**Watch out:** 再说 does not join two descriptions: ✗ 她很聪明，再说很用功 → 她不但很聪明，而且很用功.

## Dialogues

### Dialogue 1 · Too much birthday cake
*Setting: The morning after 安娜's birthday party, 马克 drags himself to the hospital with a stomach ache.*

| Speaker | Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 医生 | 你好，请坐。你哪儿不舒服？ | Nǐ hǎo, qǐng zuò. Nǐ nǎr bù shūfu? | Hello, have a seat. What is troubling you? |
| 马克 | 医生，我肚子疼死了，夜里上了好几次厕所。 | Yīshēng, wǒ dùzi téngsǐ le, yèli shàngle hǎo jǐ cì cèsuǒ. | Doctor, my stomach is killing me. I went to the toilet quite a few times in the night. |
| 医生 | 你昨天吃什么了？ | Nǐ zuótiān chī shénme le? | What did you eat yesterday? |
| 马克 | 昨天是安娜的生日，我吃了三块蛋糕，喝了四杯冰茶，回家以后又把冰箱里的半个西瓜吃完了。 | Zuótiān shì Ānnà de shēngrì, wǒ chīle sān kuài dàngāo, hēle sì bēi bīngchá, huí jiā yǐhòu yòu bǎ bīngxiāng li de bàn ge xīgua chīwán le. | Yesterday was Anna's birthday. I had three pieces of cake and four glasses of iced tea, and after I got home I finished the half watermelon in the fridge as well. |
| 医生 | 吃了那么多！你先躺下，我给你检查一下。发烧吗？ | Chīle nàme duō! Nǐ xiān tǎng xia, wǒ gěi nǐ jiǎnchá yíxià. Fāshāo ma? | You ate that much! Lie down first and I'll examine you. Any fever? |
| 马克 | 不发烧，可是我一吃东西，肚子就疼起来。 | Bù fāshāo, kěshì wǒ yì chī dōngxi, dùzi jiù téng qilai. | No fever, but the moment I eat anything my stomach starts to hurt. |
| 医生 | 没什么大问题，你是把肚子吃坏了。 | Méi shénme dà wèntí, nǐ shì bǎ dùzi chīhuài le. | It's nothing serious. You have upset your stomach with what you ate. |
| 马克 | 要打针吗？我最不喜欢打针了。 | Yào dǎzhēn ma? Wǒ zuì bù xǐhuan dǎzhēn le. | Do I need an injection? I hate injections more than anything. |
| 医生 | 不用打针，吃这种药就行。一天三次，一次两片。 | Búyòng dǎzhēn, chī zhè zhǒng yào jiù xíng. Yì tiān sān cì, yí cì liǎng piàn. | No injection needed, this medicine will do. Three times a day, two tablets each time. |
| 马克 | 对不起，您说得太快了，请再说一遍。 | Duìbuqǐ, nín shuō de tài kuài le, qǐng zài shuō yí biàn. | Sorry, you said that too fast. Please say it once more. |
| 医生 | 一天三次，一次两片。还有，这两天最好别吃冰的东西。 | Yì tiān sān cì, yí cì liǎng piàn. Háiyǒu, zhè liǎng tiān zuìhǎo bié chī bīng de dōngxi. | Three times a day, two tablets each time. Also, for the next couple of days you had better not eat anything ice-cold. |
| 马克 | 好吧。没办法，冰箱里的那块蛋糕只能送给安娜了。 | Hǎo ba. Méi bànfǎ, bīngxiāng li de nà kuài dàngāo zhǐ néng sòng gěi Ānnà le. | All right. Nothing for it, then: that piece of cake in the fridge will have to go to Anna. |

### Dialogue 2 · It is not a cold
*Setting: 林月 drops in on her cousin 陈乐, who got a puppy two weeks ago and now has red eyes.*

| Speaker | Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 林月 | 陈乐，你的眼睛怎么那么红？是不是生病了？ | Chén Lè, nǐ de yǎnjing zěnme nàme hóng? Shì bu shì shēngbìng le? | Chen Le, why are your eyes so red? Are you ill? |
| 陈乐 | 我想是感冒了。这几天眼睛和鼻子越来越痒。 | Wǒ xiǎng shì gǎnmào le. Zhè jǐ tiān yǎnjing hé bízi yuè lái yuè yǎng. | I think I've caught a cold. These last few days my eyes and nose have been getting itchier and itchier. |
| 林月 | 感冒会痒吗？你是不是对你的小狗过敏？ | Gǎnmào huì yǎng ma? Nǐ shì bu shì duì nǐ de xiǎo gǒu guòmǐn? | Does a cold make you itch? Could you be allergic to your puppy? |
| 陈乐 | 不会吧！它那么可爱。我昨天去药店买了一些感冒药，已经吃了两次了。 | Bú huì ba! Tā nàme kě'ài. Wǒ zuótiān qù yàodiàn mǎile yìxiē gǎnmào yào, yǐjīng chīle liǎng cì le. | Surely not! It is so cute. I went to the pharmacy yesterday and bought some cold medicine; I've already taken it twice. |
| 林月 | 你怎么能乱吃药呢？乱吃药对身体不好。赶快去医院检查一下吧。 | Nǐ zěnme néng luàn chī yào ne? Luàn chī yào duì shēntǐ bù hǎo. Gǎnkuài qù yīyuàn jiǎnchá yíxià ba. | How can you just take any old medicine? That is bad for you. Go to the hospital right away and get checked. |
| 陈乐 | 我这个星期工作忙死了，没空去。再说，看病要等好几个小时。 | Wǒ zhège xīngqī gōngzuò mángsǐ le, méi kòng qù. Zàishuō, kànbìng yào děng hǎo jǐ ge xiǎoshí. | Work is crazy this week, I have no time to go. Besides, seeing a doctor means waiting for hours. |
| 林月 | 你就是懒！你不是有健康保险吗？看病不用花很多钱。 | Nǐ jiùshì lǎn! Nǐ bú shì yǒu jiànkāng bǎoxiǎn ma? Kànbìng búyòng huā hěn duō qián. | You are just lazy! Don't you have health insurance? Seeing a doctor won't cost you much. |
| 陈乐 | 上次我感冒，休息了两天就好了。 | Shàngcì wǒ gǎnmào, xiūxile liǎng tiān jiù hǎo le. | Last time I had a cold, I rested for two days and was fine. |
| 林月 | 这次不一样。你最好明天就去，要不然病会越来越重的。 | Zhè cì bù yíyàng. Nǐ zuìhǎo míngtiān jiù qù, yàobùrán bìng huì yuè lái yuè zhòng de. | This time is different. You had better go tomorrow, otherwise it will get worse and worse. |
| 陈乐 | 好吧，我明天就去。可是要是我真的对狗过敏，怎么办？ | Hǎo ba, wǒ míngtiān jiù qù. Kěshì yàoshi wǒ zhēn de duì gǒu guòmǐn, zěnme bàn? | Fine, I'll go tomorrow. But what if I really am allergic to dogs? |
| 林月 | 那就把小狗送给我吧！ | Nà jiù bǎ xiǎo gǒu sòng gěi wǒ ba! | Then give the puppy to me! |

## Exercises

### E1 · Choose the right word {type=choice}
1. 我今天没吃早饭，现在饿___了。 [死 | 完 | 错] → 死
2. 我___花过敏，春天眼睛常常很痒。 [对 | 给 | 跟] → 对
3. 请你___这杯水喝了。 [把 | 对 | 比] → 把
4. 这本书我看过两___，很有意思。 [遍 | 片 | 位] → 遍
5. 冬天到了，天气___冷了。 [越来越 | 再说 | 要不然] → 越来越

### E2 · Put the words in order {type=order}
1. 把 / 弟弟 / 吃完了 / 蛋糕 / 冰箱里的 → 弟弟把冰箱里的蛋糕吃完了。
2. 好 / 多喝水 / 身体 / 对 → 多喝水对身体好。
3. 一天 / 这种药 / 三次 / 吃 → 这种药一天吃三次。
4. 起来 / 大家 / 了 / 都 / 笑 → 大家都笑起来了。
5. 越来越 / 他的 / 好 / 中文 / 了 → 他的中文越来越好了。

### E3 · Translate into Chinese {type=translate}
1. My head hurts terribly. → 我头疼死了。
2. I am allergic to this kind of medicine. → 我对这种药过敏。
3. Please say it one more time. → 请你再说一遍。
4. Put the medicine in the refrigerator. → 把药放在冰箱里。
5. You had better see a doctor right away, otherwise the illness will get worse and worse. → 你最好赶快去看病，要不然病会越来越重。

### E4 · Match the word {type=match}
1. 医院 → hospital
2. 发烧 → to have a fever
3. 检查 → to examine
4. 冰箱 → refrigerator
5. 感冒 → to have a cold
6. 过敏 → to be allergic to
7. 药店 → pharmacy
8. 休息 → to take a break, to rest
