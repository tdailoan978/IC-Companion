## Objectives

- After this lesson you can use 把 to say where something is put or who it is handed to.
- After this lesson you can greet a friend's family, use the right kinship terms and give a modest "not bad" answer with 还.

## Grammar

### G1 · 的, 得 and 地 compared
**Pattern:** `modifier + 的 + noun · verb / adj + 得 + comment · manner word + 地 + verb`

All three particles are pronounced de, but each has its own job, and the writing shows which job is being done. 的 ties a description or an owner to the noun that follows it. 得 comes straight after a verb or adjective and introduces a comment on how well the action goes or how far the state reaches. 地 turns an adjective, usually a doubled or two-syllable one, into a description of manner placed before the verb. A quick test: look at the word right after de. A noun calls for 的, a verb calls for 地, and if de follows a verb and what comes next is a judgement such as 很快 or 不得了, write 得.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这是我新买的箱子。 | Zhè shì wǒ xīn mǎi de xiāngzi. | This is the suitcase I just bought. |
| 她走得很快，已经到登机口了。 | Tā zǒu de hěn kuài, yǐjīng dào dēngjīkǒu le. | She walks fast and is already at the gate. |
| 奶奶高兴地笑了。 | Nǎinai gāoxìng de xiào le. | Grandma smiled happily. |
| 请你慢慢地说，我的中文不好。 | Qǐng nǐ mànmàn de shuō, wǒ de Zhōngwén bù hǎo. | Please speak slowly; my Chinese is not good. |

**Watch out:** a comment after the verb needs 得, not 的: ✗ 他说的很好 → 他说得很好.

### G2 · The 把 construction (II)
**Pattern:** `Subject + 把 + object + verb + 在 / 到 / 给 / 成 + place / person / new form`

Lesson 15 introduced 把 for saying what you do to a particular thing. With some verb endings 把 stops being a choice and becomes the only natural order: when the verb is followed by 在 or 到 plus a place, 给 plus a receiver, or 成 plus what the thing turns into. That slot after the verb is already taken, so the object has to move in front with 把. The object must be something specific that both speakers can identify, and negatives and modal verbs (没, 别, 想, 可以) stand before 把, not before the verb.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 请把箱子放在这儿。 | Qǐng bǎ xiāngzi fàng zài zhèr. | Please put the suitcase here. |
| 我把登机牌放到包里了。 | Wǒ bǎ dēngjīpái fàngdào bāo li le. | I put the boarding pass in my bag. |
| 请你把这个包送给林月。 | Qǐng nǐ bǎ zhège bāo sònggěi Lín Yuè. | Please give this bag to Lin Yue. |
| 他把“千”写成“十”了。 | Tā bǎ "qiān" xiěchéng "shí" le. | He wrote 千 as 十. |

**Watch out:** the object cannot be squeezed in between the verb and 在 / 到: ✗ 我放登机牌在包里了 → 我把登机牌放在包里了.

### G3 · …的时候 compared with …以后
**Pattern:** `A + 的时候，B (B happens during A) · A + 以后，B (B happens once A is over)`

Both are attached to the end of the first clause and both often come out as "when" in English, which is the source of the confusion. Use 的时候 if the two things overlap in time: B happens while A is going on. Use 以后 if A is finished before B starts; the verb in A then often carries 了. Ask yourself whether you could say "while" (的时候) or "after" (以后) in English and the choice becomes clear.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 托运行李的时候，要把护照拿出来。 | Tuōyùn xíngli de shíhou, yào bǎ hùzhào ná chulai. | When you check your luggage, you need to take out your passport. |
| 托运行李以后，我们去喝杯咖啡吧。 | Tuōyùn xíngli yǐhòu, wǒmen qù hē bēi kāfēi ba. | After we check the luggage, let's go for a coffee. |
| 飞机起飞的时候，别用手机。 | Fēijī qǐfēi de shíhou, bié yòng shǒujī. | Do not use your phone while the plane is taking off. |
| 飞机起飞以后，她就睡觉了。 | Fēijī qǐfēi yǐhòu, tā jiù shuìjiào le. | Once the plane had taken off, she went to sleep. |

**Watch out:** "I'll call you when I arrive" means after arriving, so it takes 以后: ✗ 我到北京的时候给你打电话 → 我到了北京以后给你打电话.

### G4 · 还 + positive adjective
**Pattern:** `还 + 好 / 可以 / 不错 / other positive adjective`

Before a positive adjective, 还 does not mean "still" or "also". It lowers the praise to "acceptable, fairly good, not bad", a notch below 很. People use it for modest answers about themselves and for lukewarm verdicts on food, hotels or exams. It only works with words that are positive to begin with, so 还 + 糟糕 or 还 + 贵 does not carry this meaning. Do not read 还好 as "even better": that would be 更好.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 坐了十几个小时的飞机，我还好，不太累。 | Zuò le shí jǐ ge xiǎoshí de fēijī, wǒ hái hǎo, bú tài lèi. | After a flight of ten-plus hours I am all right, not too tired. |
| 飞机上的饭还可以。 | Fēijī shang de fàn hái kěyǐ. | The food on the plane was passable. |
| 这家旅馆还不错，也不太贵。 | Zhè jiā lǚguǎn hái búcuò, yě bú tài guì. | This hotel is pretty decent, and not too expensive either. |
| 他的中文说得还不错。 | Tā de Zhōngwén shuō de hái búcuò. | He speaks Chinese fairly well. |

**Watch out:** 还 and 很 do not combine: ✗ 还很不错 → 还不错.

### G5 · Kinship terms
**Pattern:** `father's side: 爷爷, 奶奶, 伯伯, 叔叔, 姑姑 · mother's side: 外公, 外婆, 舅舅, 阿姨`

Chinese gives relatives different names depending on the side of the family, the generation and, for the father's brothers, who is older. Your father's parents are 爷爷 and 奶奶; your mother's parents are 外公 and 外婆, where 外 marks the "outside" line. Your father's older brother is 伯伯, his younger brother 叔叔 and his sister 姑姑; your mother's brother is 舅舅 and her sister 阿姨. Cousins on the mother's side, or through the father's sisters, take 表 (表哥, 表姐). Children and young adults also say 叔叔 and 阿姨 to unrelated people of their parents' age, which is the polite way to address a friend's father and mother.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我爷爷奶奶住在北京。 | Wǒ yéye nǎinai zhù zài Běijīng. | My father's parents live in Beijing. |
| 外公外婆是妈妈的父母。 | Wàigōng wàipó shì māma de fùmǔ. | 外公 and 外婆 are your mother's parents. |
| 爸爸的哥哥叫伯伯，爸爸的弟弟叫叔叔。 | Bàba de gēge jiào bóbo, bàba de dìdi jiào shūshu. | Dad's older brother is called 伯伯 and Dad's younger brother is called 叔叔. |
| 妈妈的弟弟是我舅舅，爸爸的妹妹是我姑姑。 | Māma de dìdi shì wǒ jiùjiu, bàba de mèimei shì wǒ gūgu. | Mom's younger brother is my 舅舅, and Dad's younger sister is my 姑姑. |

**Watch out:** one English word covers two Chinese ones, so do not call your mother's father 爷爷; he is 外公.

## Dialogues

### Dialogue 1 · An overweight suitcase
*Setting: Anna has driven Mark and Lin Yue to the airport and stands with them at the check-in desk.*

| Speaker | Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 服务员 | 您好！请把护照给我。您有几件行李要托运？ | Nín hǎo! Qǐng bǎ hùzhào gěi wǒ. Nín yǒu jǐ jiàn xíngli yào tuōyùn? | Hello! Your passport, please. How many pieces of luggage are you checking? |
| 马克 | 托运一个箱子，这个包我自己带上飞机。 | Tuōyùn yí ge xiāngzi, zhège bāo wǒ zìjǐ dài shàng fēijī. | One suitcase to check. I will carry this bag onto the plane myself. |
| 服务员 | 请把箱子放在这儿。……对不起，您的箱子超重了。 | Qǐng bǎ xiāngzi fàng zài zhèr. ... Duìbuqǐ, nín de xiāngzi chāozhòng le. | Please put the suitcase here. ... I am sorry, your suitcase is overweight. |
| 林月 | 我早就告诉你了！你给我父母买的礼物太多了。 | Wǒ zǎo jiù gàosu nǐ le! Nǐ gěi wǒ fùmǔ mǎi de lǐwù tài duō le. | I told you ages ago! You bought far too many presents for my parents. |
| 马克 | 没关系，我把这几本书放到包里。现在呢？ | Méi guānxi, wǒ bǎ zhè jǐ běn shū fàngdào bāo li. Xiànzài ne? | Never mind, I will move these books into my bag. How about now? |
| 服务员 | 现在可以了。这是您的登机牌，登机口在那边。 | Xiànzài kěyǐ le. Zhè shì nín de dēngjīpái, dēngjīkǒu zài nàbian. | Now it is fine. Here is your boarding pass; the gate is over there. |
| 安娜 | 飞机几点起飞？你们到了以后，马上给我发个短信。 | Fēijī jǐ diǎn qǐfēi? Nǐmen dào le yǐhòu, mǎshàng gěi wǒ fā ge duǎnxìn. | What time does the plane take off? Text me as soon as you have arrived. |
| 马克 | 十一点起飞。安娜，你怎么哭了？ | Shíyī diǎn qǐfēi. Ānnà, nǐ zěnme kū le? | At eleven. Anna, why are you crying? |
| 安娜 | 我没哭！我只是眼睛有点儿痒。 | Wǒ méi kū! Wǒ zhǐshì yǎnjing yǒudiǎnr yǎng. | I am not crying! My eyes are just a bit itchy. |
| 林月 | 别难受，我们八月就回来了。你回去的时候慢慢地开，小心一点儿。 | Bié nánshòu, wǒmen bāyuè jiù huílai le. Nǐ huíqu de shíhou mànmàn de kāi, xiǎoxīn yìdiǎnr. | Do not be sad, we will be back in August. Drive slowly on your way home, and be careful. |
| 安娜 | 好。你们快走吧，一路平安！ | Hǎo. Nǐmen kuài zǒu ba, yí lù píng'ān! | I will. Off you go now. Have a safe trip! |

### Dialogue 2 · Welcome to Beijing
*Setting: Chen Le is waiting for Lin Yue and Mark in the arrivals hall of Beijing Capital Airport.*

| Speaker | Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 陈乐 | 林月！马克！欢迎你们来北京！ | Lín Yuè! Mǎkè! Huānyíng nǐmen lái Běijīng! | Lin Yue! Mark! Welcome to Beijing! |
| 林月 | 表哥！谢谢你来首都机场接我们。你好像瘦了。 | Biǎogē! Xièxie nǐ lái Shǒudū Jīchǎng jiē wǒmen. Nǐ hǎoxiàng shòu le. | Cousin! Thanks for coming to the Capital Airport to meet us. You seem to have lost weight. |
| 陈乐 | 工作忙得不得了，当然瘦了。马克，坐了那么久的飞机，累不累？ | Gōngzuò máng de bùdéliǎo, dāngrán shòu le. Mǎkè, zuò le nàme jiǔ de fēijī, lèi bu lèi? | Work is crazy busy, so of course I have. Mark, are you tired after such a long flight? |
| 马克 | 还好，我在飞机上睡得还不错。 | Hái hǎo, wǒ zài fēijī shang shuì de hái búcuò. | I am OK. I slept fairly well on the plane. |
| 陈乐 | 你的中文说得真好！来，我帮你把箱子拿到车上。 | Nǐ de Zhōngwén shuō de zhēn hǎo! Lái, wǒ bāng nǐ bǎ xiāngzi nádào chē shang. | Your Chinese is really good! Here, let me carry your suitcase to the car. |
| 马克 | 谢谢。见到林月的父母，我应该怎么叫他们？ | Xièxie. Jiàndào Lín Yuè de fùmǔ, wǒ yīnggāi zěnme jiào tāmen? | Thank you. When I meet Lin Yue's parents, what should I call them? |
| 陈乐 | 叫叔叔、阿姨就可以了。爷爷奶奶也来了，他们已经在饭馆等我们了。 | Jiào shūshu, āyí jiù kěyǐ le. Yéye nǎinai yě lái le, tāmen yǐjīng zài fànguǎn děng wǒmen le. | Just call them 叔叔 and 阿姨. Grandpa and Grandma have come too; they are already waiting for us at the restaurant. |
| 林月 | 饭馆？我们不先回家吗？ | Fànguǎn? Wǒmen bù xiān huí jiā ma? | The restaurant? Are we not going home first? |
| 陈乐 | 爷爷说，马克第一次来北京，一定要先吃烤鸭。 | Yéye shuō, Mǎkè dì yī cì lái Běijīng, yídìng yào xiān chī kǎoyā. | Grandpa says that since it is Mark's first time in Beijing, he absolutely has to eat roast duck first. |
| 马克 | 太好了！我在飞机上吃的是素餐，现在饿得不得了。 | Tài hǎo le! Wǒ zài fēijī shang chī de shì sùcān, xiànzài è de bùdéliǎo. | Great! I had the vegetarian meal on the plane and now I am starving. |
| 林月 | 那你要慢慢地吃，奶奶点的菜一定多得不得了。 | Nà nǐ yào mànmàn de chī, nǎinai diǎn de cài yídìng duō de bùdéliǎo. | Then pace yourself: Grandma is sure to have ordered a mountain of dishes. |

## Exercises

### E1 · Choose the right word {type=choice}
1. 他高兴___笑了。 [地 | 得 | 的] → 地
2. 她中文说___很好。 [得 | 的 | 地] → 得
3. 请___箱子放在这儿。 [把 | 被 | 比] → 把
4. 我到了北京___，马上给你打电话。 [以后 | 的 | 地] → 以后
5. 爸爸的妈妈是我的___。 [奶奶 | 阿姨 | 叔叔] → 奶奶

### E2 · Put the words in order {type=order}
1. 把 / 我 / 包里了 / 登机牌 / 放到 → 我把登机牌放到包里了。
2. 安娜 / 哭了 / 地 / 难受 → 安娜难受地哭了。
3. 吃饭 / 别 / 的时候 / 看手机 → 吃饭的时候别看手机。
4. 还不错 / 烤鸭 / 这家饭馆 / 的 → 这家饭馆的烤鸭还不错。
5. 来 / 爷爷奶奶 / 欢迎 / 北京 / 你们 → 爷爷奶奶欢迎你们来北京。

### E3 · Translate into Chinese {type=translate}
1. Please put your luggage on the table. → 请把行李放在桌子上。
2. My suitcase is overweight. → 我的箱子超重了。
3. After the plane takes off, I want to sleep. → 飞机起飞以后，我想睡觉。
4. This bag is all right, and it is not too expensive either. → 这个包还可以，也不太贵。
5. Grandpa walked slowly to the boarding gate. → 爷爷慢慢地走到登机口。

### E4 · Match the word {type=match}
1. 行李 → luggage
2. 托运 → to check (luggage)
3. 箱子 → suitcase
4. 登机牌 → boarding pass
5. 起飞 → to take off
6. 欢迎 → to welcome
7. 瘦 → thin
8. 烤鸭 → roast duck
