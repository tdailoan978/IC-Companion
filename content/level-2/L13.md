# Lesson 13 · 问路 · Asking Directions

## Objectives
- After this lesson you can say where a place is by using location words such as 旁边, 中间, 里边 and the four compass directions.
- After this lesson you can ask for and give walking directions: go straight, pass an intersection, turn left or right.
- After this lesson you can compare two things with 没(有) and say something is "not as … as" another.
- After this lesson you can talk about things you have or have not experienced with 过.
- After this lesson you can link two actions that happen one right after the other with 一…就….

## Grammar

### G1 · Direction and location words
**Pattern:** `A + 在 + B (的) + location word`

Chinese builds location words from a direction plus 边 or 面: 上边, 下边, 前边 / 前面, 后边, 左边, 右边, 里边, 外边, and for the compass 东边, 南边, 西边, 北边. 旁边 (beside) and 中间 (in between) work the same way. The key point is the order: the reference point comes first and the location word comes after it, the reverse of English "next to the library". The 的 between them is usually dropped in speech. A whole "place + location word" phrase can also start a sentence, followed by 有 to say what is there.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 书店在图书馆旁边。 | Shūdiàn zài túshūguǎn pángbiān. | The bookstore is next to the library. |
| 运动场在学校的东边。 | Yùndòngchǎng zài xuéxiào de dōngbian. | The sports field is on the east side of the school. |
| 咖啡店在书店和饭馆中间。 | Kāfēidiàn zài shūdiàn hé fànguǎn zhōngjiān. | The coffee shop is between the bookstore and the restaurant. |
| 电脑中心里边有很多学生。 | Diànnǎo zhōngxīn lǐbian yǒu hěn duō xuésheng. | There are a lot of students inside the computer center. |

**Watch out:** do not copy English word order: ✗ 书店在旁边图书馆 should be 书店在图书馆旁边.

### G2 · Comparative sentences with 没(有)
**Pattern:** `A + 没(有) + B + Adj`

This is the everyday way to say "A is not as … as B". It is the natural negative of a 比 sentence: if 地铁比公共汽车快, then 公共汽车没有地铁快. The adjective stays bare, with no 很 and no 不 in front of it, and 有 can be left out in casual speech. Use it whenever A falls short of B on some scale. Avoid reaching for 不比 as the negative: that phrase only denies that A is ahead and is mostly used to contradict someone.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 书店没有图书馆远。 | Shūdiàn méiyǒu túshūguǎn yuǎn. | The bookstore is not as far as the library. |
| 我弟弟没有我高。 | Wǒ dìdi méiyǒu wǒ gāo. | My younger brother is not as tall as I am. |
| 坐公共汽车没有坐地铁快。 | Zuò gōnggòng qìchē méiyǒu zuò dìtiě kuài. | Taking the bus is not as fast as taking the subway. |
| 这家饭馆的菜没有那家好吃。 | Zhè jiā fànguǎn de cài méiyǒu nà jiā hǎochī. | The food at this restaurant is not as good as at that one. |

**Watch out:** no degree adverb before the adjective: ✗ 我没有他很高 should be 我没有他高.

### G3 · 那么 to indicate degree
**Pattern:** `(A + 没有 + B) + 那么 + Adj`

那么 before an adjective means "that", "so" or "to that degree". It points at a level both speakers already have in mind, either because it was just mentioned or because it is obvious from the situation. It is especially common in 没有 comparisons, where it adds the feeling of "not as … as that". For something right here in front of you, 这么 is used in the same position. The common slip is treating 那么 as a simple replacement for 很: 很 just describes, while 那么 always refers back to a known degree.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 运动场没有你说的那么远。 | Yùndòngchǎng méiyǒu nǐ shuō de nàme yuǎn. | The sports field is not as far as you said. |
| 这儿的冬天没有北京那么冷。 | Zhèr de dōngtiān méiyǒu Běijīng nàme lěng. | Winter here is not as cold as in Beijing. |
| 中国城那么远，我们坐地铁去吧。 | Zhōngguóchéng nàme yuǎn, wǒmen zuò dìtiě qù ba. | Chinatown is so far away, let's take the subway. |
| 你为什么走得那么快？ | Nǐ wèishénme zǒu de nàme kuài? | Why are you walking so fast? |

**Watch out:** 那么 goes directly before the adjective, never after it: ✗ 没有北京冷那么.

### G4 · 到 + place + 去 + action
**Pattern:** `Subject + 到 + place + 去 / 来 + action`

Use this frame to say that someone goes (or comes) to a place in order to do something there. The destination is named first with 到, then 去 or 来, and the purpose comes last, so the sentence follows the order in which things actually happen: arrive, then act. It means the same as the shorter 去 + place + action that you already know and is a little more explicit about the destination. Learners often put the purpose first or push the place to the end, as English does.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我到图书馆去看书。 | Wǒ dào túshūguǎn qù kàn shū. | I'm going to the library to read. |
| 他们到中国城去吃饭了。 | Tāmen dào Zhōngguóchéng qù chīfàn le. | They went to Chinatown to eat. |
| 明天我要到书店去买地图。 | Míngtiān wǒ yào dào shūdiàn qù mǎi dìtú. | Tomorrow I'm going to the bookstore to buy a map. |
| 你到我家来玩儿吧。 | Nǐ dào wǒ jiā lái wánr ba. | Come over to my place and hang out. |

**Watch out:** the place cannot trail at the end: ✗ 我去看书到图书馆 should be 我到图书馆去看书.

### G5 · The dynamic particle 过 (experience)
**Pattern:** `Subject + Verb + 过 + (Object)`

过 right after a verb says that the action has happened at least once at some point before now. The focus is on having had the experience, not on when it happened or how it ended. That is the difference from 了, which reports one particular completed event. The negative is 没(有) + Verb + 过, and 过 must stay in the negative. To say how many times, put the number and 次 after 过. In this use 过 is pronounced in the neutral tone.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我去过中国城。 | Wǒ qùguo Zhōngguóchéng. | I have been to Chinatown. |
| 你吃过日本菜吗？ | Nǐ chīguo Rìběn cài ma? | Have you ever eaten Japanese food? |
| 我没学过日文。 | Wǒ méi xuéguo Rìwén. | I have never studied Japanese. |
| 那个公园我们去过两次。 | Nàge gōngyuán wǒmen qùguo liǎng cì. | We have been to that park twice. |

**Watch out:** negate with 没, not 不, and keep 过: ✗ 我不去过东京 should be 我没去过东京.

### G6 · Verb reduplication (I)
**Pattern:** `Verb + Verb + (Object)`

Saying a one-syllable verb twice makes the action sound short, light and casual, like "have a look" or "think it over for a moment". This softens requests and suggestions, so it turns up constantly with 吧, 请 and 让. The second syllable is read in the neutral tone: kànkan, wènwen, zǒuzou. The doubled verb is for actions that have not happened yet or that happen from time to time. It does not fit an action that is in progress, so it cannot follow 正在, and it is not combined with 过.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你看看这张地图。 | Nǐ kànkan zhè zhāng dìtú. | Take a look at this map. |
| 让我想想。 | Ràng wǒ xiǎngxiang. | Let me think for a moment. |
| 你问问那位师傅吧。 | Nǐ wènwen nà wèi shīfu ba. | Why don't you ask that gentleman over there. |
| 我们到公园去走走吧。 | Wǒmen dào gōngyuán qù zǒuzou ba. | Let's go for a little walk in the park. |

**Watch out:** not with an action in progress: ✗ 我正在看看地图 should be 我正在看地图.

### G7 · Resultative complements (II)
**Pattern:** `Verb + result word (到 / 错 / 懂 / 好 / 完 / 清楚) + (Object)`

A resultative complement is a second word stuck onto a verb to tell you how the action turned out. 找 only means "to look for", while 找到 means the search succeeded. Useful ones at this stage are 到 (managed to reach or get), 错 (did it wrongly), 懂 (understood), 好 (done and ready), 完 (finished) and 清楚 (clearly). Verb and result act as one unit, so the object and 了 come after both. Because the negative says the result was not achieved, it uses 没(有), and 了 is then dropped.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我找到那家书店了。 | Wǒ zhǎodào nà jiā shūdiàn le. | I found that bookstore. |
| 对不起，我走错路了。 | Duìbuqǐ, wǒ zǒucuò lù le. | Sorry, I took the wrong road. |
| 师傅说的话你听懂了吗？ | Shīfu shuō de huà nǐ tīngdǒng le ma? | Did you understand what the driver said? |
| 那张地图我还没买到。 | Nà zhāng dìtú wǒ hái méi mǎidào. | I still haven't managed to buy that map. |

**Watch out:** nothing may come between the verb and its result: ✗ 我找了到书店 should be 我找到书店了.

### G8 · 一…就… (as soon as… then…)
**Pattern:** `Subject + 一 + Verb 1 + (Subject 2) + 就 + Verb 2`

This pair ties two actions tightly together: the moment the first one happens, the second follows. It can describe a single occasion ("as soon as I get there, I'll call") or a habit ("whenever I'm nervous, I say the wrong thing"). Both 一 and 就 are adverbs, so each one sits after its subject and directly before its verb. If the two halves share a subject, say it once at the start. In pinyin, 一 is written yí before a fourth tone and yì before the other tones.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我一下课就回宿舍。 | Wǒ yí xiàkè jiù huí sùshè. | I go back to the dorm as soon as class is over. |
| 你一到路口就往右拐。 | Nǐ yí dào lùkǒu jiù wǎng yòu guǎi. | Turn right as soon as you get to the intersection. |
| 他一看地图就知道怎么走了。 | Tā yí kàn dìtú jiù zhīdào zěnme zǒu le. | One look at the map and he knew how to get there. |
| 我一紧张就说错话。 | Wǒ yì jǐnzhāng jiù shuōcuò huà. | Whenever I get nervous, I say the wrong thing. |

**Watch out:** 就 never goes in front of the subject: ✗ 我一到，就我给你打电话 should be 我一到就给你打电话.

## Dialogues

### Dialogue 1 · A new bookstore
*Setting: After class, 林月 tries to talk 马克 into visiting a new bookstore instead of going to the sports field.*

| Speaker | Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 林月 | 马克，你上哪儿去？ | Mǎkè, nǐ shàng nǎr qù? | Mark, where are you off to? |
| 马克 | 我到运动场去打球。你去不去？ | Wǒ dào yùndòngchǎng qù dǎ qiú. Nǐ qù bu qù? | I'm going to the sports field to play ball. Are you coming? |
| 林月 | 我不去。听说学校旁边新开了一家书店，你去过吗？ | Wǒ bú qù. Tīngshuō xuéxiào pángbiān xīn kāi le yì jiā shūdiàn, nǐ qùguo ma? | No. I hear a new bookstore has opened next to campus. Have you been? |
| 马克 | 没去过。在什么地方？离这儿远不远？ | Méi qùguo. Zài shénme dìfang? Lí zhèr yuǎn bu yuǎn? | Never. Where is it? Is it far from here? |
| 林月 | 很近，就在咖啡店和电脑中心中间，没有运动场那么远。 | Hěn jìn, jiù zài kāfēidiàn hé diànnǎo zhōngxīn zhōngjiān, méiyǒu yùndòngchǎng nàme yuǎn. | It's close, right between the coffee shop and the computer center, not as far as the sports field. |
| 马克 | 书店有什么好玩的？我一看书就想睡觉。 | Shūdiàn yǒu shénme hǎowán de? Wǒ yí kàn shū jiù xiǎng shuìjiào. | What's fun about a bookstore? The minute I open a book I want to sleep. |
| 林月 | 那家书店里边有茶，有音乐，还常常有活动。 | Nà jiā shūdiàn lǐbian yǒu chá, yǒu yīnyuè, hái chángcháng yǒu huódòng. | Inside that bookstore there's tea, there's music, and they often hold events too. |
| 马克 | 什么活动？ | Shénme huódòng? | What kind of events? |
| 林月 | 今天下午有人教大家写汉字。你去看看吧！ | Jīntiān xiàwǔ yǒu rén jiāo dàjiā xiě Hànzì. Nǐ qù kànkan ba! | This afternoon someone is teaching everybody to write Chinese characters. Come and have a look! |
| 马克 | 我写的字没有你的那么好看……好吧，我去看看，球明天再打。 | Wǒ xiě de zì méiyǒu nǐ de nàme hǎokàn… Hǎo ba, wǒ qù kànkan, qiú míngtiān zài dǎ. | My characters aren't as nice as yours… Fine, I'll take a look. The ball game can wait until tomorrow. |
| 林月 | 太好了！走吧，我知道怎么走。 | Tài hǎo le! Zǒu ba, wǒ zhīdào zěnme zǒu. | Great! Let's go, I know the way. |

### Dialogue 2 · Lost in Chinatown
*Setting: 安娜 is walking to meet 林月 at a dumpling restaurant in Chinatown and phones her for directions.*

| Speaker | Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 安娜 | 喂，林月，我到中国城了，可是还没找到你说的那家饭馆。 | Wéi, Lín Yuè, wǒ dào Zhōngguóchéng le, kěshì hái méi zhǎodào nǐ shuō de nà jiā fànguǎn. | Hello, Lin Yue, I've made it to Chinatown, but I still haven't found that restaurant you mentioned. |
| 林月 | 别紧张。你现在在哪儿？前面有什么？ | Bié jǐnzhāng. Nǐ xiànzài zài nǎr? Qiánmiàn yǒu shénme? | Don't worry. Where are you now? What's in front of you? |
| 安娜 | 我在地铁站外边，前面是一个大路口，路口旁边有一家书店。 | Wǒ zài dìtiězhàn wàibian, qiánmiàn shì yí ge dà lùkǒu, lùkǒu pángbiān yǒu yì jiā shūdiàn. | I'm outside the subway station. There's a big intersection ahead with a bookstore beside it. |
| 林月 | 好。你从地铁站一直往南走，过两个路口，到第三个路口往右拐。 | Hǎo. Nǐ cóng dìtiězhàn yìzhí wǎng nán zǒu, guò liǎng ge lùkǒu, dào dì-sān ge lùkǒu wǎng yòu guǎi. | OK. From the station go straight south, pass two intersections, and turn right at the third. |
| 安娜 | 等等，你说得太快了，我没听清楚。 | Děngdeng, nǐ shuō de tài kuài le, wǒ méi tīng qīngchu. | Hang on, you're talking too fast, I didn't catch that. |
| 林月 | 没关系，你看看手机上的谷歌地图吧。 | Méi guānxi, nǐ kànkan shǒujī shang de Gǔgē dìtú ba. | Never mind, just have a look at Google Maps on your phone. |
| 安娜 | 好。我在东京的时候也用过，可是那儿的地图都是日文，我没看懂，走错了三次。 | Hǎo. Wǒ zài Dōngjīng de shíhou yě yòngguo, kěshì nàr de dìtú dōu shì Rìwén, wǒ méi kàndǒng, zǒucuò le sān cì. | Sure. I used it in Tokyo too, but the map there was all in Japanese. I couldn't read it and went the wrong way three times. |
| 林月 | 这次的地图是英文的，没问题吧？ | Zhè cì de dìtú shì Yīngwén de, méi wèntí ba? | This time the map is in English, so no problem, right? |
| 安娜 | 没问题，我看到了。往右拐以后呢？ | Méi wèntí, wǒ kàndào le. Wǎng yòu guǎi yǐhòu ne? | No problem, I see it. And after I turn right? |
| 林月 | 再往前走一点儿，饭馆就在你的左边，旁边有一个小公园。 | Zài wǎng qián zǒu yìdiǎnr, fànguǎn jiù zài nǐ de zuǒbian, pángbiān yǒu yí ge xiǎo gōngyuán. | Walk on a little further and the restaurant is right there on your left, with a small park next to it. |
| 安娜 | 好，我一找到就进去。你先点菜吧，我饿了！ | Hǎo, wǒ yì zhǎodào jiù jìnqu. Nǐ xiān diǎncài ba, wǒ è le! | Good, I'll come in the moment I find it. Go ahead and order, I'm starving! |
| 林月 | 好，我先点两盘饺子。你快来吧！ | Hǎo, wǒ xiān diǎn liǎng pán jiǎozi. Nǐ kuài lái ba! | OK, I'll start with two plates of dumplings. Hurry up! |

## Exercises

### E1 · Choose the right word {type=choice}
1. 书店在图书馆___。 [旁边 | 一直 | 路口] → 旁边
2. 我的宿舍___你的宿舍那么远。 [没有 | 比 | 离] → 没有
3. 我没吃___日本菜。 [过 | 了 | 的] → 过
4. 我一下课___回家。 [就 | 才 | 再] → 就
5. 我还没找___那家书店。 [到 | 懂 | 完] → 到

### E2 · Put the words in order {type=order}
1. 在 / 中间 / 饭馆和公园 / 书店 → 书店在饭馆和公园中间。
2. 远 / 运动场 / 没有 / 那么 / 你说的 → 运动场没有你说的那么远。
3. 去 / 到 / 我们 / 吃饭 / 中国城 → 我们到中国城去吃饭。
4. 日文 / 没 / 她 / 学过 → 她没学过日文。
5. 就 / 一 / 往右拐 / 到路口 / 你 → 你一到路口就往右拐。

### E3 · Translate into Chinese {type=translate}
1. The sports field is not far from my dorm. → 运动场离我的宿舍不远。
2. Have you ever been to Tokyo? → 你去过东京吗？
3. Go straight south from here and turn left at the second intersection. → 从这儿一直往南走，到第二个路口往左拐。
4. Take a look at the map. Did we go the wrong way? → 你看看地图，我们是不是走错了？
5. The subway station is not as close as you said. → 地铁站没有你说的那么近。

### E4 · Match the word {type=match}
1. 远 → far
2. 近 → near
3. 地图 → map
4. 路口 → intersection
5. 拐 → to turn
6. 地方 → place
7. 中间 → middle
8. 一直 → straight, continuously
