# Lesson 11 · 天气 · Weather

## Objectives
- After this lesson you can say what the weather is like and name the four seasons.
- After this lesson you can compare two days, places or things with 比 and say how big the difference is.
- After this lesson you can report a forecast with 会 and announce that something has changed with sentence-final 了.
- After this lesson you can complain that something has happened again with 又.
- After this lesson you can admit a point and then give the other side with "A 是 A，可是…".

## Grammar

### G1 · Comparative sentences with 比 (I)
**Pattern:** `A + 比 + B + Adj (+ 一点儿 / 得多 / 多了)`

比 sets one thing against another: A comes first, then 比, then the thing A is measured against, and the adjective comes last. Chinese has no "-er" ending, so the bare adjective already means "colder", "bigger" and so on. To say how large the gap is, add the amount after the adjective: 一点儿 for "a little", 得多 or 多了 for "a lot". The only degree adverb allowed in front of the adjective is 更 ("even more"), which tells the listener that B already has plenty of that quality. The commonest mistake is to put 很 or 非常 before the adjective, as if translating "very".

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这个公园比那个公园大。 | Zhège gōngyuán bǐ nàge gōngyuán dà. | This park is bigger than that park. |
| 今天比昨天暖和一点儿。 | Jīntiān bǐ zuótiān nuǎnhuo yìdiǎnr. | Today is a little warmer than yesterday. |
| 纽约的冬天比加州冷得多。 | Niǔyuē de dōngtiān bǐ Jiāzhōu lěng de duō. | Winter in New York is much colder than in California. |
| 昨天很热，今天比昨天更热。 | Zuótiān hěn rè, jīntiān bǐ zuótiān gèng rè. | Yesterday was hot, and today is even hotter than yesterday. |

**Watch out:** never put 很 inside a 比 sentence: ✗ 今天比昨天很冷 should be 今天比昨天冷得多.

### G2 · Sentence-final 了 for a change of situation (了 III)
**Pattern:** `Sentence + 了` (the situation is new or has changed)

A 了 at the very end of a sentence can announce that things are different now from how they were, or from what the listener assumed. It is not a past-tense marker: 下雪了 means the snow has just started and is probably still falling. The predicate can be an adjective, a verb, a time or a noun. With 不 it gives "not any more" or "not after all", so 我不去了 means the plan to go has been dropped. The typical mistake is to read every 了 as "past" and miss this "new situation" meaning.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你看，下雪了！ | Nǐ kàn, xiàxuě le! | Look, it's snowing! |
| 春天来了，天气暖和了。 | Chūntiān lái le, tiānqì nuǎnhuo le. | Spring is here and the weather has turned warm. |
| 下雨了，我今天不去公园了。 | Xiàyǔ le, wǒ jīntiān bú qù gōngyuán le. | It has started raining, so I'm not going to the park today after all. |
| 十点了，我们回去吧。 | Shí diǎn le, wǒmen huíqu ba. | It's ten o'clock already; let's go back. |

**Watch out:** 我不去了 (I've changed my mind, I'm not going) is not the same as 我没去 (I didn't go).

### G3 · The modal verb 会 (will) (II)
**Pattern:** `Subject + (不) 会 + Verb / Adj (+ 的)`

You already know 会 as "know how to". Its second job is prediction: the speaker thinks something will, or will probably, happen. That makes it the natural word for forecasts, promises and guesses about the future. The negative is 不会 ("won't"), the question is 会…吗 or 会不会, and a final 的 can be added to sound reassuring. Learners often leave 会 out and rely on a time word alone; 明天下雨 is understandable, but it sounds like a fixed fact, not a prediction.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 天气预报说明天会下雪。 | Tiānqì yùbào shuō míngtiān huì xiàxuě. | The weather forecast says it will snow tomorrow. |
| 别紧张，面试不会很难。 | Bié jǐnzhāng, miànshì bú huì hěn nán. | Don't be nervous, the interview won't be very hard. |
| 下个星期会更冷吗？ | Xià ge xīngqī huì gèng lěng ma? | Will it be even colder next week? |
| 他说他今天晚上会给我打电话。 | Tā shuō tā jīntiān wǎnshang huì gěi wǒ dǎ diànhuà. | He said he would call me tonight. |

**Watch out:** negate a prediction with 不会, never with 没: ✗ 明天没会下雨.

### G4 · Adjective + (一)点儿 (a bit)
**Pattern:** `Adj + (一)点儿`

Placed after an adjective, (一)点儿 means "a bit more of that quality". It always implies a comparison, either with something else or with how things are right now, which is why it is so common in requests (快点儿, "a bit quicker") and at the end of 比 sentences. The 一 is often dropped in speech. Do not confuse it with 有(一)点儿, which goes before the adjective and voices a mild complaint: 有点儿冷 is "it's rather cold", while 冷一点儿 is "a bit colder". Mixing up these two positions is the classic error.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你说得太快了，慢一点儿，好吗？ | Nǐ shuō de tài kuài le, màn yìdiǎnr, hǎo ma? | You're speaking too fast. A bit slower, OK? |
| 这件衬衫太贵了，便宜一点儿吧。 | Zhè jiàn chènshān tài guì le, piányi yìdiǎnr ba. | This shirt is too expensive. Make it a bit cheaper. |
| 快点儿，我们没有时间了！ | Kuài diǎnr, wǒmen méiyǒu shíjiān le! | Hurry up a bit, we've run out of time! |
| 加州的冬天比这儿暖和一点儿。 | Jiāzhōu de dōngtiān bǐ zhèr nuǎnhuo yìdiǎnr. | Winter in California is a bit warmer than here. |

**Watch out:** (一)点儿 cannot stand before the adjective: ✗ 今天一点儿冷 should be 今天有一点儿冷.

### G5 · The adverb 又 (again)
**Pattern:** `Subject + 又 + Verb (+ 了)`

又 says that an action or situation has repeated: it happened before, and now it has happened once more. Because the repetition is already real, the sentence usually ends in 了, and the tone is often one of surprise or annoyance. 又 sits before the verb, and before 没 or 不 when the repeated thing is a failure to act. English "again" covers the future as well, but Chinese uses 再 for a repetition that has not happened yet. Choosing between 又 and 再 is where learners slip most.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 昨天下雨，今天又下雨了。 | Zuótiān xiàyǔ, jīntiān yòu xiàyǔ le. | It rained yesterday, and today it's raining again. |
| 你怎么又没做功课？ | Nǐ zěnme yòu méi zuò gōngkè? | How come you didn't do your homework again? |
| 他上个周末去滑冰，这个周末又去了。 | Tā shàng ge zhōumò qù huábīng, zhège zhōumò yòu qù le. | He went skating last weekend, and he went again this weekend. |
| 糟糕，又下雪了，我们今天不能打球了。 | Zāogāo, yòu xiàxuě le, wǒmen jīntiān bù néng dǎqiú le. | Oh no, it's snowing again. We can't play ball today now. |

**Watch out:** for "again" in the future use 再: ✗ 我明天又来 should be 我明天再来.

### G6 · Adj/Verb + 是 + Adj/Verb, 可是/但是… (it is…, but…)
**Pattern:** `A + 是 + A，可是 / 但是 + B`

This pattern lets you agree with a point before you push back: "cold it is, but…". The same adjective or verb appears on both sides of 是, and the second clause, introduced by 可是 or 但是, carries what you really want to say. It sounds softer and more polite than a flat contradiction. Keep the repeated word bare and identical; adverbs such as 很 belong in the second clause. Learners tend either to forget the repetition or to add 很 to the first half.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 滑冰难是难，可是很好玩。 | Huábīng nán shì nán, kěshì hěn hǎowán. | Skating is hard all right, but it's a lot of fun. |
| 这儿的夏天热是热，但是晚上很舒服。 | Zhèr de xiàtiān rè shì rè, dànshì wǎnshang hěn shūfu. | Summer here is hot, true, but the evenings are very pleasant. |
| 那件衣服漂亮是漂亮，可是太贵了。 | Nà jiàn yīfu piàoliang shì piàoliang, kěshì tài guì le. | That piece of clothing is pretty, sure, but it's too expensive. |
| 我喜欢是喜欢，可是没有钱买。 | Wǒ xǐhuan shì xǐhuan, kěshì méiyǒu qián mǎi. | I do like it, but I have no money to buy it. |

**Watch out:** do not put 很 in the first half: ✗ 很冷是很冷，可是… should be 冷是冷，可是….

## Dialogues

### Dialogue 1 · Too warm to skate
*Setting: At the end of winter, 马克 catches 林月 after class with a plan for the weekend.*

| Speaker | Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 马克 | 林月，这个周末我们去公园滑冰，好吗？ | Lín Yuè, zhège zhōumò wǒmen qù gōngyuán huábīng, hǎo ma? | Lin Yue, shall we go skating in the park this weekend? |
| 林月 | 滑冰？现在天气暖和了，公园里不能滑冰了。 | Huábīng? Xiànzài tiānqì nuǎnhuo le, gōngyuán li bù néng huábīng le. | Skating? The weather has warmed up, so you can't skate in the park any more. |
| 马克 | 真的吗？可是上个星期下雪了，天气很冷。 | Zhēn de ma? Kěshì shàng ge xīngqī xiàxuě le, tiānqì hěn lěng. | Really? But it snowed last week and it was very cold. |
| 林月 | 这个星期比上个星期暖和多了。你没看天气预报吗？ | Zhège xīngqī bǐ shàng ge xīngqī nuǎnhuo duō le. Nǐ méi kàn tiānqì yùbào ma? | This week is much warmer than last week. Didn't you look at the forecast? |
| 马克 | 没看。预报说什么？ | Méi kàn. Yùbào shuō shénme? | No. What does it say? |
| 林月 | 我刚才在网上看了，周末会比今天更暖和。 | Wǒ gāngcái zài wǎngshàng kàn le, zhōumò huì bǐ jīntiān gèng nuǎnhuo. | I checked online just now: the weekend will be even warmer than today. |
| 马克 | 那怎么办？我不但约了安娜，而且买了一双新的滑冰鞋！ | Nà zěnme bàn? Wǒ búdàn yuē le Ānnà, érqiě mǎi le yì shuāng xīn de huábīng xié! | Then what do I do? I've not only invited Anna, I've also bought a new pair of skates! |
| 林月 | 别紧张，我们可以去公园打球、聊天。 | Bié jǐnzhāng, wǒmen kěyǐ qù gōngyuán dǎqiú, liáotiān. | Relax, we can go to the park to play ball and chat. |
| 马克 | 好吧。那我的新鞋呢？ | Hǎo ba. Nà wǒ de xīn xié ne? | All right. And what about my new skates? |
| 林月 | 鞋好是好，可是只能冬天再穿了。 | Xié hǎo shì hǎo, kěshì zhǐ néng dōngtiān zài chuān le. | They're nice skates, sure, but now they'll have to wait until winter. |
| 马克 | 那我今天晚上在宿舍里穿。 | Nà wǒ jīntiān wǎnshang zài sùshè li chuān. | Then I'll wear them in the dorm tonight. |
| 林月 | 你真有意思！ | Nǐ zhēn yǒu yìsi! | You're so funny! |

### Dialogue 2 · Rain again
*Setting: On a wet spring morning, 林月 phones 马克, who has a job interview in the afternoon.*

| Speaker | Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 林月 | 喂，马克，你今天下午有面试，准备得怎么样？ | Wéi, Mǎkè, nǐ jīntiān xiàwǔ yǒu miànshì, zhǔnbèi de zěnmeyàng? | Hello, Mark. You have an interview this afternoon. How is the preparation going? |
| 马克 | 准备了，可是你看，又下雨了！ | Zhǔnbèi le, kěshì nǐ kàn, yòu xiàyǔ le! | I've prepared, but look, it's raining again! |
| 林月 | 是啊，昨天下，今天又下。这儿的春天常常下雨。 | Shì a, zuótiān xià, jīntiān yòu xià. Zhèr de chūntiān chángcháng xiàyǔ. | I know. It rained yesterday and it's raining again today. It often rains here in spring. |
| 马克 | 糟糕！我穿了新衬衫和新鞋，怎么办？ | Zāogāo! Wǒ chuān le xīn chènshān hé xīn xié, zěnme bàn? | What a mess! I've put on a new shirt and new shoes. What do I do? |
| 林月 | 别紧张，我开车送你去。 | Bié jǐnzhāng, wǒ kāichē sòng nǐ qù. | Don't worry, I'll drive you there. |
| 马克 | 太好了，谢谢你！加州的春天不常下雨，非常舒服。 | Tài hǎo le, xièxie nǐ! Jiāzhōu de chūntiān bù cháng xiàyǔ, fēicháng shūfu. | Great, thank you! In California it doesn't rain much in spring. It's really pleasant. |
| 林月 | 那夏天呢？加州的夏天热不热？ | Nà xiàtiān ne? Jiāzhōu de xiàtiān rè bu rè? | And summer? Is summer in California hot? |
| 马克 | 热是热，可是我觉得比这儿的夏天舒服一点儿。 | Rè shì rè, kěshì wǒ juéde bǐ zhèr de xiàtiān shūfu yìdiǎnr. | It's hot all right, but I find it a bit more comfortable than summer here. |
| 林月 | 你那么喜欢加州，以后会回去工作吗？ | Nǐ nàme xǐhuan Jiāzhōu, yǐhòu huì huíqu gōngzuò ma? | You like California so much. Will you go back there to work later on? |
| 马克 | 我还不知道。这儿冬天冷，夏天热，可是很好玩，朋友也多。 | Wǒ hái bù zhīdào. Zhèr dōngtiān lěng, xiàtiān rè, kěshì hěn hǎowán, péngyou yě duō. | I don't know yet. It's cold here in winter and hot in summer, but it's a lot of fun and I have plenty of friends. |
| 林月 | 对，秋天更好，不冷也不热。你会喜欢的。 | Duì, qiūtiān gèng hǎo, bù lěng yě bú rè. Nǐ huì xǐhuan de. | Right, and autumn is even better, neither cold nor hot. You'll like it. |
| 马克 | 好。你快点儿来吧，面试两点开始！ | Hǎo. Nǐ kuài diǎnr lái ba, miànshì liǎng diǎn kāishǐ! | OK. Now hurry over, the interview starts at two! |

## Exercises

### E1 · Choose the right word {type=choice}
1. 昨天很冷，今天比昨天___冷。 [更 | 很 | 非常] → 更
2. 昨天下雨，今天___下雨了。 [又 | 再 | 更] → 又
3. 天气预报说明天___下雪。 [会 | 了 | 比] → 会
4. 夏天热是热，___晚上很舒服。 [可是 | 所以 | 因为] → 可是
5. 这件衣服太贵了，便宜___吧。 [一点儿 | 有一点儿 | 非常] → 一点儿

### E2 · Put the words in order {type=order}
1. 暖和 / 比 / 加州的冬天 / 得多 / 纽约 → 加州的冬天比纽约暖和得多。
2. 了 / 又 / 下雨 / 今天 → 今天又下雨了。
3. 下雪 / 预报说 / 不会 / 明天 → 预报说明天不会下雪。
4. 可是 / 冷是冷 / 很好玩 / 冬天 → 冬天冷是冷，可是很好玩。
5. 不去 / 我 / 了 / 今天 / 公园 → 我今天不去公园了。

### E3 · Translate into Chinese {type=translate}
1. Look, it's snowing! → 你看，下雪了！
2. Winter here is much colder than in California. → 这儿的冬天比加州冷得多。
3. The forecast says it will rain tomorrow. → 天气预报说明天会下雨。
4. Skating is hard, but it's really fun. → 滑冰难是难，可是非常好玩。
5. Hurry up a bit, it's raining again. → 快一点儿，又下雨了。

### E4 · Match the word {type=match}
1. 暖和 → warm
2. 预报 → forecast
3. 滑冰 → to ice skate
4. 糟糕 → how terrible
5. 舒服 → comfortable
6. 面试 → interview
7. 秋天 → autumn
8. 刚才 → just now
