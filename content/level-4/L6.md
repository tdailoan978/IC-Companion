# Lesson 6 · 环境保护与节约能源 · Environmental Protection and Energy Conservation

## Objectives
- After this lesson you can talk about recycling, bottled water, disposable tableware and plastic bags, and say what each of us can do for the environment with 从…做起 and 随手.
- After this lesson you can paint a busy scene in which everyone is doing something different with the pattern V的V, V的V.
- After this lesson you can talk yourself round in circles between two options with …吧，…吧, and suggest relaxing or cheering someone up with ABAB adjectives such as 轻松轻松.
- After this lesson you can discuss smog, coal, oil, solar and wind power and government rules in a more formal register with 有益于, 高于 / 低于, 造成 and four-character phrases such as 环境保护.
- After this lesson you can agree warmly with 可不是嘛 and tell 想出来 (come up with) from 想起来 (remember).

## Grammar

### G1 · V1 的 V1，V2 的 V2: everyone busy with their own thing
**Pattern:** `V1 + 的 + V1，V2 + 的 + V2（，V3 + 的 + V3）`

This pattern describes a lively scene in which a group of people split up into different activities. Each verb, or verb-object phrase, is said twice with 的 in between: the first half (跑步的) works like a noun, "those who run", and the second half says what they are doing (跑步). Two or three such pairs follow each other, separated by commas, and the picture is of a place where everyone is busy and nobody is standing around. You know 有的…有的… for dividing a group; that is neutral, while this pattern stresses bustle and is typical of lively descriptions: a park at the weekend, a campus after class, a family after dinner. Keep it short: no 人 after 的 and no 在 before the second verb.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 下课以后，同学们打球的打球，看书的看书，聊天的聊天。 | Xià kè yǐhòu, tóngxuémen dǎ qiú de dǎ qiú, kàn shū de kàn shū, liáotiān de liáotiān. | After class, some of the students play ball, some read and some chat. |
| 周末公园里人很多，跑步的跑步，跳舞的跳舞，散步的散步。 | Zhōumò gōngyuán li rén hěn duō, pǎobù de pǎobù, tiàowǔ de tiàowǔ, sànbù de sànbù. | The park is crowded at the weekend: people running, people dancing, people out for a stroll. |
| 考试前一天，图书馆里复习的复习，写报告的写报告，没有人说话。 | Kǎoshì qián yì tiān, túshūguǎn li fùxí de fùxí, xiě bàogào de xiě bàogào, méiyǒu rén shuōhuà. | The day before the exam, the library is full of people reviewing or writing reports, and nobody says a word. |

**Watch out:** do not add 人: ✗ 跑步的人跑步，跳舞的人跳舞 should be 跑步的跑步，跳舞的跳舞.

### G2 · Adjective reduplication (II): ABAB
**Pattern:** `(想 / 让 + someone / 好好儿) + AB + AB`

In an earlier level you doubled two-syllable adjectives as AABB (高高兴兴, 干干净净) to make a description more vivid. Some two-syllable adjectives can also be doubled the way verbs are, as ABAB, and then the meaning changes: it is no longer a description but an action, "do something so that someone gets into this state for a while". 轻松轻松 means "relax a bit", 高兴高兴 "have some fun, cheer up", 暖和暖和 "warm up". The ABAB form is used in suggestions and wishes, so it often comes after 想 or 去, sits in a suggestion ending in 吧, or follows 让 / 叫 + a person. Only some adjectives work this way (轻松, 高兴, 暖和, 凉快, 热闹). The typical slip is to use AABB where an action is meant.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 考完试了，周末我们去公园轻松轻松吧。 | Kǎowán shì le, zhōumò wǒmen qù gōngyuán qīngsōng qīngsōng ba. | Exams are over. Let's go to the park this weekend and unwind a bit. |
| 外边太冷了，快进来暖和暖和。 | Wàibian tài lěng le, kuài jìnlai nuǎnhuo nuǎnhuo. | It's freezing out there. Come in quick and warm up. |
| 奶奶过生日，我们都回家，让她高兴高兴。 | Nǎinai guò shēngrì, wǒmen dōu huí jiā, ràng tā gāoxìng gāoxìng. | It's Grandma's birthday, so we're all going home to give her a happy day. |
| 天太热了，我们找个地方凉快凉快吧。 | Tiān tài rè le, wǒmen zhǎo ge dìfang liángkuai liángkuai ba. | It's too hot. Let's find somewhere to cool off. |

**Watch out:** for "relax a bit" use the ABAB form: ✗ 我们去公园轻轻松松吧 should be 我们去公园轻松轻松吧.

### G3 · Weighing up options with …吧，…吧
**Pattern:** `Option 1 + 吧，drawback；Option 2 + 吧，(又) drawback`

This spoken pattern lets you think aloud when you cannot make up your mind. You name the first option and put 吧 after it, as if to say "if I do this...", then give its drawback; then you do the same with the second option. The two options are often opposites (去吧 / 不去吧, 买吧 / 不买吧), or two competing choices (住宿舍吧 / 搬出去吧). 又 often appears in the second drawback, and the sentence usually ends with something like 我真不知道怎么办 or 真难决定. Here 吧 is not a suggestion: it marks a pause after a supposition. Learners tend to use 吗 instead, which turns each option into a question.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 买新电脑吧，太贵了；不买吧，旧的又太慢了。 | Mǎi xīn diànnǎo ba, tài guì le; bù mǎi ba, jiù de yòu tài màn le. | If I buy a new computer, it's too expensive; if I don't, the old one is far too slow. |
| 住宿舍吧，太吵；搬到校外吧，房租又太贵。 | Zhù sùshè ba, tài chǎo; bān dào xiàowài ba, fángzū yòu tài guì. | Living in the dorm is too noisy; moving off campus, the rent is too high. |
| 去吧，我明天有考试；不去吧，又怕朋友不高兴。我真不知道怎么办。 | Qù ba, wǒ míngtiān yǒu kǎoshì; bú qù ba, yòu pà péngyou bù gāoxìng. Wǒ zhēn bù zhīdào zěnme bàn. | If I go, there's my exam tomorrow; if I don't, I'm afraid my friend will be upset. I really don't know what to do. |

**Watch out:** each option takes 吧, not 吗: ✗ 坐火车吗，太慢；坐飞机吗，太贵 should be 坐火车吧，太慢；坐飞机吧，太贵.

### G4 · 有益于: good for
**Pattern:** `Subject + 有益于 + noun phrase`

于 is a formal preposition from written Chinese, and it usually follows a verb or adjective rather than standing in front of one. In 有益于 it means "to, for", so A 有益于 B says that A is good for B. It means the same as A 对 B 有好处, which you already know, but it sounds more formal and is typical of written texts, speeches and advice from doctors or teachers. The object is usually an abstract noun phrase: 身体健康, 环境保护, 学习, 孩子的成长. Because 于 already does the job of 对, the two cannot be combined: either 对 B 有益 or 有益于 B.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 常常爬山有益于身体健康。 | Chángcháng pá shān yǒuyì yú shēntǐ jiànkāng. | Hiking often is good for your health. |
| 看中文电影有益于提高中文水平。 | Kàn Zhōngwén diànyǐng yǒuyì yú tígāo Zhōngwén shuǐpíng. | Watching Chinese films helps you improve your Chinese. |
| 少开车、多骑车有益于环境保护。 | Shǎo kāichē, duō qí chē yǒuyì yú huánjìng bǎohù. | Driving less and cycling more is good for the environment. |

**Watch out:** do not use 对 and 于 together: ✗ 爬山对身体健康有益于 should be 爬山有益于身体健康 (or 爬山对身体健康有益).

### G5 · Adjective + 于: formal comparison
**Pattern:** `A + Adj + 于 + B`

After an adjective, 于 means "than" and makes a comparison, just like 比. A 高于 B says the same as A 比 B 高; likewise 低于, 大于, 小于, 多于, 少于. The word order is the reverse of the 比 sentence: the adjective comes first, then 于 and the thing compared. This is formal language, common in news, reports, rules and statistics, especially with numbers, temperatures and prices; in conversation people normally use 比. Adverbs such as 很 or 非常 cannot go before Adj + 于, and you cannot use 比 and 于 in the same sentence.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 今天的温度高于昨天。 | Jīntiān de wēndù gāo yú zuótiān. | Today's temperature is higher than yesterday's. |
| 北京一月的温度常常低于零度。 | Běijīng Yīyuè de wēndù chángcháng dī yú líng dù. | In January, the temperature in Beijing is often below zero. |
| 这个学期选中文课的学生多于上个学期。 | Zhège xuéqī xuǎn Zhōngwén kè de xuésheng duō yú shàng ge xuéqī. | More students are taking Chinese this semester than last. |

**Watch out:** use either 比 or 于, never both: ✗ 今天的温度比昨天高于 should be 今天的温度高于昨天.

## Words & Phrases

### W1 · 想出(来) vs. 想起(来) (to come up with vs. to recall)
Both are 想 plus a directional complement, but they look in opposite directions. 想出来 means to produce something new by thinking: an idea, a method, a name for a new shop, an answer to a problem. 想起来 means to bring back to mind something you once knew and had forgotten: a name, a place, where you left your keys. With an object, 来 is often dropped or the object goes in between: 想出了一个办法, 想出一个办法来; 想起了那件事. The negative potential forms are common: 想不出来 "can't think of (a solution)", 想不起来 "can't remember".

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 大家想了半天，安娜想出了一个好办法。 | Dàjiā xiǎngle bàntiān, Ānnà xiǎngchūle yí ge hǎo bànfǎ. | Everyone thought about it for ages, and Anna came up with a good idea. |
| 这个问题我想了一个晚上，还是没想出来。 | Zhège wèntí wǒ xiǎngle yí ge wǎnshang, háishi méi xiǎng chulai. | I spent a whole evening on this problem and still couldn't work it out. |
| 我见过那个人，可是想不起来他叫什么名字了。 | Wǒ jiànguo nàge rén, kěshì xiǎng bu qǐlai tā jiào shénme míngzi le. | I've met that man, but I can't remember his name. |
| 我想起来了，我的钥匙放在桌子上了。 | Wǒ xiǎng qilai le, wǒ de yàoshi fàng zài zhuōzi shang le. | Now I remember: I left my keys on the table. |

**Watch out:** a new idea is "thought out", not remembered: ✗ 我想起了一个新办法 should be 我想出了一个新办法.

### W2 · 四字词组 (four-character phrases)
Formal Chinese likes to pack an idea into four characters by putting a two-syllable noun before a two-syllable verb: 环境保护 (protecting the environment), 空气污染 (air pollution), 能源节约 (saving energy). The noun is really the object of the verb, so 环境保护 means the same as 保护环境, but the four-character phrase behaves like a noun. It can be a subject, an object, or stand directly before another noun as a modifier without 的: 环境保护问题, 空气污染情况. In conversation the verb-object order (保护环境, 节约能源) is more common; the four-character phrase sounds like a title, a report or the news.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 环境保护跟每个人都有关系。 | Huánjìng bǎohù gēn měi ge rén dōu yǒu guānxi. | Protecting the environment concerns every one of us. |
| 这几年，这个城市的空气污染越来越严重了。 | Zhè jǐ nián, zhège chéngshì de kōngqì wūrǎn yuè lái yuè yánzhòng le. | Air pollution in this city has been getting worse in recent years. |
| 老师让我们写一篇关于能源节约的报告。 | Lǎoshī ràng wǒmen xiě yì piān guānyú néngyuán jiéyuē de bàogào. | The teacher asked us to write a report on energy conservation. |

**Watch out:** in front of another noun use the noun-verb order: ✗ 保护环境问题 should be 环境保护问题.

### W3 · 可不是吗/可不是嘛 (Isn't that so? How true!)
可不是吗 looks like a negative question, but it is a reply that agrees strongly with what the other person has just said: "Exactly!", "You can say that again!". It is purely spoken and friendly, and it stands at the start of the reply, usually followed by a comment that adds a detail or a reason. The final 吗 or 嘛 can be left out, giving the short 可不是. It is not an answer to a real question asking for information; it reacts to an opinion or a complaint.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| “今天真热啊！”“可不是嘛，都三十五度了。” | "Jīntiān zhēn rè a!" "Kě bú shì ma, dōu sānshíwǔ dù le." | "It's so hot today!" "Tell me about it, it's thirty-five degrees." |
| “这个超市的东西越来越贵了。”“可不是吗！连塑料袋都要钱。” | "Zhège chāoshì de dōngxi yuè lái yuè guì le." "Kě bú shì ma! Lián sùliào dài dōu yào qián." | "This supermarket keeps getting pricier." "Doesn't it just! Even the plastic bags cost money." |
| “他做饭做得真好。”“可不是，比饭馆儿的还好吃。” | "Tā zuòfàn zuò de zhēn hǎo." "Kě bú shì, bǐ fànguǎnr de hái hǎochī." | "He's a really good cook." "He sure is, it's better than restaurant food." |

**Watch out:** 可不是嘛 means "yes, exactly", never "no": to disagree say 不见得, not 可不是嘛.

### W4 · 造成 (to cause, to give rise to)
造成 is a verb whose object is a result, usually a two-syllable noun: 污染, 问题, 影响, 麻烦. That result is almost always something bad, so 造成 belongs to talk about damage, accidents and pollution. The cause stands as the subject (汽车太多会造成污染), or the sentence is turned round with 是…造成的 to name the cause at the end. 给 + person often marks who suffers (给大家造成麻烦). For good results use 带来 or 让 instead.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 汽车太多会造成空气污染。 | Qìchē tài duō huì zào chéng kōngqì wūrǎn. | Too many cars cause air pollution. |
| 雾霾会给大家的健康造成很大的影响。 | Wùmái huì gěi dàjiā de jiànkāng zào chéng hěn dà de yǐngxiǎng. | Smog can do serious harm to everyone's health. |
| 这次的问题是他的马虎造成的。 | Zhè cì de wèntí shì tā de mǎhu zào chéng de. | This problem was caused by his carelessness. |

**Watch out:** 造成 is for bad results: ✗ 老师的帮助造成了我的进步 should be 老师的帮助让我进步了.

### W5 · 从…做起 (to start with)
从…做起 names the point where an effort should begin. 从 introduces that starting point (a person, a small habit, a particular area) and 做起 means "begin doing it". The pattern often follows 应该, 要 or 得, and is typical of slogans and advice: a big goal such as protecting the environment or learning a language is broken down into a first, concrete step. The starting point is usually something small or close at hand: 从我做起, 从小事做起, 从今天做起.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 学中文得从发音做起。 | Xué Zhōngwén děi cóng fāyīn zuòqǐ. | Learning Chinese has to start with pronunciation. |
| 保护环境不难，可以从随手把灯关上这样的小事做起。 | Bǎohù huánjìng bù nán, kěyǐ cóng suíshǒu bǎ dēng guānshang zhèyàng de xiǎoshì zuòqǐ. | Protecting the environment isn't hard: you can start with little things like switching off the light as you leave. |
| 要想身体好，得从每天早睡早起做起。 | Yào xiǎng shēntǐ hǎo, děi cóng měitiān zǎo shuì zǎo qǐ zuòqǐ. | If you want to be healthy, start by going to bed early and getting up early every day. |

**Watch out:** 做起 comes at the end: ✗ 从小事起做 should be 从小事做起.

### Dialogue 2 · Pedal power
