# Lesson 4 · 生活与健康 · Lifestyle and Health

## Objectives
- After this lesson you can talk about different kinds of exercise, from tai chi in the square to yoga and the gym, and list examples with 有的…，有的….
- After this lesson you can compare how people in China and the U.S. keep fit, and join or contrast two things in formal style with 与.
- After this lesson you can give advice on eating, sleeping and losing weight, with a minimal condition (只要…就…) or an extreme case (即使…也…).
- After this lesson you can point out habits that harm your health, such as staying up late or smoking, and say that one thing does not amount to another with 不等于.
- After this lesson you can recognise the one-syllable short forms (已, 应, 因, 虽, 但…) that appear in formal written Chinese.

## Grammar

### G1 · One-syllable forms of two-syllable words
**Pattern:** `已经 → 已 · 应该 → 应 · 可以 → 可 · 因为 → 因 · 比较 → 较 · 多半 → 多 · 虽然 → 虽 · 但是 → 但`

Many everyday function words have two syllables in speech but shrink to one syllable in formal writing: notices, newspaper articles, health advice, signs. The meaning does not change. 已 is 已经, 应 is 应该, 可 is 可以, 因 is 因为, 较 is 比较, 多 is 多半 ("mostly"), and the pair 虽然…但是… becomes 虽…但…. The short forms still sit where the long ones would, usually right before the verb or adjective. You need them mainly for reading: once you can expand them in your head, a formal sentence looks much less strange. In conversation, though, the full forms are the normal choice, and the short ones sound stiff or bookish. Learners who have just met them tend to sprinkle them into casual talk.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 李先生已退休多年，每天早晨都去公园锻炼。 | Lǐ xiānsheng yǐ tuìxiū duō nián, měitiān zǎochen dōu qù gōngyuán duànliàn. | Mr. Li retired many years ago; every morning he goes to the park to exercise. |
| 晚饭应少吃，睡觉以前不必再吃东西。 | Wǎnfàn yīng shǎo chī, shuìjiào yǐqián búbì zài chī dōngxi. | Eat a light dinner; there is no need to eat anything more before bed. |
| 因天气不好，博物馆今天下午不开门。 | Yīn tiānqì bù hǎo, bówùguǎn jīntiān xiàwǔ bù kāimén. | Owing to the bad weather, the museum is closed this afternoon. |
| 这家健身房虽小，但很干净。 | Zhè jiā jiànshēnfáng suī xiǎo, dàn hěn gānjìng. | Although this gym is small, it is very clean. |

**Watch out:** in everyday speech use the full forms: ✗ 我已吃饭了 should be 我已经吃饭了.

### G2 · The conjunction and preposition 与
**Pattern:** `A + 与 + B` (A and B) · `Subj + 与 + Person + V` (with) · `A + 与 + B + 不同 / 一样`

与 does the jobs of 和 and 跟, but in a formal register. As a conjunction it links two nouns, which is why it turns up so often in titles of books, articles and lessons such as 生活与健康. As a preposition it introduces the person you do something with, or the thing you compare with, before the verb or before 一样 / 不同. Like 和, it joins nouns and noun phrases; an activity can also be joined when it is used as a subject or topic (熬夜与吸烟), but 与 never links two predicates or whole clauses. In casual conversation 和 and 跟 sound more natural, so keep 与 for writing, presentations and set phrases. The common slip is using 与 to chain actions, the way English uses "and".

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我在图书馆借了一本书，叫《运动与健康》。 | Wǒ zài túshūguǎn jièle yì běn shū, jiào "Yùndòng yǔ Jiànkāng". | I borrowed a book from the library called "Exercise and Health". |
| 中国人与美国人锻炼身体的习惯不太一样。 | Zhōngguórén yǔ Měiguórén duànliàn shēntǐ de xíguàn bú tài yíyàng. | Chinese and Americans have rather different exercise habits. |
| 退休以后，他常常与老朋友一起去公园散步。 | Tuìxiū yǐhòu, tā chángcháng yǔ lǎo péngyou yìqǐ qù gōngyuán sàn bù. | Since retiring, he often goes for walks in the park with old friends. |
| 熬夜与吸烟对身体都不好。 | Áo yè yǔ xī yān duì shēntǐ dōu bù hǎo. | Staying up late and smoking are both bad for your health. |

**Watch out:** 与 does not join two actions: ✗ 我去了健身房与做了瑜伽 should be 我去了健身房，还做了瑜伽.

### G3 · 只要…就… (as long as)
**Pattern:** `只要 + condition，(Subj +) 就 + result`

只要 sets up a condition that is enough on its own: meet it, and the result follows. The speaker is saying that nothing else is needed, so the pattern often sounds encouraging ("it's easy, all you have to do is…"). 就 introduces the result and must go after the subject of the second clause and before its predicate, including any 会, 能 or 对… phrase. 只要 can stand before or after the subject of the first clause. Do not mix it up with 只有…才…, which names the one condition without which the result will not happen ("only if"): 只要 lowers the bar, 只有 raises it. The usual mistakes are dropping 就 or pairing 只要 with 才.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 只要每天走半个小时路，就对身体有好处。 | Zhǐyào měitiān zǒu bàn ge xiǎoshí lù, jiù duì shēntǐ yǒu hǎochu. | As long as you walk for half an hour every day, it does your body good. |
| 只要不下雨，奶奶就去广场打太极拳。 | Zhǐyào bú xiàyǔ, nǎinai jiù qù guǎngchǎng dǎ tàijíquán. | As long as it isn't raining, Grandma goes to the square to do tai chi. |
| 太极拳不难学，只要动作慢一点儿就行。 | Tàijíquán bù nán xué, zhǐyào dòngzuò màn yìdiǎnr jiù xíng. | Tai chi isn't hard to learn; you just have to keep your movements slow. |
| 你只要早点儿睡，第二天上课就不会那么累。 | Nǐ zhǐyào zǎo diǎnr shuì, dì èr tiān shàngkè jiù bú huì nàme lèi. | As long as you go to bed a bit earlier, you won't be so tired in class the next day. |

**Watch out:** 只要 goes with 就, not 才: ✗ 只要你多锻炼，身体才会好 should be 只要你多锻炼，身体就会好.

### G4 · 即使…也… (even if)
**Pattern:** `即使 + (extreme or imagined) situation，(Subj +) 也 + unchanged result` · `即使是 + Noun，…也…`

即使 imagines a situation, often an extreme or unfavourable one, and 也 says that the result stays the same even then: "even if X, still Y". The first clause is usually hypothetical, which is the key difference from 虽然…但是…, which concedes a fact that is already true. 也 must come in the second clause, after its subject and after time words, before the predicate (and before any 会, 应该 or 不必). When the extreme case is a noun (a weekend, a holiday, the best student), add 是 after 即使. Learners often use 还 or 但是 instead of 也, or use 即使 for a plain fact where 虽然 belongs.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 即使下大雪，爷爷每天早晨也去广场打太极拳。 | Jíshǐ xià dà xuě, yéye měitiān zǎochen yě qù guǎngchǎng dǎ tàijíquán. | Even if it snows heavily, Grandpa goes to the square every morning to do tai chi. |
| 即使不饿，早饭也应该吃一点儿。 | Jíshǐ bú è, zǎofàn yě yīnggāi chī yìdiǎnr. | Even if you aren't hungry, you should eat a little breakfast. |
| 即使明天有考试，你也不必熬夜。 | Jíshǐ míngtiān yǒu kǎoshì, nǐ yě búbì áo yè. | Even if you have an exam tomorrow, there's no need to stay up late. |
| 即使是周末，他也七点起床。 | Jíshǐ shì zhōumò, tā yě qī diǎn qǐchuáng. | Even on weekends he gets up at seven. |

**Watch out:** the second clause needs 也: ✗ 即使下雨，我还去散步 should be 即使下雨，我也去散步.

## Words & Phrases

### W1 · 有的…，有的… (some… others…)
有的 means "some (of them)". Used twice or more in parallel clauses, it splits a group into parts and says what each part does: some dance, some do tai chi. The group is normally named first as the topic (广场上的人, 我的同学), and each 有的 is followed by a verb phrase, or by a noun and then a verb phrase (有的人…，有的人…). The list can run to three or more clauses, often ending with 还有的. Because 有的 phrases work as subjects, they come before the verb, not after it as "some" can in English.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 广场上的老人，有的跳舞，有的打太极拳，还有的在聊天。 | Guǎngchǎng shang de lǎorén, yǒude tiàowǔ, yǒude dǎ tàijíquán, hái yǒude zài liáotiān. | Of the old people in the square, some dance, some do tai chi, and others are chatting. |
| 我们宿舍的人有的早睡早起，有的天天熬夜。 | Wǒmen sùshè de rén yǒude zǎo shuì zǎo qǐ, yǒude tiāntiān áo yè. | Of the people in our dorm, some go to bed early and get up early, and others stay up late every night. |
| 这些菜有的太油，有的太甜，都不太健康。 | Zhèxiē cài yǒude tài yóu, yǒude tài tián, dōu bú tài jiànkāng. | Some of these dishes are too oily and some are too sweet; none of them is very healthy. |

**Watch out:** 有的 + noun goes before the verb: ✗ 我喜欢有的运动 should be 有的运动我喜欢，有的我不喜欢.

### W2 · 重视 (to attach importance to, to think highly of)
重视 is a verb that says someone takes a person or a matter seriously and gives it weight: health, education, a child's opinion. It takes an object directly and is often strengthened with 很, 特别 or 最. It can also describe a noun through a 的 phrase (他最重视的事). It expresses a considered judgment over time, not momentary care. That is the difference from 注意, which means paying attention to something in a particular situation (注意安全, 注意营养). The opposite is 不重视, as in a school that does not take sports seriously.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我们学校很重视学生的健康。 | Wǒmen xuéxiào hěn zhòngshì xuésheng de jiànkāng. | Our school attaches great importance to students' health. |
| 健康是我父母最重视的事。 | Jiànkāng shì wǒ fùmǔ zuì zhòngshì de shì. | Health is what my parents care about most. |
| 他以前不重视锻炼，现在每个星期去三次健身房。 | Tā yǐqián bú zhòngshì duànliàn, xiànzài měi ge xīngqī qù sān cì jiànshēnfáng. | He used not to take exercise seriously; now he goes to the gym three times a week. |

**Watch out:** for watching out in a situation use 注意: ✗ 过马路要重视车 should be 过马路要注意车.

### W3 · 等于 (to equal, to be equivalent to, to amount to)
等于 is a verb. In arithmetic it is the "equals" of a sum: 三加五等于八. More often in conversation it says that two things come to the same in effect: skipping a class you paid for amounts to throwing money away. The negative 不等于 is very common for correcting an assumption: doing one thing does not automatically mean the other. It needs no 是 in front of it, and it is negated with 不, not 没.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 三加五等于八。 | Sān jiā wǔ děngyú bā. | Three plus five equals eight. |
| 一个月不吸烟，就等于省了三百块钱。 | Yí ge yuè bù xī yān, jiù děngyú shěngle sānbǎi kuài qián. | Not smoking for a month is the same as saving three hundred dollars. |
| 少吃饭不等于减肥。 | Shǎo chī fàn bù děngyú jiǎn féi. | Eating less is not the same as losing weight. |

**Watch out:** no 是 before 等于: ✗ 去健身房是等于锻炼 should be 去健身房就等于锻炼 (negative: 去健身房不等于锻炼).

### W4 · 随便 (casual, careless, as one pleases)
随便 means "as it suits you". Before a verb it says that someone may do something however they like (你随便看), or that something is done casually, without a plan (随便问问). As a predicate it can be a relaxed reply, "whatever you like, I don't mind", or a criticism: 他太随便了 means he is too careless or sloppy. Tone and context decide whether it is friendly or critical. As a reply to a host's question it can sound offhand, so add something warmer such as 什么都行 or 你决定吧.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这是我的书，你随便看。 | Zhè shì wǒ de shū, nǐ suíbiàn kàn. | These are my books; feel free to look at any of them. |
| 周末想去哪儿玩？——随便，你决定吧。 | Zhōumò xiǎng qù nǎr wán? — Suíbiàn, nǐ juédìng ba. | Where do you want to go this weekend? — I don't mind, you decide. |
| 他吃饭太随便了，常常一边走路一边吃。 | Tā chīfàn tài suíbiàn le, chángcháng yìbiān zǒulù yìbiān chī. | He's too careless about meals; he often eats while walking. |

**Watch out:** as an adverb 随便 goes before the verb: ✗ 你坐随便 should be 你随便坐.

### Dialogue 2 · Panda eyes
*Setting: In the week before midterms, 林月 and 安娜 find 马克 in the library café at noon with nothing but a large coffee in front of him.*

## Exercises

### E1 · Choose the right word {type=choice}
1. 只要你每天锻炼半个小时，身体___会越来越好。 [才 | 就 | 也] → 就
2. ___明天下雨，爷爷也会出门散步。 [只要 | 因为 | 即使] → 即使
3. 来广场锻炼的老人，___跳舞，有的打太极拳。 [有的 | 有时候 | 有点儿] → 有的
4. 少吃晚饭不___不吃晚饭。 [注意 | 等于 | 重视] → 等于
5. 我们学校很___学生的健康，每年都请医生来给学生检查身体。 [随便 | 等于 | 重视] → 重视
6. 他吃饭太___了，饿了就在路上买一块蛋糕。 [随便 | 科学 | 营养] → 随便
7. 即使工作很忙，你每天晚上___应该睡七个小时。 [就 | 也 | 才] → 也
8. 你明天___来得那么早，九点到就行。 [必须 | 只要 | 不必] → 不必

### E2 · Put the words in order {type=order}
1. 去广场 / 爷爷就 / 锻炼 / 只要不下雨 → 只要不下雨，爷爷就去广场锻炼。
2. 即使很累 / 半个小时瑜伽 / 她也 / 要做 → 即使很累，她也要做半个小时瑜伽。
3. 我奶奶 / 重视 / 特别 / 吃早饭 → 我奶奶特别重视吃早饭。
4. 已经 / 熬夜 / 他的习惯了 / 成为 → 熬夜已经成为他的习惯了。
5. 吧 / 你 / 坐 / 随便 → 你随便坐吧。
6. 补充 / 你 / 应该 / 睡眠 / 多 → 你应该多补充睡眠。
7. 陈乐家 / 小狗 / 有 / 一只 → 陈乐家有一只小狗。

### E3 · Translate into Chinese {type=translate}
1. As long as you don't stay up late, you won't be so tired. → 只要你不熬夜，就不会那么累。
2. Even if it's very cold, Grandma still goes for a walk in the park every morning. → 即使天气很冷，奶奶每天早晨也去公园散步。
3. My grandparents retired three years ago. → 我爷爷奶奶三年前退休了。
4. Some of my classmates like going to the gym, and others like doing yoga. → 我的同学有的喜欢去健身房，有的喜欢做瑜伽。
5. Exercising is not the same as losing weight. → 锻炼不等于减肥。
6. If you want to lose weight, you must pay attention to nutrition. → 要是你想减肥，就必须注意营养。
7. What would you like to drink? — Anything is fine. → 你想喝什么？——随便，什么都行。
