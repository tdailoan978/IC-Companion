# Lesson 3 · 去云南旅游 · A Trip to Yunnan

## Objectives
- After this lesson you can sign up for a group tour and ask what its price includes, using 报名, 参加 and 包括.
- After this lesson you can explain how hard and soft sleepers on a Chinese train differ.
- After this lesson you can describe Yunnan's scenery and customs, and recognise a few idioms built on two numbers, such as 一清二楚 and 乱七八糟.
- After this lesson you can put several descriptions in front of a noun in the right order (一个很幽默的导游, 两个漂亮的小灯笼).
- After this lesson you can talk about the high and low points of a trip with 印象, 分享, 只好, 千万 and 不过.

## Grammar

### G1 · Numbers in idioms
**Pattern:** `千…万… · 千…百… · 一…二… · 五…六… · 七…八…`

Chinese has a large stock of four-character idioms (成语 chéngyǔ) with two numbers in them, and the numbers are almost never meant literally. 千 and 万, or 千 and 百, stand for "a great many": 千奇百怪 describes all sorts of strange shapes, 千山万水 a long, hard journey. 一 with 二 suggests something simple and complete, so 一清二楚 means "perfectly clear". 五 with 六 means many and varied, as in 五颜六色 (of every colour). 七 with 八 suggests disorder: 乱七八糟 is "a total mess", 七上八下 "on edge, anxious". The two numbers can be consecutive (一…二…, 五…六…, 七…八…) or not (千…万…), and they can even run downwards (千…百…); usually they stand in the first and third places (五颜六色), sometimes in the middle (乱七八糟). Learn each idiom as a single block, together with how it is used: many work as predicates, often with 的 (房间乱七八糟的), or after 得 (记得一清二楚), and many also go before a noun with 的 (五颜六色的灯笼). Learners tend to swap the numbers or try to work out what they "count".

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 那天在火车上发生的事，他记得一清二楚。 | Nà tiān zài huǒchē shang fāshēng de shì, tā jì de yìqīng-èrchǔ. | He remembers exactly what happened on the train that day. |
| 过年的时候，街上挂着五颜六色的灯笼。 | Guònián de shíhou, jiē shang guàzhe wǔyán-liùsè de dēnglong. | At New Year the streets are hung with lanterns of every colour. |
| 马克的箱子里总是乱七八糟的。 | Mǎkè de xiāngzi li zǒngshì luànqībāzāo de. | Mark's suitcase is always a complete mess. |
| 明天要见导游了，安娜心里七上八下的，怕自己听不懂。 | Míngtiān yào jiàn dǎoyóu le, Ānnà xīnli qīshàng-bāxià de, pà zìjǐ tīng bu dǒng. | Anna is on edge about meeting the guide tomorrow, afraid she won't understand. |

**Watch out:** the numbers and their order are fixed: ✗ 乱八七糟 should be 乱七八糟, and 七上八下 has nothing to do with seven or eight of anything.

### G2 · Nouns with several modifiers
**Pattern:** `(this/that) + Num + MW + descriptive phrase + 的 + (one-syllable adj) + Noun`

When a noun has several modifiers, Chinese puts them in a fixed order, all before the noun. In an ordinary statement the number and measure word come first (一个, 两条, 三座). After them come the longer descriptions with 的: an adjective phrase such as 很幽默的, or a whole clause such as 只有八个人的. A short one-syllable adjective like 小, 老 or 红 sits right next to the noun with no 的 (小灯笼, 老房子). So the path runs from the outside in: 一个 + 很有意思的 + 老 + 故事. To stress the description, a speaker can sometimes put it in front of the number (最干净的一条河), mostly with superlatives or "the one I…" clauses. The usual learner error is to let the number slip behind an adjective in a plain sentence.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我们参加了一个只有八个人的旅游团。 | Wǒmen cānjiāle yí ge zhǐ yǒu bā ge rén de lǚyóutuán. | We joined a tour group of only eight people. |
| 大理有三座很古老的塔。 | Dàlǐ yǒu sān zuò hěn gǔlǎo de tǎ. | Dali has three very old pagodas. |
| 房东送给我们一个很漂亮的小灯笼。 | Fángdōng sòng gěi wǒmen yí ge hěn piàoliang de xiǎo dēnglong. | The landlord gave us a very pretty little lantern. |
| 这是我见过的最干净的一条河。 | Zhè shì wǒ jiànguo de zuì gānjìng de yì tiáo hé. | This is the cleanest river I have ever seen. |

**Watch out:** in a plain statement the number goes first: ✗ 他买了便宜的三个纪念品 should be 他买了三个便宜的纪念品.

## Words & Phrases

### W1 · 印象 (impression)
印象 is a noun, and it lives in three frames. X 对 Y 的印象 + adjective tells what X thinks of Y: 我对昆明的印象很好. Y 给 X 留下了 + adjective + 的印象 says that Y left X with an impression; 很深的印象 (a deep impression) is the classic pairing. And 有印象 / 没有印象 means "to remember / not remember at all", often with 一点儿…都/也. The usual adjectives are 好, 不错, 不好, 很深 and 不深; "big" or "strong" impressions are not said with 大 or 强. Note that the person who has the impression comes after 给 but before 对.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我对昆明的印象很好，天气舒服，人也很热情。 | Wǒ duì Kūnmíng de yìnxiàng hěn hǎo, tiānqì shūfu, rén yě hěn rèqíng. | I have a very good impression of Kunming: the weather is pleasant and the people are friendly. |
| 那位幽默的导游给我留下了很深的印象。 | Nà wèi yōumò de dǎoyóu gěi wǒ liúxiale hěn shēn de yìnxiàng. | That humorous guide left a deep impression on me. |
| 那家饭馆的名字，我一点儿印象也没有了。 | Nà jiā fànguǎn de míngzi, wǒ yìdiǎnr yìnxiàng yě méiyǒu le. | I have no memory at all of that restaurant's name. |

**Watch out:** the impression is what is left, not the subject: ✗ 他给我的印象留下很深 should be 他给我留下了很深的印象.

### W2 · 分享 (to share something good)
分享 is a verb for sharing something pleasant: happiness, good news, an experience, photos, food you enjoy. The person you share with comes before the verb with 跟 or 和: 跟朋友分享. The object can be abstract (快乐, 好消息) or, in everyday modern usage, things like photos and stories, especially on WeChat. It is not used for splitting costs or using something together, such as a room or a bill; for those, say 一起住 or 一起付钱. It sounds warm and slightly formal, which is why it is common on social media.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我想跟大家分享一个好消息。 | Wǒ xiǎng gēn dàjiā fēnxiǎng yí ge hǎo xiāoxi. | I'd like to share some good news with everyone. |
| 有了好吃的东西，他总是跟朋友一起分享。 | Yǒule hǎochī de dōngxi, tā zǒngshì gēn péngyou yìqǐ fēnxiǎng. | Whenever he has something tasty, he always shares it with his friends. |
| 回来以后，我们在微信上分享了这次旅行的照片。 | Huílai yǐhòu, wǒmen zài Wēixìn shang fēnxiǎngle zhè cì lǚxíng de zhàopiàn. | After we got back, we shared the photos from this trip on WeChat. |

**Watch out:** 分享 is not for sharing a room: ✗ 我跟他分享一个房间 should be 我跟他住一个房间.

### W3 · 只好 (have no choice but)
只好 is an adverb. It goes after the subject and right before the verb, and says that circumstances leave only one option, usually not the one you wanted. The reason normally comes first, in its own clause (票卖完了, 下大雨了), and 只好 introduces what you were forced to do. It often carries a touch of disappointment or resignation. Do not confuse it with 最好 ("had better"), which gives advice about the best choice, while 只好 reports the only one left.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 外边下大雨，我们只好在茶馆里坐着聊天。 | Wàibian xià dà yǔ, wǒmen zhǐhǎo zài cháguǎn li zuòzhe liáotiān. | It was pouring outside, so we had to sit and chat in the teahouse. |
| 我忘了带护照，只好回去拿。 | Wǒ wàngle dài hùzhào, zhǐhǎo huíqu ná. | I forgot my passport, so I had to go back for it. |
| 饭馆都关门了，马克只好吃了一碗面。 | Fànguǎn dōu guānmén le, Mǎkè zhǐhǎo chīle yì wǎn miàn. | All the restaurants had closed, so Mark had to make do with a bowl of noodles. |

**Watch out:** 只好 follows the subject: ✗ 只好我们坐火车 should be 我们只好坐火车.

### W4 · 千万 (by all means, absolutely must)
As an adverb, 千万 makes a request or warning very strong: "whatever you do". It goes before the verb, after the subject if there is one. Most often it is negative, 千万别 or 千万不要 + verb, to warn someone off something. It also works with positive reminders: 千万要…, 千万记得…. It only appears in advice, requests and warnings addressed to someone, never in statements of fact, so it cannot mean "very much". (As a number, 千万 means "ten million".)

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这家饭馆的菜很辣，你千万别点太多。 | Zhè jiā fànguǎn de cài hěn là, nǐ qiānwàn bié diǎn tài duō. | The food here is very spicy, so whatever you do, don't order too much. |
| 到了昆明，千万记得给我打个电话。 | Dàole Kūnmíng, qiānwàn jìde gěi wǒ dǎ ge diànhuà. | When you get to Kunming, be sure to give me a call. |
| 在火车上睡觉的时候，千万要看好自己的东西。 | Zài huǒchē shang shuìjiào de shíhou, qiānwàn yào kānhǎo zìjǐ de dōngxi. | When you sleep on the train, make absolutely sure you keep an eye on your things. |

**Watch out:** 千万 is not "very": ✗ 他千万喜欢云南 should be 他非常喜欢云南.

### W5 · 不过 (however; only, no more than)
You know 不过 as a conjunction: at the head of a clause it means "but" and turns the thought, a little more softly than 可是. In this lesson it is also an adverb meaning "only, no more than". In that use it stands right before a number, an amount or a verb phrase and plays it down: 不过五十块 (just fifty yuan). It is often strengthened as 只不过, and 不过是 + phrase means "it's merely…". So look at the position: at the start of a clause, 不过 means "but"; inside a clause before an amount, it means "only".

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这个团包括交通和门票，一个人不过两百块钱。 | Zhège tuán bāokuò jiāotōng hé ménpiào, yí ge rén búguò liǎngbǎi kuài qián. | This tour includes transport and admission, and it's only two hundred yuan a person. |
| 他们在大理只不过住了一天，可是对那儿的印象很深。 | Tāmen zài Dàlǐ zhǐ búguò zhùle yì tiān, kěshì duì nàr de yìnxiàng hěn shēn. | They only stayed in Dali for a day, but it left a deep impression on them. |
| 别生气，他不过是开个玩笑。 | Bié shēngqì, tā búguò shì kāi ge wánxiào. | Don't be angry, he was only joking. |
| 坐火车很便宜，不过时间比较长。 | Zuò huǒchē hěn piányi, búguò shíjiān bǐjiào cháng. | The train is cheap, but it takes quite a long time. |

**Watch out:** in the "only" sense 不过 goes before the amount, not at the end: ✗ 门票五十块钱不过 should be 门票不过五十块钱.

### Dialogue 2 · A call from Lijiang
*Setting: Two days later, after an overnight train from Kunming, the three friends are strolling by the river in Lijiang's old town in the evening when 陈乐 calls from Beijing.*

| Speaker | Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 陈乐 | 喂，你们到丽江了吧？坐了一晚上的火车，累不累？ | Wèi, nǐmen dào Lìjiāng le ba? Zuòle yì wǎnshang de huǒchē, lèi bu lèi? | Hello, you've made it to Lijiang, right? A whole night on the train: are you tired? |
| 林月 | 还好。我们想买软卧，可是软卧的票早就卖完了，只好买了硬卧。 | Hái hǎo. Wǒmen xiǎng mǎi ruǎnwò, kěshì ruǎnwò de piào zǎo jiù màiwán le, zhǐhǎo mǎile yìngwò. | Not too bad. We wanted soft sleepers, but they had sold out long ago, so we had to get hard sleepers. (软卧 ruǎnwò: soft sleeper; 硬卧 yìngwò: hard sleeper) |
| 陈乐 | 硬卧也不错，便宜多了。安娜，这是你第一次坐中国的火车吧？你觉得硬卧跟软卧有什么不一样？ | Yìngwò yě búcuò, piányi duō le. Ānnà, zhè shì nǐ dì yī cì zuò Zhōngguó de huǒchē ba? | 安娜 | 美极了！这儿山美，水也美。古城后边有一座很高的雪山，天气好的时候，在街上就能看见。我拍了很多照片，已经跟大卫分享了。 | Měi jí le! Zhèr shān měi, shuǐ yě měi. ## Exercises

### E1 · Choose the right word {type=choice}
1. 这个旅游团的价钱___交通和门票。 [参加 | 包括 | 分享] → 包括
2. 旅馆没有房间了，我们___去别的地方找。 [只好 | 千万 | 不过] → 只好
3. 晚上很冷，你___别穿得太少。 [只好 | 不过 | 千万] → 千万
4. 这家茶馆的茶很便宜，一杯___十块钱。 [千万 | 不过 | 只好] → 不过
5. 那个幽默的导游给我们___了很深的印象。 [参加 | 包括 | 留下] → 留下
6. 安娜想跟大卫___她在云南拍的照片。 [报名 | 分享 | 包括] → 分享
7. 我对丽江的印象很___，到现在还常常想起来。 [软 | 硬 | 深] → 深
8. 林月在丽江买了___。 [两个漂亮的灯笼 | 漂亮的两个灯笼 | 两个灯笼漂亮的] → 两个漂亮的灯笼

### E2 · Put the words in order {type=order}
1. 一个 / 我们的导游 / 很幽默的人 / 是 → 我们的导游是一个很幽默的人。
2. 讲了 / 他给我们 / 故事 / 一个很有意思的 → 他给我们讲了一个很有意思的故事。
3. 忘了 / 千万别 / 带门票 / 你 → 你千万别忘了带门票。
4. 留下了 / 很深的印象 / 给我 / 石林 → 石林给我留下了很深的印象。
5. 不 / 这个团的 / 包括门票和午饭 / 价钱 → 这个团的价钱不包括门票和午饭。
6. 跟朋友 / 他 / 分享好吃的东西 / 喜欢 → 他喜欢跟朋友分享好吃的东西。
7. 关上 / 请你 / 车厢的门 / 把 → 请你把车厢的门关上。

### E3 · Translate into Chinese {type=translate}
1. We signed up for a tour group; the price includes transportation and admission tickets. → 我们报名参加了一个旅游团，价钱包括交通和门票。
2. Whatever you do, don't forget your passport! → 你千万别忘了带护照！
3. What's your impression of Kunming? → 你对昆明的印象怎么样？
4. The guide was very humorous and told us lots of stories. → 导游很幽默，给我们讲了很多故事。
5. The tickets were only fifty yuan each. → 门票一张不过五十块钱。
6. I bought my mom two very pretty little souvenirs. → 我给妈妈买了两个很漂亮的小纪念品。
7. On the train I had a box lunch and a bowl of noodles. → 在火车上，我吃了一个盒饭和一碗面。

### E4 · Match the word {type=match}
1. 报名 → to sign up, to register
2. 包括 → to include
3. 门票 → admission ticket
4. 卧铺 → sleeping bunk on a train
5. 车厢 → train carriage
6. 盒饭 → box lunch
7. 风俗 → custom
8. 纪念品 → souvenir
