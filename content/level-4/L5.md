# Lesson 5 · 男女平等 · Gender Equality

## Objectives
- After this lesson you can talk about fairness and equal treatment between men and women, at work, in sport and between partners.
- After this lesson you can sum up how the status of women in China has changed since 1950 and since Reform and Opening-Up, using 以来 and 逐渐.
- After this lesson you can describe how couples share the household chores and back up your view with 拿…来说.
- After this lesson you can sound firm and reassuring with 是…的 around an adjective or a modal verb, and say who is responsible for something with 由.
- After this lesson you can state the one condition under which something happens with 只有…才…, and talk about coming round or finally understanding with 过来.

## Grammar

### G1 · Sentences with 是…的 (II): sounding firm and reassuring
**Pattern:** `Subj + 是 + (很) Adj / modal verb + V / V + 得 + complement / mental verb + 的`

You know 是…的 as the frame that picks out when, where or how a past action happened. This second use has nothing to do with the past. Here 是…的 wraps a whole statement to make it sound firmer: the speaker wants to reassure, persuade or convince (it really is so, believe me). 我是很喜欢这个城市的 is warmer and more insistent than plain 我很喜欢这个城市. What goes inside the frame is usually an adjective phrase, often with 很; a verb works when it follows a modal verb (会, 能, 可以), carries a potential complement (吃得完) or is a mental verb that 很 can modify (喜欢, 相信). A bare action verb cannot stand inside the frame with this meaning, and that is the typical slip.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 在大城市找工作是很不容易的。 | Zài dà chéngshì zhǎo gōngzuò shì hěn bù róngyì de. | Finding a job in a big city really isn't easy. |
| 你别担心，他的病是会好的。 | Nǐ bié dānxīn, tā de bìng shì huì hǎo de. | Don't worry, he will get better, I'm sure. |
| 这些菜我们三个人是吃得完的。 | Zhèxiē cài wǒmen sān ge rén shì chī de wán de. | The three of us can definitely finish these dishes. |
| 我是很喜欢这个城市的。 | Wǒ shì hěn xǐhuan zhège chéngshì de. | I really do like this city. |

**Watch out:** a bare action verb does not give the "I assure you" meaning: ✗ 我是做家务的 should be 我是会做家务的.

### G2 · 过来 as a resultative complement
**Pattern:** `V + 过来` (back to a normal, conscious state) · opposite: `V + 过去` (losing consciousness)

After a verb, 过来 does not always mean "come over here". It can also mark a result: someone gets back to a normal, active or clear-headed state. A person who wakes up after a long sleep or comes round after fainting 醒过来; someone who finally sees what is going on 明白过来. The opposite direction is 过去: falling deeply asleep (睡过去) or passing out goes "away" from the normal state. Treat these as fixed result meanings, not as movement toward the speaker. 了 often follows, and 才 is common before the verb when the recovery took a long time. Learners tend to mix up the two directions.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 病人睡了一天一夜，今天早上才醒过来。 | Bìngrén shuìle yì tiān yí yè, jīntiān zǎoshang cái xǐng guolai. | The patient slept for a whole day and night and only came round this morning. |
| 他想了很久，才明白过来，原来大家是在跟他开玩笑。 | Tā xiǎngle hěn jiǔ, cái míngbai guolai, yuánlái dàjiā shì zài gēn tā kāi wánxiào. | He thought about it for a long time before it dawned on him that everyone was teasing him. |
| 他太累了，一躺下就睡过去了。 | Tā tài lèi le, yì tǎngxia jiù shuì guoqu le. | He was so tired that he was out cold the moment he lay down. |

**Watch out:** coming back to the normal state is 过来, leaving it is 过去: ✗ 他终于醒过去了 should be 他终于醒过来了.

## Words & Phrases

### W1 · 逐渐 (gradually, little by little)
逐渐 is an adverb. It goes after the subject and directly before the verb or adjective, and it describes a change that develops step by step over a period of time: weather warming up, a person getting used to a new place, a situation slowly improving. It describes how things develop by themselves, so it is common in reports and summaries, and it often goes with 了 to show that the change is under way. In everyday speech 慢慢 is close, but 慢慢 can also describe the speed of one action or give advice (慢慢吃), which 逐渐 cannot.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 春天到了，天气逐渐暖和了。 | Chūntiān dào le, tiānqì zhújiàn nuǎnhuo le. | Spring is here, and the weather is gradually getting warmer. |
| 来美国半年以后，他逐渐习惯了这儿的生活。 | Lái Měiguó bàn nián yǐhòu, tā zhújiàn xíguànle zhèr de shēnghuó. | After half a year in America, he gradually got used to life here. |
| 这几十年，女孩子上大学的机会逐渐多了。 | Zhè jǐ shí nián, nǚháizi shàng dàxué de jīhuì zhújiàn duō le. | Over the last few decades, girls have gradually had more chances to go to university. |

**Watch out:** 逐渐 describes a process, not how to do something: ✗ 你逐渐吃 should be 你慢慢吃.

### W2 · 提高 (to improve, to raise; improvement)
提高 is a verb meaning to raise the level of something: grades, standards, ability, pay, status. It takes an object directly (提高成绩, 提高妇女的地位) or stands as the predicate with the thing as subject (水平提高了). With 得 it describes how fast or how much (提高得很快). It also works as a noun, "improvement", usually after 有 with a modifier: 有了很大的提高. It goes with levels and standards, not with physical height or size.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 他每天打两个小时乒乓球，水平提高得很快。 | Tā měitiān dǎ liǎng ge xiǎoshí pīngpāngqiú, shuǐpíng tígāo de hěn kuài. | He plays table tennis for two hours every day, and his game is improving fast. |
| 怎么样才能提高中文成绩？ | Zěnmeyàng cái néng tígāo Zhōngwén chéngjì? | How can I raise my grades in Chinese? |
| 这一年，你的中文有了很大的提高。 | Zhè yì nián, nǐ de Zhōngwén yǒule hěn dà de tígāo. | Your Chinese has improved a lot this year. |

**Watch out:** for growing taller use 长高, not 提高: ✗ 他的个子提高了 should be 他长高了.

### W3 · …以来 (since)
以来 goes after a time expression or an event (1950年以来, 开学以来, 改革开放以来) and means "from that point up to now". The sentence that follows describes something that has been going on, or has been changing, through the whole period, so it often contains 一直, 逐渐, 了 or a negative with 过. That is the difference from 以后: 以后 only says "after", can point to a single later moment, and can be used about the future; 以来 always reaches up to the present.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 上大学以来，我一直住在校外。 | Shàng dàxué yǐlái, wǒ yìzhí zhù zài xiàowài. | Ever since I started university, I've lived off campus. |
| 2020年以来，这个城市出现了很多新企业。 | 2020 nián yǐlái, zhège chéngshì chūxiànle hěn duō xīn qǐyè. | Since 2020, lots of new businesses have sprung up in this city. |
| 开学以来，他一节课都没有迟到过。 | Kāixué yǐlái, tā yì jié kè dōu méiyǒu chídàoguo. | Since the start of the semester he hasn't been late for a single class. |

**Watch out:** 以来 cannot point to the future: ✗ 毕业以来我要去北京工作 should be 毕业以后我要去北京工作.

### W4 · 拿…来说 (take … for example)
拿…来说 puts a noun into a frame to bring in a concrete example right after a general statement: first the claim, then 拿 X 来说, then the details about X. In conversation it is very often followed by 吧, which makes it sound relaxed, like "take X, for instance". The frame stands at the head of its clause, followed by a comma. Do not confuse it with 对…来说, which means "as far as X is concerned" and gives someone's point of view, not an example.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 很多年轻人不喜欢做家务，拿我弟弟来说吧，他从来不洗碗。 | Hěn duō niánqīngrén bù xǐhuan zuò jiāwù, ná wǒ dìdi láishuō ba, tā cónglái bù xǐ wǎn. | Lots of young people don't like doing chores. Take my younger brother: he never washes the dishes. |
| 这个城市变化很大，拿交通来说，十年前还没有地铁。 | Zhège chéngshì biànhuà hěn dà, ná jiāotōng láishuō, shí nián qián hái méiyǒu dìtiě. | This city has changed a lot. Take transport: ten years ago there was no subway. |
| 女性的工作机会多了，拿我们公司来说，一半的经理是女的。 | Nǚxìng de gōngzuò jīhuì duō le, ná wǒmen gōngsī láishuō, yíbàn de jīnglǐ shì nǚ de. | Women have more job opportunities now. In our company, for example, half the managers are women. |

**Watch out:** for "as far as I'm concerned" use 对…来说: ✗ 拿我来说，这个问题太难了 should be 对我来说，这个问题太难了.

### W5 · 只有…才… (only if)
只有…才… states that there is one and only one condition under which a result can happen. 只有 opens the first clause and introduces that condition; 才 goes in the second clause, after the subject and directly before the verb or modal verb (才能, 才会). Compare 只要…就… from the last lesson: 只要 gives a condition that is enough ("as long as"), while 只有 gives a condition that is necessary and rules out all the others ("only if"). So 只有 sounds stricter and is often used to insist or to warn.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 只有多说多练，才能把中文学好。 | Zhǐyǒu duō shuō duō liàn, cái néng bǎ Zhōngwén xuéhǎo. | Only by speaking and practising a lot can you master Chinese. |
| 只有天气好的时候，我们才在外边练球。 | Zhǐyǒu tiānqì hǎo de shíhou, wǒmen cái zài wàibian liàn qiú. | We only practise outdoors when the weather is good. |
| 只有大家都参加，比赛才有意思。 | Zhǐyǒu dàjiā dōu cānjiā, bǐsài cái yǒu yìsi. | The game is only fun if everyone takes part. |

**Watch out:** 只有 pairs with 才, not with 就: ✗ 只有你去，我就去 should be 只有你去，我才去.

### W6 · 表现 (to show, to display; performance)
As a verb, 表现 means to show a quality or a feeling, or to perform well or badly in a test, a match or a job. It is often followed by 得 and a description (表现得很好) or by 出 / 出来 when something becomes visible (表现出高兴的样子). As a noun it means someone's performance or behaviour: 他的表现, 这个学期的表现. It is about how someone does or comes across; putting on a show on stage is a different word, 表演.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 昨天德国队和西班牙队比赛，德国队表现得不太好，输了。 | Zuótiān Déguó duì hé Xībānyá duì bǐsài, Déguó duì biǎoxiàn de bú tài hǎo, shū le. | Germany played Spain yesterday; Germany didn't play very well and lost. |
| 他听到这个消息，一点儿也没表现出高兴的样子。 | Tā tīngdào zhège xiāoxi, yìdiǎnr yě méi biǎoxiàn chū gāoxìng de yàngzi. | When he heard the news, he didn't look the least bit pleased. |
| 你觉得他这个学期的表现怎么样？ | Nǐ juéde tā zhège xuéqī de biǎoxiàn zěnmeyàng? | What do you think of how he's done this semester? |

**Watch out:** a performance on stage is 表演, not 表现: ✗ 他在晚会上表现了一个节目 should be 他在晚会上表演了一个节目.

### W7 · 由 (by)
由 is a preposition that introduces the person or group responsible for doing something. The agent comes right after 由 and before the verb: 由你决定, 由学校安排. It is typical for arrangements, decisions, duties and who pays, and 来 often comes before the verb (由我来付). The thing being arranged usually stands at the start of the sentence as the topic. Unlike 被, which reports that something happened to the subject and often sounds unpleasant, 由 is neutral and is very often used about plans for the future.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这次晚会由安娜安排。 | Zhè cì wǎnhuì yóu Ānnà ānpái. | Anna is in charge of organising this party. |
| 饭钱由我来付，你们别客气。 | Fànqián yóu wǒ lái fù, nǐmen bié kèqi. | I'll pay for the meal, don't stand on ceremony. |
| 去哪儿旅行，由你们决定吧。 | Qù nǎr lǚxíng, yóu nǐmen juédìng ba. | Where we travel to is up to you. |

**Watch out:** for who is responsible use 由, not 被: ✗ 这件事被你决定 should be 这件事由你决定.

### Dialogue 2 · Who does the dishes?
*Setting: On Saturday evening, after 马克 has cooked dinner for the three of them at his apartment, an argument about the washing-up turns into a talk about men, women and housework.*

| Speaker | Chinese | Pinyin | English |

## Exercises

### E1 · Choose the right word {type=choice}
1. 只有多练习，你的中文___能提高。 [就 | 才 | 都] → 才
2. 这次晚会的饭菜___我们队员来准备。 [由 | 对 | 把] → 由
3. ___我们学校来说吧，女生比男生多。 [比 | 由 | 拿] → 拿
4. 他看电视看到很晚，最后在沙发上睡___了。 [过去 | 过来 | 起来] → 过去
5. 开学___，我们队一场比赛都没输过。 [起来 | 以内 | 以来] → 以来
6. 你别担心，我是很相信你___。 [了 | 的 | 过] → 的
7. 来中国以后，她___习惯了这儿的天气。 [互相 | 逐渐 | 只有] → 逐渐
8. 昨天的比赛，他___得非常好，大家都很高兴。 [出现 | 超过 | 表现] → 表现

### E2 · Put the words in order {type=order}
1. 由 / 这件事 / 决定吧 / 你 → 这件事由你决定吧。
2. 每天 / 才能赢比赛 / 我们只有 / 多练习 → 我们只有每天多练习，才能赢比赛。
3. 是很 / 他的中文 / 的 / 好 → 他的中文是很好的。
4. 以来 / 改革开放 / 变化很大 / 这个城市的 → 改革开放以来，这个城市的变化很大。
5. 互相 / 我们两个人 / 学习 / 应该 → 我们两个人应该互相学习。
6. 来说吧 / 拿 / 我家 / 家务都是我爸爸做的 → 拿我家来说吧，家务都是我爸爸做的。
7. 超过了 / 她的薪水 / 已经 / 她丈夫的 → 她的薪水已经超过了她丈夫的。

### E3 · Translate into Chinese {type=translate}
1. Since I started university, my Chinese has gradually improved. → 上大学以来，我的中文逐渐提高了。
2. You can only get up early if you go to bed early. → 只有早点儿睡觉，才能早点儿起床。
3. This matter is up to the teacher to decide. → 这件事由老师决定。
4. Take my mom, for example: she is a manager at a big company. → 拿我妈妈来说吧，她是一家大公司的经理。
5. He will definitely agree. → 他是一定会同意的。
6. Our team lost; the score was one to three. → 我们队输了，比分是一比三。
7. Women and men doing the same work should get the same salary. → 做一样工作的女性和男人应该拿一样的薪水。
