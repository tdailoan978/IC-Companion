# Lesson 10 · 世界变小了 · The World Is Getting Smaller

## Objectives
- After this lesson you can explain what a get-together is for: a celebration, a welcome meal (接风) or a farewell meal (饯行).

- After this lesson you can talk about getting used to life in another country and about how closer contact is making the world smaller.

## Grammar

### G1 · Word order in Chinese: a summary
**Pattern:** `Subject + (time / place / means / other adverbials) + verb + (complement) + (object)`

This last grammar point pulls together what you have learned about word order. The core order is subject, then adverbials, then the verb, then its complement, then the object: 我昨天在图书馆看了三个小时书. The key rule is that a describing element comes before what it describes: adjectives and 的 phrases before nouns, and time, place, manner and means before the verb, which is the reverse of English "I read at the library yesterday". Three patterns move the object forward: a topic-comment sentence puts known information first (那本书我看完了), 把 places the object before the verb, and 被 makes the receiver of the action the subject. Two principles settle most other questions: go from the general to the specific (year, month, day; country, city, street), and tell events in the order they happen (坐地铁去公司, 去饭馆吃饭). The classic learner error is copying English order and putting time or place at the end.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 下周六晚上七点，我们在马克家聚会。 | Xià zhōuliù wǎnshang qī diǎn, wǒmen zài Mǎkè jiā jùhuì. | We're getting together at Mark's place at seven next Saturday evening. |
| 他每天坐地铁去公司上班。 | Tā měitiān zuò dìtiě qù gōngsī shàng bān. | He takes the subway to the office every day. |
| 这几本书我都看完了。 | Zhè jǐ běn shū wǒ dōu kànwán le. | I've finished reading all of these books. |
| 我的蛋糕被弟弟吃了。 | Wǒ de dàngāo bèi dìdi chī le. | My cake was eaten by my little brother. |

**Watch out:** time and place go before the verb, not at the end: ✗ 我们聚会在马克家下周六 should be 我们下周六在马克家聚会.

### G2 · The 把 construction (review II): handing over, putting somewhere, turning into
**Pattern:** `Subj + 把 + known object + V + 给 + person / 在 + place / 成 + new thing`

The first review of 把 showed that its object must be specific and its verb cannot stand alone. This review looks at three common things that can follow the verb. 给 + a person says who receives the thing (把书还给他); 在 or 到 + a place says where the thing ends up (把菜放在桌子上); 成 + a noun says what the thing turns into, often by mistake (把"十"写成"千"). With 在/到 and 成, 把 is practically required, because Chinese does not allow both an object and a place or 成 phrase after one verb; with 给 you can also say 送给他一本书, but when the thing is specific and already known, the 把 sentence is the natural choice. In a request the subject is often dropped (把门关上). Two more rules: 不, 没 and modal verbs such as 要, 能 and 应该 stand before 把, never after the object (only 都 or 全, which sum up the object, come after it: 把零食都吃完); and a potential complement (吃不完, 拿不动) cannot appear in a 把 sentence, so use a topic sentence instead.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 请你把这本书还给王老师。 | Qǐng nǐ bǎ zhè běn shū huán gěi Wáng lǎoshī. | Please return this book to Teacher Wang. |
| 你把火锅放在桌子中间吧。 | Nǐ bǎ huǒguō fàng zài zhuōzi zhōngjiān ba. | Put the hotpot in the middle of the table. |
| 他不小心把“十”写成了“千”。 | Tā bù xiǎoxīn bǎ "shí" xiěchéngle "qiān". | He accidentally wrote "ten" as "thousand". |
| 我还没把这个消息告诉我爸爸妈妈。 | Wǒ hái méi bǎ zhège xiāoxi gàosu wǒ bàba māma. | I haven't told my parents the news yet. |

**Watch out:** no potential complement with 把, and the negative goes first: ✗ 我把这些饺子吃不完 should be 这些饺子我吃不完; ✗ 我把这件事没告诉他 should be 我没把这件事告诉他.

## Words & Phrases

### W1 · 接受 (to accept, to take on)
接受 is a verb that takes an object, and it means to accept something willingly or to agree to take it on. The object is usually abstract: 意见, 建议, 批评, 工作, 邀请, someone's 关照 or good intentions. It can also be a concrete gift, typically in polite refusals (这个礼物我不能接受). It is the normal verb for accepting advice or an arrangement, and 已经接受了 often means "has come to terms with it". Do not confuse it with 收 or 收到, which only say that something physically reached you: you 收到 a letter, but you 接受 a job offer or an apology.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 他接受了那家公司的工作，下个月去上海上班。 | Tā jiēshòule nà jiā gōngsī de gōngzuò, xià ge yuè qù Shànghǎi shàng bān. | He accepted the job with that company and starts in Shanghai next month. |
| 你的意见我接受，以后我一定注意。 | Nǐ de yìjiàn wǒ jiēshòu, yǐhòu wǒ yídìng zhùyì. | I accept your criticism; I'll definitely be more careful from now on. |
| 这个礼物太贵了，我不能接受。 | Zhège lǐwù tài guì le, wǒ bù néng jiēshòu. | This present is too expensive; I can't accept it. |

**Watch out:** for something that simply arrives, use 收到: ✗ 我昨天接受了你的信 should be 我昨天收到了你的信.

### W2 · 我说 (I was wondering why)
This 我说 does not report what you said. It is a colloquial opener at the start of a sentence, used the moment a small puzzle is cleared up: you had noticed something odd, and now you know the reason. The puzzle follows straight after 我说, usually with 怎么 or 为什么 (我说他怎么没来), and the explanation comes next, very often introduced by 原来. The whole sentence sounds like "So that's why …!" or "No wonder …!". It is spoken, friendly and a little surprised, and it is close to 怪不得 ("no wonder"), but 我说 is more colloquial and is almost always followed by a 怎么 or 为什么 question.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我说他今天怎么这么高兴，原来找到工作了。 | Wǒ shuō tā jīntiān zěnme zhème gāoxìng, yuánlái zhǎodào gōngzuò le. | I was wondering why he was so happy today. Turns out he's found a job. |
| 我说你怎么不接电话，原来手机没电了。 | Wǒ shuō nǐ zěnme bù jiē diànhuà, yuánlái shǒujī méi diàn le. | So that's why you didn't answer: your phone was dead. |
| 我说这个菜怎么这么辣，原来你放了这么多辣椒！ | Wǒ shuō zhège cài zěnme zhème là, yuánlái nǐ fàngle zhème duō làjiāo! | No wonder this dish is so spicy: you put in all those chilies! |

**Watch out:** this 我说 needs a puzzle and its answer; to report your own words, say 我刚才说 or 我说过.

### W3 · 而已 (and no more)
而已 is a particle that stands at the very end of a statement and makes what was said smaller: "just", "that's all", "nothing more". It often works together with 只, 只是, 不过 or 才 earlier in the sentence, so the idea of "only" is stated twice, which is normal. Typical uses are modest descriptions of yourself, reassuring someone that something is not serious, and noting that an amount is small. 而已 sounds a little more written than its spoken partner 罢了, but it is very common in conversation too. Because it plays things down, it does not fit sentences that praise or exaggerate.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我只是随便说说而已，你别生气。 | Wǒ zhǐshì suíbiàn shuōshuo éryǐ, nǐ bié shēngqì. | I was only talking off the top of my head; don't be angry. |
| 这个菜不难做，只不过多放了一点儿糖而已。 | Zhège cài bù nán zuò, zhǐ búguò duō fàngle yìdiǎnr táng éryǐ. | This dish isn't hard to make; you just add a bit more sugar, that's all. |
| 他学中文才学了半年而已，已经说得很不错了。 | Tā xué Zhōngwén cái xuéle bàn nián éryǐ, yǐjīng shuō de hěn búcuò le. | He's only studied Chinese for half a year, and he already speaks it quite well. |

**Watch out:** 而已 makes things smaller, so it cannot go with praise: ✗ 她是一流的演员而已 should be 她只是个三流演员而已.

### W4 · 在…下 (under, with)
在…下 wraps around a noun phrase to name the condition or support under which something happens: 在老师的帮助下 "with the teacher's help", 在大家的努力下 "thanks to everyone's efforts". The noun phrase is usually someone's 帮助, 关照, 领导, 努力 or 影响, joined with 的. The whole frame stands before the verb, normally at the start of the sentence before the subject, and the main clause tells the result. It sounds somewhat formal and is common in speeches, thank-you words and reports. The common mistake is to drop 下, which leaves the sentence hanging.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 在老师的帮助下，我的中文越来越好了。 | Zài lǎoshī de bāngzhù xià, wǒ de Zhōngwén yuè lái yuè hǎo le. | With my teacher's help, my Chinese has been getting better and better. |
| 在朋友们的关照下，她很快就习惯了这儿的生活。 | Zài péngyoumen de guānzhào xià, tā hěn kuài jiù xíguànle zhèr de shēnghuó. | With her friends looking after her, she soon got used to life here. |
| 在大家的努力下，这次聚会搞得很成功。 | Zài dàjiā de nǔlì xià, zhè cì jùhuì gǎo de hěn chénggōng. | Thanks to everyone's efforts, the party was a great success. |

**Watch out:** do not leave out 下: ✗ 在老师的帮助，我考得很好 should be 在老师的帮助下，我考得很好.

### W5 · 你说呢? (what do you think?)
你说呢 is a short question tagged onto the end of a suggestion or a choice to ask the other person's opinion: "What do you say?" It softens the suggestion and invites the listener to decide too. Said as a reply, with a playful or slightly impatient tone, it becomes rhetorical: the answer is obvious, so why ask? In that use it answers a question by throwing it back, and the speaker often adds the reasons that make the answer obvious. With an older person or a superior you can say 您说呢. It differs from 你觉得呢, which is a neutral request for an opinion and is not used rhetorically.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我们是吃火锅还是吃烤鸭？你说呢？ | Wǒmen shì chī huǒguō háishi chī kǎoyā? Nǐ shuō ne? | Shall we have hotpot or roast duck? What do you say? |
| 我觉得应该早点儿出发，你说呢？ | Wǒ juéde yīnggāi zǎo diǎnr chūfā, nǐ shuō ne? | I think we should leave a bit earlier. What do you think? |
| ——你会做饭吗？——我一个人住了三年了，你说呢？ | —— Nǐ huì zuò fàn ma? —— Wǒ yí ge rén zhùle sān nián le, nǐ shuō ne? | "Can you cook?" "I've lived on my own for three years. What do you think?" |

## Exercises

### E1 · Choose the right word {type=choice}
1. 今天晚上我们去吃火锅，___？ [我说 | 而已 | 你说呢] → 你说呢
2. 我不是生气，只是有点儿累___。 [而已 | 你说呢 | 我说] → 而已
3. ___这儿今天怎么这么多人，原来是周末。 [你说呢 | 我说 | 而已] → 我说
4. 在王教授的帮助___，他写完了这篇文章。 [里 | 下 | 上] → 下
5. 请你把这封信交___王经理。 [给 | 成 | 在] → 给
6. 我把“卖”字看___了“买”字。 [给 | 在 | 成] → 成
7. 你把行李放___门口吧。 [成 | 在 | 给] → 在
8. 公司请他去上海工作，他已经___了。 [关照 | 庆祝 | 接受] → 接受

### E2 · Put the words in order {type=order}
1. 门口 / 我们七点 / 见吧 / 在学校 → 我们七点在学校门口见吧。
2. 出租车上了 / 忘在 / 我 / 把钱包 → 我把钱包忘在出租车上了。
3. 写成了 / 把二十 / 服务员 / 十二 → 服务员把二十写成了十二。
4. 两个月 / 我们 / 而已 / 只认识 → 我们只认识两个月而已。
5. 去上海出差 / 他下个月 / 坐火车 / 打算 → 他下个月打算坐火车去上海出差。
6. 放在冰箱里 / 把红酒 / 他 / 没 → 他没把红酒放在冰箱里。
7. 生病了 / 我说她 / 原来 / 怎么没来上课 → 我说她怎么没来上课，原来生病了。

### E3 · Translate into Chinese {type=translate}
1. Our teacher is going back to China next week, so tomorrow we're giving her a farewell dinner. → 我们老师下周要回中国了，明天我们给她饯行。
2. Your American friend arrives tomorrow; let's take him out for hotpot as a welcome dinner. → 你的美国朋友明天到，我们请他去吃火锅，给他接风吧。
3. Don't leave your phone on the table. → 别把手机放在桌子上。
4. I only had one glass of wine, that's all. → 我只喝了一杯酒而已。
5. With everyone's help, I found a stable job. → 在大家的帮助下，我找到了一个稳定的工作。
6. The batteries this company makes are selling really well in Europe. → 这家公司做的电池在欧洲卖得很火。
7. Let's go by subway. What do you think? → 我们坐地铁去吧，你说呢？
8. However far apart we are, our friendship will never change. → 不管我们离得多远，我们的友谊永远不变。

### E4 · Match the word {type=match}
1. 庆祝 → to celebrate
2. 接风 → to host a welcome meal for a visitor from afar
3. 饯行 → to host a farewell meal
4. 出差 → to be away on a business trip
5. 推销 → to market, to promote the sale of goods
6. 校友 → schoolmate, alumnus
7. 面熟 → familiar-looking
8. 稳定 → stable, steady
