# Lesson 2 · 中国的变化 · Changes in China

## Objectives
- After this lesson you can describe how a place has changed and what it has kept, with 变化, 盖, 保留 and vivid pictures like 一座座大厦.
- After this lesson you can say that a place is different from what you imagined, and draw a conclusion from what you see with 看起来.
- After this lesson you can voice concern about where a continuing change may lead, and answer it with 不管….
- After this lesson you can thank someone with 要不是, say "never" with 从来, and promise to do your best with 尽可能.
- After this lesson you can colour your speech with 啊 and its variants, and recognise the classical 以 X 为 Y in sayings such as 民以食为天.

## Grammar

### G1 · The modal particle 啊 and its variants
**Pattern:** `Sentence + 啊 (呀 / 哇 / 哪)`

At the end of a sentence 啊 adds feeling rather than meaning. In an exclamation it makes surprise or admiration stronger; after an explanation or a reminder it sounds like "you know" or "I'm telling you"; in a question it softens the tone and sounds friendlier than a bare question; after a request or a warning it makes the order less abrupt, though said quickly it can also sound urgent. Because 啊 runs into the syllable before it, its sound changes. After -a, -e, -i, -o or -ü it becomes ya, written 呀; after -u, -ao or -ou it becomes wa, written 哇; after -n it becomes na, written 哪; after -ng it sounds like nga but is still written 啊. In everyday writing 啊 is also acceptable in all these places; the variant characters just show the sound. It belongs to speech and to informal writing such as text messages. The usual slip is to stack it on top of 吗.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这儿的变化真大呀！ | Zhèr de biànhuà zhēn dà ya! | This place has really changed so much! |
| 你怎么还不走哇？ | Nǐ zěnme hái bù zǒu wa? | Why haven't you left yet? |
| 今天的作业真难哪！ | Jīntiān de zuòyè zhēn nán na! | Today's homework is really hard! |
| 外边好冷啊，多穿点儿衣服吧。 | Wàibian hǎo lěng a, duō chuān diǎnr yīfu ba. | It's freezing outside, put on some more clothes. |

**Watch out:** 啊 never follows 吗: ✗ 你去吗呀？ should be 你去吗？ or 你去不去呀？

### G2 · 以 X 为 Y (to take X as Y)
**Pattern:** `以 + X + 为 + Y`

This is a pattern from classical Chinese that survives in sayings and in formal or written style. 以 means "to take" and 为 means "as" or "to be", so the whole means "to regard X as Y" or "to treat X as Y". Note that 为 is read wéi here, not wèi as in 为什么. The best-known example is the saying 民以食为天: to ordinary people, food is heaven. Two fixed frames are common in modern Chinese too: 以…为主 (to be mainly…) and 以…为特色 (to be known for…). In everyday conversation people usually say the same thing with 把 X 当作 Y, so do not use 以 X 为 Y for casual remarks about friends or daily life.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这家饭馆以南京小吃为特色。 | Zhè jiā fànguǎn yǐ Nánjīng xiǎochī wéi tèsè. | This restaurant specialises in Nanjing snacks. |
| 这个城市的建筑以传统建筑为主。 | Zhège chéngshì de jiànzhù yǐ chuántǒng jiànzhù wéi zhǔ. | The buildings in this city are mainly traditional. |
| 他工作特别忙，常常住在办公室，真是以公司为家。 | Tā gōngzuò tèbié máng, chángcháng zhù zài bàngōngshì, zhēn shì yǐ gōngsī wéi jiā. | He is so busy at work that he often sleeps in the office; he really treats the company as his home. |

**Watch out:** 为 is read wéi in this pattern: ✗ yǐ … wèi … should be yǐ … wéi ….

### G3 · 一 + reduplicated measure word
**Pattern:** `一 + MW + MW (+ 的) + Noun`

Doubling a measure word after 一 paints a picture: it shows a large number of similar things one after another, in rows, crowds or streams, as when you stand on a street and see building after building or car after car. The effect is vivid and descriptive, a little like "row upon row" in English, so it suits scenery and scene-setting. It is not just a fancier way to say 很多. If you only want to state a quantity as a fact, 很多 or 不少 is the right word. The phrase usually sits in a description (是, 有, 盖起了 …), and 一 changes tone as usual: yí zuòzuò, yì jiājiā.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这几年，城里盖起了一座座新大厦。 | Zhè jǐ nián, chéng li gài qile yí zuòzuò xīn dàshà. | In the last few years, one new tower after another has gone up in the city. |
| 一辆辆汽车从我们旁边开过去了。 | Yí liàngliàng qìchē cóng wǒmen pángbiān kāi guoqu le. | Car after car drove past us. |
| 墙上挂着一张张老照片。 | Qiáng shang guàzhe yì zhāngzhāng lǎo zhàopiàn. | Old photos hang all over the wall, one after another. |

**Watch out:** do not use it for a plain count: ✗ 我有一本本书 should be 我有很多书.

## Words & Phrases

### W1 · 要不是 (if it were not for; but for)
要不是 is a spoken conjunction that opens the first clause of a sentence and names something that really did happen; the second clause then says what would have happened without it, usually with 就 or 早就, and often with 了 at the end. So 要不是你帮我 means "you did help me, and if you hadn't…". This makes it a natural way to thank someone or to blame something. Compare 要是 (if), which is an open condition about something that may or may not happen. The thing after 要不是 can be a person (要不是你) or a whole event (要不是下雨).

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 要不是下雨，我们今天就去长城了。 | Yàobúshì xià yǔ, wǒmen jīntiān jiù qù Chángchéng le. | If it weren't for the rain, we'd have gone to the Great Wall today. |
| 要不是你告诉我，我真不知道这儿以前是一个小饭馆。 | Yàobúshì nǐ gàosu wǒ, wǒ zhēn bù zhīdào zhèr yǐqián shì yí ge xiǎo fànguǎn. | If you hadn't told me, I'd never have known there used to be a little restaurant here. |
| 要不是高铁这么快，我们一天回不来。 | Yàobúshì gāotiě zhème kuài, wǒmen yì tiān huí bu lái. | If the high-speed train weren't so fast, we couldn't get back in one day. |

**Watch out:** the "not" is already inside 要不是, so do not add 没 to say "if you hadn't…": ✗ 要不是你没带地图，我们就找不到了 should be 要不是你带了地图，我们就找不到了.

### W2 · 从来 (from past till present, always, at all times)
从来 is an adverb that looks back over the whole of the past up to now. It sits before the verb and is mostly used with a negative. 从来不 + verb describes a habit or a principle (someone never does something), while 从来没(有) + verb + 过 says that something has never once happened. In positive sentences it usually takes 都 and means "has always been so", often to answer someone's surprise. It does not look forward, so it cannot promise that you will never do something in the future.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我从来没去过法国。 | Wǒ cónglái méi qùguo Fǎguó. | I have never been to France. |
| 我爸爸从来不喝咖啡，他只喝茶。 | Wǒ bàba cónglái bù hē kāfēi, tā zhǐ hē chá. | My father never drinks coffee; he only drinks tea. |
| 这条街从来都是这么热闹。 | Zhè tiáo jiē cónglái dōu shì zhème rènao. | This street has always been this lively. |

**Watch out:** with 没 you need 过: ✗ 我从来没吃这种小吃 should be 我从来没吃过这种小吃.

### W3 · 看起来 (it seems)
看起来 introduces an impression or a conclusion drawn from what you can see or from facts just mentioned: "it looks as if", "judging by this". It usually opens the clause, and the guess follows (看起来要下雨了). It can also stand right after a subject to describe how someone or something looks (你看起来很累). The 起来 is pronounced lightly, kàn qǐlai. Use it when you are inferring; when you know something for certain, simply state it.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 门口有这么多人，看起来这家饭馆的小吃很好吃。 | Ménkǒu yǒu zhème duō rén, kàn qǐlai zhè jiā fànguǎn de xiǎochī hěn hǎochī. | There are so many people at the door; the snacks here must be good. |
| 你今天看起来不太高兴，怎么了？ | Nǐ jīntiān kàn qǐlai bú tài gāoxìng, zěnme le? | You don't look too happy today. What's up? |
| 看起来要下雨了，我们快回去吧。 | Kàn qǐlai yào xià yǔ le, wǒmen kuài huíqu ba. | It looks like rain; let's hurry back. |

**Watch out:** 看起来 is not "to see": ✗ 我看起来一座大厦 should be 我看见了一座大厦.

### W4 · 尽可能 (as much as possible, to do one's utmost)
尽可能 goes before a verb phrase or an adjective and says that you will do something to the greatest extent you can. It is common in promises, advice and requests, and often has 会 or 要 in front of it (我会尽可能…, 你要尽可能…). It combines naturally with 多, 少, 早 and 快 before the verb: 尽可能多说, 尽可能少吃, 尽可能早点儿. The tone is earnest and slightly formal, a little stronger than just saying 多 or 早.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我会尽可能早点儿回来，你别担心。 | Wǒ huì jǐn kěnéng zǎo diǎnr huílai, nǐ bié dānxīn. | I'll get back as early as I can, don't worry. |
| 晚上尽可能少喝咖啡，要不然睡不着。 | Wǎnshang jǐn kěnéng shǎo hē kāfēi, yàobùrán shuì bu zháo. | Drink as little coffee as possible in the evening, or you won't be able to sleep. |
| 考试以前，你要尽可能多休息。 | Kǎoshì yǐqián, nǐ yào jǐn kěnéng duō xiūxi. | Before the exam, get as much rest as you can. |

**Watch out:** 尽 is read jǐn (third tone) here, not jìn.

## Exercises

### E1 · Choose the right word {type=choice}
1. ___你帮忙，我今天就来不及了。 [要是 | 要不是 | 不管] → 要不是
2. 这句话我___没听说过，是什么意思？ [刚才 | 马上 | 从来] → 从来
3. ___天气怎么样，我们明天都要去长城。 [不管 | 要不是 | 尽可能] → 不管
4. 你今天___很累，昨天晚上没睡好吧？ [看见 | 看起来 | 看过] → 看起来
5. 这次来北京，我想___多尝几种小吃。 [从来 | 看起来 | 尽可能] → 尽可能
6. 路上一辆___汽车开过去，一个骑自行车的人也没有。 [辆 | 座 | 本] → 辆
7. 中国人常常说“民以食___天”。 [是 | 为 | 把] → 为
8. 你们的新家真大___！ [哇 | 哪 | 呀] → 呀

### E2 · Put the words in order {type=order}
1. 不吃 / 快餐 / 从来 / 我爷爷 → 我爷爷从来不吃快餐。
2. 来接我们 / 我们就得坐地铁了 / 要不是 / 开车 / 表哥 → 要不是表哥开车来接我们，我们就得坐地铁了。
3. 几张照片 / 尽可能 / 我会 / 多拍 → 我会尽可能多拍几张照片。
4. 游客 / 多了 / 这条街上的 / 比以前 → 这条街上的游客比以前多了。
5. 为特色 / 这条老街 / 传统建筑 / 以 → 这条老街以传统建筑为特色。
6. 传统建筑 / 夫子庙 / 一座座 / 附近有 → 夫子庙附近有一座座传统建筑。

### E3 · Translate into Chinese {type=translate}
1. This building was only finished at the end of last year. → 这座大厦是去年年末才盖好的。
2. If it weren't for high-speed rail, we couldn't have got to Shanghai so quickly. → 要不是有高速铁路，我们不可能这么快就到上海。
3. Beijing is completely different from what I imagined. → 北京跟我想像的完全不一样。
4. No matter how the city changes, I hope it keeps its own character. → 不管城市怎么变，我希望它能保留自己的特色。
5. I have never tasted this kind of snack. → 我从来没尝过这种小吃。
6. When you're in China, try to speak as much Chinese as possible. → 在中国的时候，你要尽可能多说中文。
7. It's too late to go to the Great Wall today. → 今天去长城已经来不及了。

### E4 · Match the word {type=match}
1. 变化 → to change; change
2. 盖 → to build, to construct
3. 骑 → to ride
4. 游客 → tourist
5. 对面 → opposite side
6. 服装 → clothing, apparel
7. 保留 → to retain
8. 尝 → to taste
