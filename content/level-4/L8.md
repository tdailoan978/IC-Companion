# Lesson 8 · 中国历史 · China's History

## Objectives
- After this lesson you can give a short outline of Chinese history, from the Qin dynasty to the founding of the Republic of China.
- After this lesson you can say what some of the major dynasties are known for: the First Emperor's unification, the Han and the Silk Road, Tang poetry, Song inventions.
- After this lesson you can talk about historical objects and inventions you see in a museum, and say that something is "one of" a group with 之一 or pick out part of a group with 其中.
- After this lesson you can weigh up the contributions and the faults of a historical figure, using 在…方面 and 跟…有关系.
- After this lesson you can say firmly that something will never happen again, or never happened again, with 再也不…了 and 再也没….

## Grammar

### G1 · 之一 (one of …)
**Pattern:** `[group: superlative or plural category] + 之一`

之 is a particle inherited from classical Chinese that does roughly the job of 的, so 之一 literally means "one of". It is how you say that something belongs to a set without being the only member: one of the greatest poets, one of my favourite cities. The group always comes first and 之一 closes the phrase, which then usually stands after 是. The group is very often a superlative with 最 (最有名的…之一), because "one of the most …" is the commonest use. 之一 cannot stand on its own, and it never takes a measure word: do not slip 一个 in front of the phrase as English "one of" might tempt you to. In speech 其中一个 says something similar, but 之一 is neater and more formal.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 长城是世界上最有名的建筑之一。 | Chángchéng shì shìjiè shang zuì yǒumíng de jiànzhù zhī yī. | The Great Wall is one of the most famous structures in the world. |
| 孔子是中国历史上最伟大的思想家之一。 | Kǒngzǐ shì Zhōngguó lìshǐ shang zuì wěidà de sīxiǎngjiā zhī yī. | Confucius is one of the greatest thinkers in Chinese history. |
| 打篮球是我弟弟最大的爱好之一。 | Dǎ lánqiú shì wǒ dìdi zuì dà de àihào zhī yī. | Playing basketball is one of my younger brother's biggest hobbies. |
| 交通是这个城市最大的问题之一。 | Jiāotōng shì zhège chéngshì zuì dà de wèntí zhī yī. | Traffic is one of this city's biggest problems. |

**Watch out:** no measure word in front: ✗ 他是一个我最好的朋友之一 should be 他是我最好的朋友之一.

### G2 · 其中 (among them, within it)
**Pattern:** `[group or whole], 其中 + (的 + N / clause)`

其 is a literary pronoun meaning "that" or "those", so 其中 means "among them" or "within it". It always points back to a group or a whole that has just been mentioned, and then picks out part of it: one member, a few members, the best or the most interesting one. Typically the first clause sets up the group (我们班有二十个学生) and the next clause starts with 其中 (其中五个是从中国来的). 其中 can be followed directly by a number or 有, by 最 + adjective, or by 的 + noun. Compare 之一: 之一 ends the phrase and means "one of"; 其中 begins a new clause and can pick out any part. Without a group in front, 其 has nothing to refer to, so never open a topic with 其中; and since 其 already means "them", careful writers do not put 他们 in front of it either.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我们班有二十个学生，其中五个是从中国来的。 | Wǒmen bān yǒu èrshí ge xuésheng, qízhōng wǔ ge shì cóng Zhōngguó lái de. | There are twenty students in our class, and five of them come from China. |
| 这个展厅里有很多兵马俑，其中有一个特别高。 | Zhège zhǎntīng li yǒu hěn duō bīngmǎyǒng, qízhōng yǒu yí ge tèbié gāo. | There are lots of terracotta warriors in this gallery, and one of them is especially tall. |
| 我借了三本书，其中最有意思的是写李白的那本。 | Wǒ jièle sān běn shū, qízhōng zuì yǒu yìsi de shì xiě Lǐ Bái de nà běn. | I borrowed three books, and the most interesting of them is the one about Li Bai. |
| 中国有很多伟大的发明，造纸就是其中之一。 | Zhōngguó yǒu hěn duō wěidà de fāmíng, zào zhǐ jiù shì qízhōng zhī yī. | China has many great inventions, and papermaking is one of them. |

**Watch out:** 其中 needs a group mentioned before it: ✗ 其中我最喜欢李白 should be 唐朝有很多诗人，其中我最喜欢李白.

## Words & Phrases

### W1 · 参观 vs. 游览 (to visit vs. to tour)
Both verbs take a place as their object, but the purpose is different. 参观 is a visit to learn or to see how something works: you 参观 a school, a museum, a factory, a company or an exhibition hall, often as a group and often guided. 游览 is sightseeing for pleasure at a scenic or historic place: mountains, lakes, an old town, a famous park. So a class trip to a factory is 参观, and a holiday walk around a famous mountain is 游览. Both are a little formal; in everyday speech 去看看 or 去玩儿 is common. Neither is used for visiting people: for friends and family use 看 or 去…家.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 下个星期我们班要去参观一家汽车工厂。 | Xià ge xīngqī wǒmen bān yào qù cānguān yì jiā qìchē gōngchǎng. | Next week our class is going to visit a car factory. |
| 今天我们只参观了博物馆的两个展厅。 | Jīntiān wǒmen zhǐ cānguānle bówùguǎn de liǎng ge zhǎntīng. | Today we only looked round two of the museum's galleries. |
| 去年夏天，我和父母游览了长城。 | Qùnián xiàtiān, wǒ hé fùmǔ yóulǎnle Chángchéng. | Last summer my parents and I went sightseeing on the Great Wall. |

**Watch out:** a scenic place is toured, not "visited" to study: ✗ 我们在云南参观了石林 should be 我们在云南游览了石林.

### W2 · 在…方面 (in terms of, in the area of)
在…方面 is a frame that names the field you are talking about. The topic goes between 在 and 方面 (在学习方面, 在技术方面, 在经济方面), and the frame usually stands at the head of the sentence or right after the subject, before the main verb or adjective. A word like 很多 or 各个 can also fill the slot (在很多方面, "in many respects"). With 的 it can also describe a noun (在交通方面的问题). It is a little more formal than English "when it comes to", so it suits presentations and serious discussion. Do not add 上 after 方面, and keep the topic inside the frame.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 在学习方面，他从来不让父母担心。 | Zài xuéxí fāngmiàn, tā cónglái bú ràng fùmǔ dānxīn. | When it comes to his studies, he has never given his parents anything to worry about. |
| 这家公司在技术方面是最先进的。 | Zhè jiā gōngsī zài jìshù fāngmiàn shì zuì xiānjìn de. | In terms of technology, this company is the most advanced. |
| 唐朝在很多方面都很发达。 | Tángcháo zài hěn duō fāngmiàn dōu hěn fādá. | The Tang dynasty flourished in many respects. |

**Watch out:** nothing comes after 方面: ✗ 在学习方面上，他很好 should be 在学习方面，他很好.

### W3 · X 跟 Y 有关(系) (related to, having to do with)
This pattern says that X has to do with Y. It works in two positions. As a predicate it closes the sentence: 这件事跟他有关系, "this has something to do with him"; the negative is 没有关系 or 没关系, and here 系 stays. As a modifier it goes before 的 and a noun: 跟历史有关的书, "books related to history"; in this position the short form 有关 is usual, especially in writing. Note the word order: 跟 Y comes before 有关, unlike English "related to Y". The colloquial 没关系 "never mind" is the same phrase without the 跟 part.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 他找的工作跟历史有关系。 | Tā zhǎo de gōngzuò gēn lìshǐ yǒu guānxì. | The job he's found has to do with history. |
| 图书馆里跟汉朝有关的书很多。 | Túshūguǎn li gēn Hàncháo yǒuguān de shū hěn duō. | The library has lots of books on the Han dynasty. |
| 他生病跟每天熬夜很有关系。 | Tā shēng bìng gēn měitiān áo yè hěn yǒu guānxì. | His getting ill has a lot to do with staying up late every night. |

**Watch out:** 跟 Y comes before 有关系, not after it as in English: ✗ 这件事有关系我 should be 这件事跟我有关系.

### W4 · 再也没…了 / 再也不…了 (no more, not anymore)
再也 + a negative is a strong "never again". Choose the negative by meaning. 再也不 + verb (+ 了) states a decision or a new state that will last from now on: 我再也不喝可乐了, "I'm never drinking cola again". 再也没(有) + verb looks back and says that something did not happen again after a certain point: 他走了以后，再也没回来, "he left and never came back"; it often takes 过 after the verb or 了 at the end. Sentence-final 了 marks the change. Both are emotional and emphatic, much stronger than plain 不 or 没. Do not use 再也没 for a promise about the future.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 那家饭馆的菜又贵又难吃，我再也不去了。 | Nà jiā fànguǎn de cài yòu guì yòu nánchī, wǒ zài yě bú qù le. | That restaurant's food is expensive and awful; I'm never going there again. |
| 他搬到上海以后，再也没给我打过电话。 | Tā bāndào Shànghǎi yǐhòu, zài yě méi gěi wǒ dǎguo diànhuà. | After he moved to Shanghai, he never phoned me again. |
| 考完试以后，我再也不熬夜了。 | Kǎowán shì yǐhòu, wǒ zài yě bù áo yè le. | Once the exams are over, I'm never staying up late again. |

**Watch out:** a resolution about the future takes 不: ✗ 从明天起我再也没喝可乐了 should be 从明天起我再也不喝可乐了.

### Dialogue 2 · In the China gallery
*Setting: On Saturday afternoon 林月, 马克 and 安娜 walk through the new China gallery at the city museum. 马克 picks where to start, and 林月 reads the labels for the other two.*

## Exercises

### E1 · Choose the right word {type=choice}
1. 秦始皇是中国历史上最有名的皇帝___。 [其中 | 之一 | 一些] → 之一
2. 我们班有十个学生，___三个是从中国来的。 [其中 | 之一 | 一起] → 其中
3. 下个星期我们要去___一家电脑公司。 [游览 | 进行 | 参观] → 参观
4. 我们花了一天的时间___了那座有名的山。 [参观 | 游览 | 进行] → 游览
5. 这本书跟中国历史有___。 [部分 | 方面 | 关系] → 关系
6. ___生活方面，他还需要父母帮忙。 [在 | 对 | 跟] → 在
7. 这个电影太没意思了，以后我再也___看了。 [没 | 别 | 不] → 不
8. 这个城市___是一个朝代的首都，现在只是一个小城了。 [已经 | 曾经 | 一直] → 曾经

### E2 · Put the words in order {type=order}
1. 之一 / 最伟大的 / 李白是中国历史上 / 诗人 → 李白是中国历史上最伟大的诗人之一。
2. 其中 / 一本是跟 / 我买了三本书 / 秦始皇有关的 → 我买了三本书，其中一本是跟秦始皇有关的。
3. 游戏了 / 再也不 / 他说他 / 上网玩 → 他说他再也不上网玩游戏了。
4. 宋朝在 / 方面 / 技术 / 很先进 → 宋朝在技术方面很先进。
5. 建立了 / 孙中山 / 中华民国 / 领导革命 → 孙中山领导革命，建立了中华民国。
6. 都跟 / 有关系 / 这个展厅里的东西 / 唐朝 → 这个展厅里的东西都跟唐朝有关系。
7. 打算 / 我们 / 参观兵马俑 / 去博物馆 → 我们打算去博物馆参观兵马俑。

1. Confucius's thinking has had a great influence on China. → 孔子的思想对中国的影响很大。
