# Lesson 6 · 男朋友女朋友 · Dating

## Objectives
- After this lesson you can describe someone's personality and interests, and say in what respect two people are alike or different with (在)…上.
- After this lesson you can ask a friend what is weighing on them and press for the real story with 到底.
- After this lesson you can describe a forgetful, careless person with 马虎, 丢三拉四 and 忘得一干二净.
- After this lesson you can say what makes you angry and talk about a quarrel, an apology and making up.
- After this lesson you can choose correctly between 的, 得 and 地, and use 原来 both for a sudden discovery and for "originally".

## Grammar

### G1 · (在)…上 (in terms of, as far as … goes)
**Pattern:** `(在) + abstract noun + 上，clause` · `Subj + (在) + abstract noun + 上 + predicate`

You know 上 as "on top of" a physical thing (桌子上). Attached to an abstract noun such as 学习, 工作, 生活 or 爱好, it no longer means a surface: it marks the area you are talking about, like English "in terms of", "when it comes to" or "as far as … goes". The phrase either opens the sentence or sits after the subject, right before the predicate, and 在 is often dropped, especially after the subject. It is handy for comparing people in one respect while leaving other respects aside: someone can be careful at work but careless in daily life. Learners tend to use 里 here, or to put a place noun in the frame; 在宿舍上 is not Chinese, because a dorm is a place, not an area of life.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 在学习上，我的高中同学常常帮助我。 | Zài xuéxí shang, wǒ de gāozhōng tóngxué chángcháng bāngzhù wǒ. | When it came to studying, my high school classmates often helped me. |
| 他学习上很用功，可是生活上很马虎。 | Tā xuéxí shang hěn yònggōng, kěshì shēnghuó shang hěn mǎhu. | He works hard at his studies, but he's careless in everyday life. |
| 生活上有什么问题，你可以来找我。 | Shēnghuó shang yǒu shénme wèntí, nǐ kěyǐ lái zhǎo wǒ. | If you have any problems in day-to-day life, you can come to me. |

**Watch out:** the frame takes 上, not 里: ✗ 在学习里，他很用功 should be 在学习上，他很用功.

### G2 · Verb 来 verb 去 (over and over)
**Pattern:** `V + 来 + V + 去 (，result clause)`

Put the same one-syllable verb on both sides of 来 and 去 and the action goes back and forth or round and round: 走来走去 is pacing up and down, 想来想去 is turning something over in your mind, 说来说去 is talking in circles. The pattern stresses that a lot of effort went in, so it usually sets up a second clause that tells how things came out, very often with 还是 ("in the end, still"), 最后 or a disappointing 也没…. Unlike a reduplicated verb (想想, "think a bit"), which makes an action light and short, V来V去 makes it long and repeated. The object does not go inside the pattern; mention it before or in the result clause.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 他在房间里走来走去，不知道该怎么办。 | Tā zài fángjiān li zǒu lái zǒu qù, bù zhīdào gāi zěnme bàn. | He paced up and down the room, not knowing what to do. |
| 他想来想去，还是给她打了电话。 | Tā xiǎng lái xiǎng qù, háishi gěi tā dǎle diànhuà. | He thought it over and over, and in the end he called her after all. |
| 她在商店里看来看去，最后什么也没买。 | Tā zài shāngdiàn li kàn lái kàn qù, zuìhòu shénme yě méi mǎi. | She looked all around the shop and ended up buying nothing. |
| 他们说来说去，还是那几句话。 | Tāmen shuō lái shuō qù, háishi nà jǐ jù huà. | They talked and talked, but kept saying the same few things. |

**Watch out:** the object stays out of the pattern: ✗ 我找钥匙来找钥匙去 should be 我找来找去，也没找到钥匙.

### G3 · Comparing 的, 得 and 地 (II)
**Pattern:** `modifier + 的 + noun` · `V / Adj + 得 + complement` · `adverbial + 地 + V`

All three are pronounced de, so the only way to choose is to look at what comes right after. If a noun follows, you need 的 (开朗的人). If the particle follows a verb or adjective and a description of degree or result comes after it, you need 得 (相处得很好). If a description comes first and a verb follows, saying how the action is done, you need 地 (生气地说). You met 的 and 得 in Level 2; what this lesson adds is a firmer grip on 地, which is most common after two-syllable adjectives (高兴地, 生气地), after 很 + adjective, and after reduplicated adjectives (高高兴兴地). The usual slip is 得 before the verb, mixing up "how he does it" (地) with "how well it turns out" (得).

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我高中的时候有一个很开朗的同学。 | Wǒ gāozhōng de shíhou yǒu yí ge hěn kāilǎng de tóngxué. | In high school I had a really outgoing classmate. |
| 他们两个人虽然爱好不同，可是相处得很好。 | Tāmen liǎng ge rén suīrán àihào bù tóng, kěshì xiāngchǔ de hěn hǎo. | The two of them have different interests, but they get along very well. |
| 她生气地说：“我不想听！” | Tā shēng qì de shuō: "Wǒ bù xiǎng tīng!" | "I don't want to hear it!" she said angrily. |
| 孩子们高高兴兴地去看演唱会了。 | Háizimen gāogāoxìngxìng de qù kàn yǎnchànghuì le. | The children went off happily to the concert. |

**Watch out:** before the verb it is 地, not 得: ✗ 她生气得走了 should be 她生气地走了.

### G4 · 原来 as adverb and adjective
**Pattern:** `原来 + clause` (so it turns out) · `原来 + 的 + noun` (original, former) · `Subj + 原来 + V` (used to, at first)

原来 has two quite different jobs. At the start of a clause it announces a discovery: you have just found out the real reason or the true situation, like English "so that's it" or "it turns out". It often follows a sentence with 以为 that states what you wrongly believed. Used about time, 原来 means "original" or "former": before a noun it needs 的 (原来的手机, my old phone), and after the subject, before the verb, it says how things were at first, usually with a later change introduced by 后来 or 现在. Tell the two apart by position and meaning: realization comes first in its clause and needs no time contrast. Learners often leave out the 的 before a noun.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 原来他是你的高中同学！ | Yuánlái tā shì nǐ de gāozhōng tóngxué! | Oh, so he was your high school classmate! |
| 我以为她生气了，原来她只是太累了。 | Wǒ yǐwéi tā shēng qì le, yuánlái tā zhǐshì tài lèi le. | I thought she was angry, but it turns out she was just too tired. |
| 我原来的手机坏了，这是新买的。 | Wǒ yuánlái de shǒujī huài le, zhè shì xīn mǎi de. | My old phone broke; this one is new. |
| 他原来住在宿舍，后来搬到校外去了。 | Tā yuánlái zhù zài sùshè, hòulái bān dào xiàowài qu le. | He used to live in the dorm, but later he moved off campus. |

**Watch out:** before a noun 原来 needs 的: ✗ 我原来手机 should be 我原来的手机.

### G5 · Set phrases
**Pattern:** `V + 得 + 一干二净` · `Subj + (很 / 有点儿) + 丢三拉四 (的)`

Chinese is full of fixed expressions, very often four characters long, whose meaning cannot be worked out from the single characters. 一干二净 has nothing to do with the numbers one and two; it means "completely, without a trace". 丢三拉四 is not about losing three and leaving four; it describes someone who is always losing or forgetting things. Learn such phrases as whole units, together with the frame they live in, because each one only fits certain slots. 一干二净 behaves like an adjective and nearly always comes after 得, as the complement of verbs such as 忘, 吃 or 洗. 丢三拉四 is a predicate on its own, often with 的 at the end, and it cannot take an object or a complement.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 朋友们把我做的菜吃得一干二净。 | Péngyoumen bǎ wǒ zuò de cài chī de yì gān èr jìng. | My friends ate up every last bit of the food I made. |
| 这件事我已经忘得一干二净了。 | Zhè jiàn shì wǒ yǐjīng wàng de yì gān èr jìng le. | I've completely forgotten about that. |
| 我妹妹有点儿丢三拉四，上学常常忘了带东西。 | Wǒ mèimei yǒudiǎnr diū sān là sì, shàngxué chángcháng wàngle dài dōngxi. | My younger sister is a bit scatterbrained and often forgets to bring things to school. |

**Watch out:** 一干二净 needs 得 in front of it: ✗ 他忘了一干二净 should be 他忘得一干二净.

## Words & Phrases

### W1 · 到底 (what on earth, in the end)
到底 is an adverb that goes before the verb of a question and presses for a clear, final answer, often because the speaker is impatient or has been kept guessing. The question must be a real open question: a question word (什么, 为什么, 谁), an affirmative-negative form (去不去) or 还是. If the subject itself is the question word, 到底 goes in front of it (到底谁…). It also works inside an embedded question after 不知道 or 想知道, showing that you cannot settle the matter. Its tone is stronger and more emotional than a plain question.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你到底想吃什么？快告诉我。 | Nǐ dàodǐ xiǎng chī shénme? Kuài gàosu wǒ. | What on earth do you want to eat? Just tell me. |
| 到底谁把我的钥匙拿走了？ | Dàodǐ shéi bǎ wǒ de yàoshi názǒu le? | Who in the world took my keys? |
| 我也不知道他到底喜欢什么。 | Wǒ yě bù zhīdào tā dàodǐ xǐhuan shénme. | I really can't tell what he actually likes. |

**Watch out:** 到底 cannot go with 吗: ✗ 你到底去吗？ should be 你到底去不去？

### W2 · 根本 (at all, simply)
根本 is an adverb placed before the verb, and in this sense it almost always comes right before 不 or 没. It turns a plain negation into a total one: not at all, simply not, never in the first place. It often corrects what someone else assumes ("you think I know him, but I've never even met him"), which gives it a slightly heated tone in arguments. It is close to 一点儿也不, but 一点儿也不 grades a quality (一点儿也不难), while 根本 denies that the thing is true or exists at all.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我根本不认识他。 | Wǒ gēnběn bú rènshi tā. | I don't know him at all. |
| 他根本没听我说话。 | Tā gēnběn méi tīng wǒ shuōhuà. | He simply wasn't listening to me. |
| 你根本没告诉过我这件事！ | Nǐ gēnběn méi gàosuguo wǒ zhè jiàn shì! | You never told me about this at all! |

**Watch out:** 根本 goes before the negative word: ✗ 我不根本知道 should be 我根本不知道.

### W3 · 一干二净 (completely, thoroughly, spotless)
一干二净 is a set expression that works like an adjective. Its normal home is the complement slot after 得: 忘得一干二净 (forgot every bit of it), 吃得一干二净 (ate up every crumb), 洗得一干二净 (washed spotless). It is often combined with 把 to say what was finished off. The picture is always of nothing left over: no memory, no food, no dirt. It is colloquial and a little exaggerated, which makes it popular for complaining and joking.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我昨天学的生词，今天就忘得一干二净了。 | Wǒ zuótiān xué de shēngcí, jīntiān jiù wàng de yì gān èr jìng le. | The new words I learned yesterday are all gone from my head today. |
| 她把衣服洗得一干二净。 | Tā bǎ yīfu xǐ de yì gān èr jìng. | She washed the clothes spotlessly clean. |
| 马克一个人把冰箱里的东西吃得一干二净。 | Mǎkè yí ge rén bǎ bīngxiāng li de dōngxi chī de yì gān èr jìng. | Mark ate everything in the fridge all by himself. |

**Watch out:** it follows the verb with 得, not before it: ✗ 我一干二净地忘了 should be 我忘得一干二净.

### W4 · 实际上 (actually, in fact, in reality)
实际上 is a sentence adverb that introduces the real situation, usually after something that only seemed true: what people say, what everybody believes, what something looks like. It normally opens the second clause, and can also stand right after the subject. It is close to 其实 (qíshí); 实际上 sounds a little more formal and more like a statement of fact, while 其实 can also introduce a gentle admission. The first clause often contains 说, 以为 or 看起来, which sets up the contrast.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 他说他很忙，实际上他在家玩电脑。 | Tā shuō tā hěn máng, shíjìshang tā zài jiā wán diànnǎo. | He says he's busy, but actually he's at home playing on his computer. |
| 大家都以为他们是男女朋友，实际上他们只是好朋友。 | Dàjiā dōu yǐwéi tāmen shì nán nǚ péngyou, shíjìshang tāmen zhǐ shì hǎo péngyou. | Everyone thinks they're a couple, but in fact they're just good friends. |
| 这个问题看起来很难，实际上很简单。 | Zhège wèntí kàn qǐlai hěn nán, shíjìshang hěn jiǎndān. | This problem looks hard, but in reality it's simple. |

### W5 · 丢三拉四 (scatterbrained, forgetful)
丢三拉四 describes a person who is always losing things or leaving them behind. It works as a predicate, much like an adjective: it takes degree words such as 很, 有点儿 or 这么, and it is often followed by 的 at the end of the clause. What it cannot do is take an object or a complement, so the thing forgotten has to go in a separate clause, often a 把 sentence with 拉 or 忘. Compare 马虎: a 马虎 person is careless in how they do things, a 丢三拉四 person loses track of their belongings.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你怎么这么丢三拉四的？又把书包拉在车上了。 | Nǐ zěnme zhème diū sān là sì de? Yòu bǎ shūbāo là zài chē shang le. | How can you be so scatterbrained? You've left your backpack in the car again. |
| 我爸爸有点儿丢三拉四，常常忘了带钥匙。 | Wǒ bàba yǒudiǎnr diū sān là sì, chángcháng wàngle dài yàoshi. | My dad is a bit absent-minded and often forgets his keys. |
| 他以前很丢三拉四，现在好多了。 | Tā yǐqián hěn diū sān là sì, xiànzài hǎo duō le. | He used to be really forgetful, but he's much better now. |

**Watch out:** no object after it: ✗ 他丢三拉四他的书 should be 他很丢三拉四，常常把书拉在教室里.

### W6 · 一会儿…，一会儿…，一会儿又… (one minute …, the next minute …)
Repeating 一会儿 before each of two or three clauses shows that someone keeps switching from one thing to another, or that something keeps changing: one moment this, the next moment that. Each 一会儿 is followed directly by a verb phrase or an adjective, and the subject is said only once, at the start. With three parts, the last one is usually 一会儿又…, where 又 stresses "and now yet another change". The pattern often carries a note of complaint about someone being moody or indecisive.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 今天的天气一会儿晴，一会儿下雨，一会儿又刮风。 | Jīntiān de tiānqì yíhuìr qíng, yíhuìr xiàyǔ, yíhuìr yòu guāfēng. | The weather today is sunny one minute, raining the next, and windy after that. |
| 孩子一会儿要喝水，一会儿要吃东西，一会儿又要出去玩。 | Háizi yíhuìr yào hē shuǐ, yíhuìr yào chī dōngxi, yíhuìr yòu yào chūqu wán. | The child wants a drink one minute, something to eat the next, and then wants to go out and play. |
| 他一会儿说想学电脑，一会儿说想学音乐，到现在还没决定。 | Tā yíhuìr shuō xiǎng xué diànnǎo, yíhuìr shuō xiǎng xué yīnyuè, dào xiànzài hái méi juédìng. | One minute he says he wants to study computers, the next music; he still hasn't decided. |

**Watch out:** with a third part, 又 goes after 一会儿: ✗ 又一会儿要出去玩 should be 一会儿又要出去玩.

## Dialogues

### Dialogue 1 · Something on Anna's mind
*Setting: After soccer practice, 林月 finds 安娜, who has been unusually quiet all week, sitting alone on a bench and asks her what is wrong.*

| Speaker | Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 林月 | 安娜，你这几天怎么了？一会儿说累，一会儿说不想吃饭，一会儿又一个人坐着不说话。是不是有什么心事？ | Ānnà, nǐ zhè jǐ tiān zěnme le? Yíhuìr shuō lèi, yíhuìr shuō bù xiǎng chīfàn, yíhuìr yòu yí ge rén zuòzhe bù shuōhuà. Shì bu shì yǒu shénme xīnshì? | Anna, what's up with you these days? One minute you're tired, the next you don't want to eat, and then you sit by yourself without saying a word. Is something on your mind? |
| 安娜 | 没什么……好吧，我跟大卫吵架了。 | Méi shénme… Hǎo ba, wǒ gēn Dàwèi chǎo jià le. | It's nothing... Oh, all right. I had a fight with David. |
| 林月 | 吵架了？到底发生了什么事？ | Chǎo jià le? Dàodǐ fāshēngle shénme shì? | A fight? What on earth happened? |
| 安娜 | 上个星期六我们有一场很重要的足球比赛。他早就答应要来看我踢球，可是他忘得一干二净，跟朋友去听演唱会了。 | Shàng ge xīngqīliù wǒmen yǒu yì chǎng hěn zhòngyào de zúqiú bǐsài. Tā zǎo jiù dāying yào lái kàn wǒ tī qiú, kěshì tā wàng de yì gān èr jìng, gēn péngyou qù tīng yǎnchànghuì le. | Last Saturday we had a really important soccer game. He'd promised ages ago to come and watch me play, but he completely forgot and went to a concert with his friends. (重要 zhòngyào: important) |
| 林月 | 原来是这样！那你一定很生气吧。 | Yuánlái shì zhèyàng! Nà nǐ yídìng hěn shēng qì ba. | So that's what it was! You must have been really angry. |
| 安娜 | 当然生气！更叫我生气的是他的态度。我跟他提这件事，他说：“不就是一场比赛吗？” | Dāngrán shēng qì! Gèng jiào wǒ shēng qì de shì tā de tàidu. Wǒ gēn tā tí zhè jiàn shì, tā shuō: "Bú jiù shì yì chǎng bǐsài ma?" | Of course I was! What made me even angrier was his attitude. When I brought it up, he said, "It's only a game, isn't it?" |
| 林月 | 你们两个人在爱好上很不同：你是球迷，他是音乐迷。可是你们以前不是一直相处得很好吗？ | Nǐmen liǎng ge rén zài àihào shang hěn bù tóng: nǐ shì qiúmí, tā shì yīnyuèmí. Kěshì nǐmen yǐqián bú shì yìzhí xiāngchǔ de hěn hǎo ma? | The two of you are very different when it comes to interests: you're a sports fan and he's a music fan. ## Exercises

### E1 · Choose the right word {type=choice}
1. 这个周末你们___去不去看电影？ [根本 | 到底 | 原来] → 到底
2. 我___不知道今天有考试。 [到底 | 一会儿 | 根本] → 根本
3. 他跟同屋相处___很好。 [的 | 得 | 地] → 得
4. 听了我的话，他很高兴___答应了。 [地 | 的 | 得] → 地
5. ___生活上，他是一个很马虎的人。 [对 | 在 | 跟] → 在
6. 我把她的生日忘得___。 [丢三拉四 | 一干二净 | 不停] → 一干二净
7. 他___是我的同屋，现在搬走了。 [原来 | 根本 | 到底] → 原来
8. 他一会儿说要去，___又说不去了。 [根本 | 实际上 | 一会儿] → 一会儿

### E2 · Put the words in order {type=order}
1. 走来走去 / 他在 / 电影院 / 门口 → 他在电影院门口走来走去。
2. 一干二净 / 他 / 这件事 / 把 / 忘得 → 他把这件事忘得一干二净。
3. 没 / 提过这件事 / 他 / 根本 → 他根本没提过这件事。
4. 钥匙 / 这是 / 我 / 原来的 → 这是我原来的钥匙。
5. 生 / 你 / 我的气 / 别 → 你别生我的气。
6. 相处得 / 他 / 不太好 / 跟同屋 → 他跟同屋相处得不太好。
7. 陪她 / 叫我 / 去买东西 / 妈妈 → 妈妈叫我陪她去买东西。

### E3 · Translate into Chinese {type=translate}
1. What on earth are you two quarrelling about? → 你们两个人到底为什么吵架？
2. She's a very cheerful person and makes friends easily. → 她是一个很开朗的人，很容易交朋友。
3. My younger brother is often scatterbrained; he's left his keys in the classroom again. → 我弟弟常常丢三拉四的，又把钥匙拉在教室里了。
4. Oh, so you're a soccer fan too! → 原来你也是足球迷！
5. When it comes to interests, the two of them are very different. → 在爱好上，他们两个人很不同。
6. Maybe he forgot. Don't be angry with him. → 说不定他忘了，你别生他的气。
7. He kept apologizing to her, but she simply didn't want to listen. → 他不停地跟她道歉，可是她根本不想听。
8. I'll go to the concert with you. → 我陪你去听演唱会。

### E4 · Match the word {type=match}
1. 心事 → something that weighs on one's mind
2. 吵架 → to quarrel

4. 相处 → to get along
5. 背景 → background
6. 陪 → to accompany
7. 态度 → attitude
8. 钥匙 → key
