# Lesson 4 · 买东西 · Shopping

## Objectives
- After this lesson you can name everyday clothes and household items (T-shirts, jeans, sweaters, toothpaste, towels, laundry powder) and round off a list with …什么的.
- After this lesson you can explain what matters to you when you shop, such as brand, price, quality and comfort, with 标准 and 在乎.
- After this lesson you can understand and state discounts with 打折 (八折, 五折) and pay in cash or by card with tax added.
- After this lesson you can say that something holds no matter what with 无论…都…, and describe things with 是…的 (是纯棉的).
- After this lesson you can disagree tactfully with A是A，可是… and insist on something with 非…不可.

## Grammar

### G1 · 无论…，都… (no matter …)
**Pattern:** `无论 + question word / A 还是 B / V不V，(Subj +) 都 + …`

无论 says that whatever the circumstances are, the result stays the same: "no matter who, what, which, whether". The part after 无论 must leave the situation open. That means a question word (谁, 什么, 哪, 怎么样), a choice with 还是, or a positive-negative pair such as 贵不贵 or 去不去. The second half almost always has 都 (sometimes 也) after its subject and before the verb, summing up all the possibilities. The subject may stand before 无论 or inside the second clause. English speakers tend to put one fixed condition after 无论, because "no matter" feels like "even if", but 无论下雨 leaves nothing open: say 无论下不下雨. Leaving out 都 is the other common slip.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 无论去哪儿，他都骑自行车。 | Wúlùn qù nǎr, tā dōu qí zìxíngchē. | Wherever he goes, he rides his bike. |
| 无论贵不贵，安娜都只买纯棉的T恤衫。 | Wúlùn guì bu guì, Ānnà dōu zhǐ mǎi chúnmián de tīxùshān. | Expensive or not, Anna only buys pure cotton T-shirts. |
| 无论天气冷还是热，他都穿牛仔裤。 | Wúlùn tiānqì lěng háishi rè, tā dōu chuān niúzǎikù. | Whether it's cold or hot, he wears jeans. |
| 这件事无论谁问，你都别说。 | Zhè jiàn shì wúlùn shéi wèn, nǐ dōu bié shuō. | No matter who asks about this, don't tell them. |

**Watch out:** 无论 needs something open after it: ✗ 无论下雨，我都去 should be 无论下不下雨，我都去.

### G2 · Non-predicative adjectives
**Pattern:** `Noun + 是 + Adj + 的` · before a noun: `Adj + Noun`

Most adjectives can be the predicate on their own with 很 in front (这件衣服很贵). A small group cannot. Words like 男, 女, 纯棉 and 国际 (guójì, international) do not describe a degree of anything; they sort people and things into kinds. A person is male or female, a towel is pure cotton or it is not. So these words cannot take 很, 非常 or 太, and they cannot be compared. Their natural place is directly before a noun (纯棉毛巾, 女老师). When one has to serve as the predicate, wrap it in 是…的: 这条毛巾是纯棉的. The negative is 不是…的. Learners treat them like ordinary adjectives and say ✗ 很纯棉, or drop 是 or 的.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这件T恤衫是纯棉的，夏天穿很舒服。 | Zhè jiàn tīxùshān shì chúnmián de, xiàtiān chuān hěn shūfu. | This T-shirt is pure cotton, so it's comfortable to wear in summer. |
| 我们的中文老师是女的。 | Wǒmen de Zhōngwén lǎoshī shì nǚ de. | Our Chinese teacher is a woman. |
| 我想买纯棉毛巾，可是这种不是纯棉的。 | Wǒ xiǎng mǎi chúnmián máojīn, kěshì zhè zhǒng bú shì chúnmián de. | I want pure cotton towels, but this kind isn't pure cotton. |
| 刚才给你打电话的是男的还是女的？ | Gāngcái gěi nǐ dǎ diànhuà de shì nán de háishi nǚ de? | Was the person who just called you a man or a woman? |

**Watch out:** no degree word, but 是…的: ✗ 这条毛巾很纯棉 should be 这条毛巾是纯棉的.

### G3 · A是A，可是/但是… (granting a point, then disagreeing) revisited
**Pattern:** `A + 是 + A，可是 / 但是 + another point`

You met this in Level 2 with adjectives (好是好，可是…). Saying the same word on both sides of 是 grants the other person's point: "it's nice, I'll give you that". The 可是 or 但是 clause then brings in what they overlooked, very often a drawback. That makes it the polite Chinese way to disagree: you agree first, and only then add your "but". What is new at this level: A can also be a verb or a verb phrase (想去是想去, 喜欢是喜欢), and two-syllable adjectives work just as well (时髦是时髦, 方便是方便). The A before 是 stays bare, with no 很 and no 了. And the second clause must really turn the argument; if it simply agrees further, the pattern makes no sense.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这家商店的东西便宜是便宜，但是质量不太好。 | Zhè jiā shāngdiàn de dōngxi piányi shì piányi, dànshì zhìliàng bú tài hǎo. | The things in this shop are cheap, sure, but the quality isn't great. |
| 这件毛衣好看是好看，可是穿着不太舒服。 | Zhè jiàn máoyī hǎokàn shì hǎokàn, kěshì chuānzhe bú tài shūfu. | This sweater does look nice, but it isn't very comfortable to wear. |
| 我想去是想去，可是星期一有考试。 | Wǒ xiǎng qù shì xiǎng qù, kěshì xīngqīyī yǒu kǎoshì. | I do want to go, but I have an exam on Monday. |
| 用信用卡方便是方便，可是很容易花太多钱。 | Yòng xìnyòngkǎ fāngbiàn shì fāngbiàn, kěshì hěn róngyì huā tài duō qián. | Paying by credit card is convenient, but it's easy to spend too much. |

**Watch out:** the first A stays bare: ✗ 很好看是好看 should be 好看是好看.

## Words & Phrases

### W1 · …什么的 (...and so on)
…什么的 goes at the end of a list to show that the items named are only examples and there are more of the same kind, like "and so on" or "and stuff like that". It is casual and spoken; in writing you will see 等 (děng) instead. The items are separated by the list comma 、, and the whole list with 什么的 usually works as the object of the verb, though it can also be a topic summed up by 都. One item is enough (买点儿水果什么的), but two or three is typical. 什么的 always closes the list; it never comes before it.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我下午得去买卫生纸、牙膏什么的。 | Wǒ xiàwǔ děi qù mǎi wèishēngzhǐ, yágāo shénme de. | This afternoon I have to go and buy toilet paper, toothpaste and so on. |
| 周末我常常洗衣服、打扫房间什么的。 | Zhōumò wǒ chángcháng xǐ yīfu, dǎsǎo fángjiān shénme de. | At weekends I usually do laundry, clean my room, that kind of thing. |
| 宿舍里桌子、椅子、床什么的都有。 | Sùshè li zhuōzi, yǐzi, chuáng shénme de dōu yǒu. | The dorm room has a desk, chairs, a bed and all that. |

**Watch out:** ✗ 我买了什么的毛巾、牙膏 should be 我买了毛巾、牙膏什么的.

### W2 · 大小, 长短, 宽窄 (size, length, width)
Chinese can join two opposite one-syllable adjectives into a noun that names the measurement itself: 大 + 小 gives "size", 长 + 短 gives "length", and 宽 (kuān, wide) + 窄 (zhǎi, narrow) gives "width". These nouns usually open the sentence as a topic, followed by a comment such as 合适, 正好 or 怎么样, which makes them very handy when trying on clothes and shoes. Because they are nouns, 很 cannot go directly in front of them; it goes before the comment instead.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这双鞋大小正好，你就买吧。 | Zhè shuāng xié dàxiǎo zhènghǎo, nǐ jiù mǎi ba. | These shoes are exactly the right size, just buy them. |
| 这条牛仔裤长短合适，就是颜色我不太喜欢。 | Zhè tiáo niúzǎikù chángduǎn héshì, jiùshì yánsè wǒ bú tài xǐhuan. | These jeans are the right length; it's just the colour I don't really like. |
| 你再试试这双，看看宽窄怎么样。 | Nǐ zài shìshi zhè shuāng, kànkan kuānzhǎi zěnmeyàng. | Try this pair on too and see how the width is. |

**Watch out:** ✗ 这件T恤衫很大小 should be 这件T恤衫大小很合适.

### W3 · 打折 (to discount, to sell at a discount)
打折 is a verb-object phrase, so other words can come between its two parts: a number (打八折), 了 (打了五折) or a question (打不打折？打几折？). The number before 折 says how many tenths of the original price you pay: 八折 means you pay 80 percent, which is twenty percent off; 五折 is half price; 七五折 is 25 percent off. This is the opposite of English, which names the part taken off. 打折 can be the predicate (这件毛衣打折) or describe a noun with 的 (打折的衣服).

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这家商店的毛衣都在打折。 | Zhè jiā shāngdiàn de máoyī dōu zài dǎzhé. | All the sweaters in this shop are on sale. |
| 一百块的牛仔裤打八折，只要八十块。 | Yìbǎi kuài de niúzǎikù dǎ bā zhé, zhǐ yào bāshí kuài. | A hundred-dollar pair of jeans at twenty percent off costs only eighty. |
| 名牌衣服很少打折，打折的时候人特别多。 | Míngpái yīfu hěn shǎo dǎzhé, dǎzhé de shíhou rén tèbié duō. | Name-brand clothes are rarely discounted, and when they are, the shops are packed. |

**Watch out:** the number is what you pay: for twenty percent off, ✗ 打二折 should be 打八折.

### W4 · (要)不然 (otherwise)
(要)不然 is a conjunction that opens the second clause and says what will go wrong if the advice or plan in the first clause is not followed: "or else". The first clause is usually a suggestion or a necessity with 最好, 得, 应该 or 吧, and the second clause often contains 会 or 就. You met 要不然 in Level 2; 不然 on its own means the same and is a little shorter. It does the work of 要是不…就…, but much more compactly. It always follows the advice and never comes before it.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 快点儿去吧，要不然好的都卖完了。 | Kuài diǎnr qù ba, yàobùrán hǎo de dōu màiwán le. | Hurry up and go, or all the good ones will be sold out. |
| 天冷了，多穿一件毛衣，不然会感冒的。 | Tiān lěng le, duō chuān yí jiàn máoyī, bùrán huì gǎnmào de. | It's turned cold. Put on an extra sweater, or you'll catch a cold. |
| 我们得早点儿出发，要不然路上车会很多。 | Wǒmen děi zǎo diǎnr chūfā, yàobùrán lùshang chē huì hěn duō. | We need to set off early, otherwise there will be a lot of traffic. |

**Watch out:** ✗ 要不然会感冒，多穿点儿 should be 多穿点儿，要不然会感冒.

### W5 · 只好 (can only, have to)
只好 is an adverb that goes after the subject and right before the verb. It says that circumstances have ruled out the other options, so you settle for the one that is left, which is usually not what you wanted. The sentence normally names the obstacle first (sold out, raining, no bus) and then the fallback with 只好, often ending in 了. Compare 只能, which states a limit more neutrally, and 非…不可, which is about insisting. 只好 is about what you are left with, not what you want.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我要的颜色卖完了，只好买了一件黑的。 | Wǒ yào de yánsè màiwán le, zhǐhǎo mǎile yí jiàn hēi de. | The colour I wanted was sold out, so I had to buy a black one. |
| 下大雨了，我们只好在家看电影。 | Xià dà yǔ le, wǒmen zhǐhǎo zài jiā kàn diànyǐng. | It's pouring, so we'll just have to watch a film at home. |
| 公共汽车一直没来，他只好走路回宿舍。 | Gōnggòng qìchē yìzhí méi lái, tā zhǐhǎo zǒulù huí sùshè. | The bus never came, so he had no choice but to walk back to the dorm. |

**Watch out:** ✗ 只好我坐出租车 should be 我只好坐出租车.

### W6 · 非…不可 (have to, must)
非…不可 brackets a verb phrase: 非 goes in front of it and 不可 at the end. Literally "if not this, it won't do", it has two uses. With a person as the subject it shows insistence or determination: nothing else will do. With a situation as the subject it predicts a result that is bound to happen if things go on like this. Either way it is much stronger than 得 or 一定要. In casual speech 不可 is sometimes left off, but keep the full frame yourself.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这本书太有意思了，我今天非看完不可。 | Zhè běn shū tài yǒu yìsi le, wǒ jīntiān fēi kànwán bù kě. | This book is so good, I simply have to finish it today. |
| 你每天睡得这么晚，非生病不可。 | Nǐ měitiān shuì de zhème wǎn, fēi shēngbìng bù kě. | Going to bed this late every night, you're bound to get sick. |
| 我妹妹过生日非吃蛋糕不可。 | Wǒ mèimei guò shēngrì fēi chī dàngāo bù kě. | My little sister insists on cake for her birthday. |

**Watch out:** the verb goes inside the frame: ✗ 我非不可买这件 should be 我非买这件不可.

### W7 · 标准 (criterion, standard)
As a noun, 标准 is the yardstick you judge something by. It usually has a modifier with 的 in front (买衣服的标准, 我的标准), and it is described as 高 or 低, or as the same or different for different people. As an adjective it means "standard, correct", most often about pronunciation: 他的发音很标准, 他说得很标准. 不太标准 is a gentle way of saying that someone's accent is a bit off. Before a noun the adjective usually takes 的.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 他找女朋友的标准太高了。 | Tā zhǎo nǚpéngyou de biāozhǔn tài gāo le. | His standards for a girlfriend are far too high. |
| 她的中文发音很标准。 | Tā de Zhōngwén fāyīn hěn biāozhǔn. | Her Chinese pronunciation is very standard. |
| 买东西的时候，每个人的标准都不一样。 | Mǎi dōngxi de shíhou, měi ge rén de biāozhǔn dōu bù yíyàng. | When it comes to shopping, everyone has different criteria. |

**Watch out:** the modifier needs 的: ✗ 我的买衣服标准 should be 我买衣服的标准.

### W8 · 在乎 (to mind, to care)
在乎 is a verb for caring whether something matters to you. It is found mostly in the negative: 不在乎 and the stronger 一点儿都不在乎 say that something makes no difference to you at all. In positive sentences it usually takes a degree adverb (很在乎, 特别在乎). Its object can be a noun (在乎钱) or a whole clause (在乎别人怎么说). Do not mix it up with 关心, "to be concerned about someone's wellbeing": parents 关心 their children, and they may also 在乎 their grades.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我不在乎价钱，只在乎质量。 | Wǒ bú zàihu jiàqian, zhǐ zàihu zhìliàng. | I don't care about the price, only about the quality. |
| 他特别在乎别人怎么看他。 | Tā tèbié zàihu biérén zěnme kàn tā. | He cares a lot about what other people think of him. |
| 只要能吃饱，马克一点儿都不在乎饭馆漂亮不漂亮。 | Zhǐyào néng chībǎo, Mǎkè yìdiǎnr dōu bú zàihu fànguǎn piàoliang bu piàoliang. | As long as he can eat his fill, Mark doesn't care at all whether a restaurant is pretty. |

**Watch out:** 不在乎 is "not mind", not "dislike": ✗ 我不在乎这件毛衣 (meaning "I don't like it") should be 我不太喜欢这件毛衣.

## Dialogues

### Dialogue 1 · Making a list
*Setting: On Saturday morning 林月 and 安娜 drop by 马克's apartment and find he has run out of nearly everything.*

| Speaker | Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 安娜 | 马克，你这儿的卫生纸用完了，牙膏也快没了，你怎么还不去买？ | Mǎkè, nǐ zhèr de wèishēngzhǐ yòngwán le, yágāo yě kuài méi le, nǐ zěnme hái bú qù mǎi? | Mark, you're out of toilet paper and nearly out of toothpaste. Why haven't you been shopping? |
| 马克 | 哎呀，我这个星期太忙，又忘了！今天下午我们一起去购物吧，学校附近新开了一家很大的商店。 | Āiyā, wǒ zhège xīngqī tài máng, yòu wàng le! Jīntiān xiàwǔ wǒmen yìqǐ qù gòuwù ba, xuéxiào fùjìn xīn kāile yì jiā hěn dà de shāngdiàn. | Gosh, I've been so busy this week, I forgot again! Let's all go shopping this afternoon. A huge store has just opened near campus. |
| 林月 | 好啊。天快冷了，我正好需要买一件毛衣和一条牛仔裤。安娜，你呢？ | Hǎo a. Tiān kuài lěng le, wǒ zhènghǎo xūyào mǎi yí jiàn máoyī hé yì tiáo niúzǎikù. Ānnà, nǐ ne? | Sure. It's getting cold, and I happen to need a sweater and a pair of jeans. Anna, what about you? |
| 安娜 | 我踢足球穿的T恤衫都太小了，得买两件新的。 | Wǒ tī zúqiú chuān de tīxùshān dōu tài xiǎo le, děi mǎi liǎng jiàn xīn de. | The T-shirts I play soccer in are all too small. I need two new ones. |
| 马克 | 买运动穿的衣服，非买名牌不可。名牌的质量好，穿着也舒服。 | Mǎi yùndòng chuān de yīfu, fēi mǎi míngpái bù kě. Míngpái de zhìliàng hǎo, chuānzhe yě shūfu. | For sports clothes, it has to be a name brand. Name brands are better quality and more comfortable too. |
| 安娜 | 我一点儿都不在乎牌子。我买衣服的标准很简单：第一要舒服，第二价钱不能太贵。 | Wǒ yìdiǎnr dōu bú zàihu páizi. Wǒ mǎi yīfu de biāozhǔn hěn jiǎndān: dì yī yào shūfu, dì èr jiàqian bù néng tài guì. | I couldn't care less about brands. My rules for buying clothes are simple: first, comfortable; second, not too expensive. |
| 林月 | 名牌好是好，可是太贵了。我觉得无论买什么牌子的，都得自己先看看质量。 | Míngpái hǎo shì hǎo, kěshì tài guì le. Wǒ juéde wúlùn mǎi shénme páizi de, dōu děi zìjǐ xiān kànkan zhìliàng. | Name brands are good, sure, but they cost too much. Whatever brand you buy, I think you should check the quality yourself first. |

## Exercises

### E1 · Choose the right word {type=choice}
1. 无论天气好不好，安娜每天___去踢足球。 [才 | 再 | 都] → 都
2. 我买的这条毛巾是纯棉___。 [了 | 的 | 吗] → 的
3. 名牌好___好，可是价钱太贵了，我不想买。 [很 | 都 | 是] → 是
4. 我需要买卫生纸、牙膏、洗衣粉___。 [什么的 | 的时候 | 一样] → 什么的
5. 这件毛衣现在打八___，只要四十块。 [块 | 折 | 次] → 折
6. 那家饭馆没有位子了，我们___去别的地方吃。 [越来越 | 无论 | 只好] → 只好
7. 天气冷了，多穿一件衣服吧，___会感冒的。 [要不然 | 无论 | 所以] → 要不然
8. 他一点儿都不___别人穿什么牌子的衣服。 [同意 | 在乎 | 需要] → 在乎

### E2 · Put the words in order {type=order}
1. 先看 / 价钱 / 他都 / 无论买什么 → 无论买什么，他都先看价钱。
2. 是男的 / 售货员 / 还是女的 / 那个 → 那个售货员是男的还是女的？
3. 可是 / 贵是贵 / 质量很好 / 这件毛衣 → 这件毛衣贵是贵，可是质量很好。
4. 不可 / 我今天 / 把功课做完 / 非 → 我今天非把功课做完不可。
5. 七折 / 这家商店的 / 打 / 牛仔裤都 → 这家商店的牛仔裤都打七折。
6. 买了一件别的 / 卖完了 / 我只好 / 那件T恤衫 → 那件T恤衫卖完了，我只好买了一件别的。
7. 明天会很累 / 你最好 / 要不然 / 早点儿睡觉 → 你最好早点儿睡觉，要不然明天会很累。

### E3 · Translate into Chinese {type=translate}
1. No matter which sweater you wear, you look good. → 无论你穿哪件毛衣，都很好看。
2. Are these towels pure cotton? → 这些毛巾是纯棉的吗？
3. That brand is fashionable, all right, but the quality isn't very good. → 那个牌子时髦是时髦，可是质量不太好。
4. I need to buy toothpaste, towels, laundry powder and so on. → 我需要买牙膏、毛巾、洗衣粉什么的。
5. These jeans are thirty percent off. → 这条牛仔裤打七折。
6. The towels in that shop were sold out, so I had no choice but to go to another shop. → 那家商店的毛巾卖完了，我只好去别的商店买。
7. He doesn't care about brands at all; he has only one criterion: comfort. → 他一点儿都不在乎牌子，他只有一个标准：舒服。

### E4 · Match the word {type=match}
1. 像 → to resemble, to be like
2. 牛仔裤 → jeans
3. 卫生纸 → toilet paper
4. 洗衣粉 → laundry powder
5. 时髦 → fashionable, stylish
6. 质量 → quality
7. 现金 → cash
8. 税 → tax
