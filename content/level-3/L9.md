# Lesson 9 · 教育 · Education

## Objectives
- After this lesson you can talk about the lessons and activities that filled your childhood, and say whether you enjoyed them or complained about them.
- After this lesson you can stress that an age, an amount or a time is small or early with 才 before a number.
- After this lesson you can judge a whole action with an adjective at the end (孩子学什么好？) and correct a wrong idea with 不是…，而是….
- After this lesson you can agree or disagree and present your view on children's education with 认为, 看法, 完全同意 and 最好.
- After this lesson you can talk about parents' hopes for their children (望子成龙, 望女成凤) and tell 幸福, 快乐 and 高兴 apart.

## Grammar

### G1 · The adverb 才 before numbers (only, just)
**Pattern:** `Subj + 才 + number + MW (+ Noun)` · with a verb: `Subj + 才 + V + 了 + number + MW (+ Noun)`

When 才 stands right before a number, it means "only" or "just": the speaker finds the age young, the amount small, the price low or the clock time early. It works with ages (才五岁), sums of money (才十块钱), durations (才睡了四个小时) and clock times (现在才六点). You already know 才 after a time phrase and before a verb, where it means "not until" and shows that something happened late (他十点才来). Both uses share one idea: reality falls short of what the speaker expected. Do not confuse it with 就 after a time phrase, which stresses that something happened early or quickly. Since 才 already marks the amount as small, it does not take a sentence-final 了, although 了 right after a verb is fine.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 他们从墨西哥移民到美国的时候，他才三岁。 | Tāmen cóng Mòxīgē yímín dào Měiguó de shíhou, tā cái sān suì. | He was only three when his family immigrated from Mexico to the US. |
| 这本书才十块钱，太便宜了！ | Zhè běn shū cái shí kuài qián, tài piányi le! | This book is only ten dollars, that's really cheap! |
| 昨天晚上我才睡了四个小时。 | Zuótiān wǎnshang wǒ cái shuìle sì ge xiǎoshí. | I only slept four hours last night. |
| 现在才六点，你怎么就起床了？ | Xiànzài cái liù diǎn, nǐ zěnme jiù qǐchuáng le? | It's only six o'clock. Why are you up already? |

**Watch out:** no sentence-final 了 after 才 + number: ✗ 他才十岁了 should be 他才十岁.

### G2 · Adjectives as predicates of a whole action
**Pattern:** `(Noun +) Verb phrase + Adj` · e.g. `孩子 + 学什么 + 好？`

In Chinese a whole action can be the subject of a sentence, and an adjective at the end then passes judgement on it. The subject is a noun plus a verb phrase (孩子学钢琴) or just a verb phrase (让孩子自己选择), and the adjective, such as 好, 合适, 重要, 难 or 容易, comes last, usually with 很, 太…了 or in a question. Question words fit inside the action: 学什么好？ "What would be good to learn?" 几点去合适？ "What time would be right to go?" English needs a dummy "it is … to …", which tempts learners to start with 是, to put the adjective first, or to use 是 instead of 很 before the adjective. Let the action come first and the judgement last.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 孩子几岁开始学钢琴合适？ | Háizi jǐ suì kāishǐ xué gāngqín héshì? | At what age is it right for a child to start learning the piano? |
| 小学生每天做三个小时功课，太多了。 | Xiǎoxuéshēng měitiān zuò sān ge xiǎoshí gōngkè, tài duō le. | Three hours of homework a day is too much for elementary school pupils. |
| 尊重孩子的选择很重要。 | Zūnzhòng háizi de xuǎnzé hěn zhòngyào. | It's important to respect children's choices. |
| 这个周末我们去哪儿玩儿好？ | Zhège zhōumò wǒmen qù nǎr wánr hǎo? | Where should we go this weekend? |

**Watch out:** the adjective takes 很, not 是: ✗ 尊重孩子的选择是重要 should be 尊重孩子的选择很重要.

### G3 · 不是X，而是Y (not X, but Y)
**Pattern:** `不是 + X，而是 + Y`

This pattern rejects X and puts Y firmly in its place. You use it to correct a wrong assumption, either your listener's or one you expect them to have: "it isn't X, it's actually Y". X and Y are normally parallel in form: two nouns, two verb phrases, two reasons with 因为, or two clauses. 而是 is more formal and more emphatic than simply saying 不是 X and then adding a second sentence, and it is common in explanations and arguments. Do not use 但是 in the second half, and do not confuse the pattern with 不是…就是…, which means "either … or". The subject usually comes before 不是, unless the two halves have different subjects.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我学中文不是因为爸爸妈妈让我学，而是因为我自己喜欢。 | Wǒ xué Zhōngwén bú shì yīnwèi bàba māma ràng wǒ xué, érshì yīnwèi wǒ zìjǐ xǐhuan. | I'm learning Chinese not because my parents make me, but because I like it myself. |
| 我不是反对你学画画儿，而是觉得你的课太多了。 | Wǒ bú shì fǎnduì nǐ xué huàhuàr, érshì juéde nǐ de kè tài duō le. | It's not that I'm against you learning to paint; I just think you have too many classes. |
| 上学不是为了考试，而是为了学知识。 | Shàngxué bú shì wèile kǎoshì, érshì wèile xué zhīshi. | You go to school not to take exams but to gain knowledge. |
| 我嫂子学的不是电脑，而是设计。 | Wǒ sǎozi xué de bú shì diànnǎo, érshì shèjì. | My sister-in-law didn't study computers; she studied design. |

**Watch out:** the second half needs 而是, not 但是: ✗ 他不是医生，但是老师 should be 他不是医生，而是老师.

## Words & Phrases

### W1 · 一直 (all along, continuously)
一直 is an adverb. It goes after the subject and before the verb, or before a negative such as 不 or 没, and says that a state or an action has gone on without a break or a change: 我一直想去中国, "I've always wanted to go to China". It often comes with a starting point (从小, 从去年) or a time span (这几天). With action verbs it likes 在 or 着 to show the action is ongoing. In directions it means "straight": 一直往前走. Compare 常常, which describes something repeated now and then; 一直 means it never stopped.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 她从小一直想做医生。 | Tā cóng xiǎo yìzhí xiǎng zuò yīshēng. | She has wanted to be a doctor ever since she was little. |
| 这几天一直下雨，我们哪儿都没去。 | Zhè jǐ tiān yìzhí xiàyǔ, wǒmen nǎr dōu méi qù. | It's been raining nonstop these last few days, so we haven't gone anywhere. |
| 你一直往前走，银行就在左边。 | Nǐ yìzhí wǎng qián zǒu, yínháng jiù zài zuǒbian. | Go straight ahead, and the bank is on the left. |

**Watch out:** 一直 goes before the verb, never after it: ✗ 我等了你一直 should be 我一直在等你.

### W2 · 幸福 / 快乐 / 高兴 (happy: three near-synonyms)
All three translate as "happy", but they cover different kinds of happiness. 幸福 is deep and lasting contentment with one's life: a good marriage, a loving family, a life without worries (幸福的家, 生活很幸福). It is also a noun, "happiness". 快乐 is cheerful joy that comes from having what you need; it can last, and it is the word in greetings: 生日快乐, 新年快乐, 快乐的童年. 高兴 is the gladness of a moment, caused by good news or by seeing someone; it shows on your face, passes, and easily takes a complement with 得 (高兴得跳起来). Only 高兴 is used for "glad to …" (很高兴认识你).

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我爷爷奶奶结婚五十年了，生活一直很幸福。 | Wǒ yéye nǎinai jiéhūn wǔshí nián le, shēnghuó yìzhí hěn xìngfú. | My grandparents have been married fifty years, and they've always had a happy life together. |
| 祝你们新年快乐！ | Zhù nǐmen xīnnián kuàilè! | Happy New Year to you all! |
| 我希望我的孩子有一个快乐的童年。 | Wǒ xīwàng wǒ de háizi yǒu yí ge kuàilè de tóngnián. | I hope my children have a happy childhood. |
| 听说你要来看我，我高兴极了。 | Tīngshuō nǐ yào lái kàn wǒ, wǒ gāoxìng jí le. | I was thrilled to hear you're coming to see me. |

**Watch out:** greetings use 快乐: ✗ 祝你生日高兴 should be 祝你生日快乐.

### W3 · 厉害 (terrible, formidable)
厉害 is an adjective with two faces. As a predicate it describes a person who is strict or fierce (我们老师很厉害), or, very often in speech, someone who is impressively capable: 你真厉害！ "You're amazing!" Context tells you which. After a verb or adjective plus 得 it marks an extreme degree, mostly of something unpleasant: pain, rain, arguing, being opposed (疼得厉害, 下得很厉害). In that position it is close to 极了 or 死了, but it is less casual than 死了 and fits a calm report better.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你画画儿画得这么好，真厉害！ | Nǐ huàhuàr huà de zhème hǎo, zhēn lìhai! | You paint so well, that's impressive! |
| 我们的中文老师很厉害，大家都有点儿怕她。 | Wǒmen de Zhōngwén lǎoshī hěn lìhai, dàjiā dōu yǒudiǎnr pà tā. | Our Chinese teacher is very strict; we're all a bit scared of her. |
| 外边的雨下得很厉害，你等一会儿再走吧。 | Wàibian de yǔ xià de hěn lìhai, nǐ děng yíhuìr zài zǒu ba. | It's pouring outside. Wait a while before you go. |

**Watch out:** for a degree, 厉害 follows 得 after the verb: ✗ 我厉害头疼 should be 我头疼得厉害.

### W4 · 最好 (had better, it's best)
最好 is an adverb used to give advice. It usually goes after the subject and before the verb phrase (你最好早点儿走); in speech it can also come before the subject (最好你早点儿走). To advise against something, add 别 or 不要: 你最好别去. It often appears in the frame 要是你想…，最好…, "if you want to …, the best thing is to …". 最好 is softer than a command such as 你得… or 你一定要…, because it only suggests the best choice. As an ordinary adjective phrase, 最好的 still means "the best" (最好的办法).

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 学钢琴最好从小开始。 | Xué gāngqín zuìhǎo cóng xiǎo kāishǐ. | It's best to start learning the piano as a child. |
| 明天的考试很重要，你最好别迟到。 | Míngtiān de kǎoshì hěn zhòngyào, nǐ zuìhǎo bié chídào. | Tomorrow's exam is important, so you'd better not be late. |
| 要是你想学好中文，最好去中国住一年。 | Yàoshi nǐ xiǎng xuéhǎo Zhōngwén, zuìhǎo qù Zhōngguó zhù yì nián. | If you want to learn Chinese well, it's best to go and live in China for a year. |

**Watch out:** advice against something uses 别 or 不要, not 没: ✗ 你最好没去 should be 你最好别去.

## Dialogues

### Dialogue 1 · The piano in the lounge

## Exercises

### E1 · Choose the right word {type=choice}
1. 我妹妹___六岁，已经认识很多汉字了。 [才 | 再 | 又] → 才
2. 我找你不是为了借钱，___为了跟你说一件事。 [但是 | 还是 | 而是] → 而是
3. 祝你生日___！ [厉害 | 快乐 | 高兴] → 快乐
4. 我有一个很___的家。 [厉害 | 高兴 | 幸福] → 幸福
5. 看见老朋友来了，他___得说不出话来。 [高兴 | 完全 | 一直] → 高兴
6. 楼上的音乐太吵了，我头疼得___。 [幸福 | 厉害 | 一直] → 厉害
7. 外边在下雨，你___别出去了。 [一直 | 完全 | 最好] → 最好
8. 从九月到现在，他___住在学校的宿舍里。 [一直 | 最好 | 才] → 一直

### E2 · Put the words in order {type=order}
1. 七岁 / 我侄女 / 就会 / 才 / 画画儿了 → 我侄女才七岁就会画画儿了。
2. 而是设计 / 他的 / 不是电脑 / 专业 → 他的专业不是电脑，而是设计。
3. 很重要 / 自己 / 让孩子 / 选择 → 让孩子自己选择很重要。
4. 抱怨 / 你最好 / 了 / 别 → 你最好别抱怨了。
5. 墨西哥 / 一直 / 我爸爸妈妈 / 住在 → 我爸爸妈妈一直住在墨西哥。
6. 很厉害 / 肚子 / 我 / 疼得 → 我肚子疼得很厉害。
7. 好 / 学什么 / 孩子 / 让 → 让孩子学什么好？

### E3 · Translate into Chinese {type=translate}
1. My niece is only six, but she already loves painting. → 我侄女才六岁，已经很喜欢画画儿了。
2. She isn't complaining; she's just too tired. → 她不是在抱怨，而是太累了。
3. Do you think it's good for children to take so many classes? → 你觉得孩子上这么多课好吗？
4. Parents should respect their children's choices. → 爸爸妈妈应该尊重孩子的选择。
5. My sister-in-law wants to go to China to study. → 我嫂子想去中国留学。
6. I completely agree with your point of view. → 我完全同意你的看法。
7. You'd better not arrange too many classes for the child. → 你最好别给孩子安排太多课。
