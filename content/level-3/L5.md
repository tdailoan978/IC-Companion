# Lesson 5 · 选课 · Choosing Classes

## Objectives
- After this lesson you can say which courses you are taking, what your major might be and what you plan to do after graduating.
- After this lesson you can say how something looks from one person's point of view with 对…来说, and add a further point with 另外.
- After this lesson you can tell 再 and 还 apart, including where a modal verb goes, and lay out a limited set of choices with 要么…，要么….
- After this lesson you can use resultative complements such as 完, 好, 懂, 累 and 干净 to say how an action turned out.
- After this lesson you can complain with 受不了, add a small reservation with 只是 or 就是, say what a job deals with using 跟…打交道, and link a plan to its result with 这样.

## Grammar

### G1 · 对…来说 (for someone, as far as someone is concerned)
**Pattern:** `对 + person + 来说，+ statement / opinion`

对…来说 tells the listener whose point of view a statement holds for: "for X", "as far as X is concerned". It usually opens the sentence, and in speech the person inside it carries the stress. What follows is true or important for that person, not necessarily for everyone: 对我来说，化学不难 leaves room for others to find chemistry very hard. Put 觉得 in front and you can judge on someone else's behalf (我觉得对你来说…), which may differ from what that person thinks. Do not mix it up with plain 对 + noun before a verb or adjective, which marks the target of a feeling or attitude (对历史有兴趣, 他对我很好); that 对 has no 来说. The usual slips are dropping 来说, or translating "for me" as 给我.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 对马克来说，写汉字比说中文难得多。 | Duì Mǎkè lái shuō, xiě Hànzì bǐ shuō Zhōngwén nán de duō. | For Mark, writing characters is much harder than speaking Chinese. |
| 对新生来说，选课是一件很麻烦的事。 | Duì xīnshēng lái shuō, xuǎnkè shì yí jiàn hěn máfan de shì. | For freshmen, choosing classes is a real hassle. |
| 我觉得对你来说，一个学期上五门课太多了。 | Wǒ juéde duì nǐ lái shuō, yí ge xuéqī shàng wǔ mén kè tài duō le. | I think five courses in one semester is too many for you. |
| 这个学位对我们家来说非常重要。 | Zhège xuéwèi duì wǒmen jiā lái shuō fēicháng zhòngyào. | This degree matters a great deal to our family. |

**Watch out:** 来说 cannot be left out, and "for me" is not 给我: ✗ 对我，历史课很有意思 or ✗ 给我来说… should be 对我来说，历史课很有意思.

### G2 · Resultative complements (III)
**Pattern:** `Subject + Verb + result (+ Object)` · negative: `没 + Verb + result`

You have met verbs like 写完, 看懂 and 听见 before: when an action produces a result, Chinese attaches that result directly to the verb. What is new here is a way to sort the results by what they describe. Some describe the action itself: 完 (finished) and 好 (done properly, ready for what comes next). Some describe the person doing it: 听懂 (the listener understands), 走累 (the walker is tired). Some describe the object: 洗干净 (the clothes end up clean), 拿走 (the thing is gone); this third type often comes with 把. Which verb takes which complement is a matter of usage, so learn each pair as one word. The negative is 没, not 不. The commonest mistake is to use the bare verb and leave the result out.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这篇文章我昨天晚上才写完。 | Zhè piān wénzhāng wǒ zuótiān wǎnshang cái xiěwán. | I only finished this essay last night. |
| 教授说得太快，我没听懂。 | Jiàoshòu shuō de tài kuài, wǒ méi tīngdǒng. | The professor spoke too fast and I didn't understand. |
| 打了一下午球，我们都打累了。 | Dǎle yí xiàwǔ qiú, wǒmen dōu dǎlèi le. | After playing ball all afternoon, we were all worn out. |
| 吃完饭，他把碗都洗干净了。 | Chīwán fàn, tā bǎ wǎn dōu xǐ gānjìng le. | After the meal he washed all the bowls clean. |

**Watch out:** do not drop the result: ✗ 我昨天把那篇文章写 should be 我昨天把那篇文章写完了.

### G3 · 另外 (the other; in addition; besides)
**Pattern:** `另外 + number / 这 / 那 (+ MW) + Noun` · `另外 + (再 / 还) + Verb phrase` · `另外，+ sentence`

另外 has three jobs. Before a number or 这/那 with a measure word and noun, it means "the other(s)": out of a group you have already mentioned, it picks out the rest (一门是中文，另外三门…). Before a verb phrase it is an adverb, "in addition, separately", and it likes to team up with 再 or 还 (另外再找一本). At the start of a sentence, followed by a pause, it is a connective that adds one more separate point, much like "also" or "on top of that". Unlike 别的 and 其他, which can go straight before a noun (其他学生, 别的书), 另外 in the "the other" use normally needs a number or 这/那 with a measure word (另外两个学生). A common error is to put it straight before a bare noun.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我选了四门课，一门是中文，另外三门是历史、哲学和文学。 | Wǒ xuǎnle sì mén kè, yì mén shì Zhōngwén, lìngwài sān mén shì lìshǐ, zhéxué hé wénxué. | I've chosen four courses: one is Chinese, and the other three are history, philosophy and literature. |
| 这本书太难了，我想另外再找一本。 | Zhè běn shū tài nán le, wǒ xiǎng lìngwài zài zhǎo yì běn. | This book is too hard; I want to find a different one. |
| 申请研究生要写一篇文章，另外还得请两位教授写信。 | Shēnqǐng yánjiūshēng yào xiě yì piān wénzhāng, lìngwài hái děi qǐng liǎng wèi jiàoshòu xiě xìn. | To apply to grad school you have to write an essay, and you also need two professors to write letters. |
| 这个周末我得写完一篇文章。另外，星期天我还得帮朋友搬家。 | Zhège zhōumò wǒ děi xiěwán yì piān wénzhāng. Lìngwài, xīngqītiān wǒ hái děi bāng péngyou bānjiā. | This weekend I have to finish an essay. On top of that, on Sunday I have to help a friend move. |

**Watch out:** in the "the other" use 另外 needs a number or 这/那: ✗ 另外学生都去了 should be 另外两个学生都去了 (or 其他学生都去了).

### G4 · Comparing 再 and 还
**Pattern:** `(Modal verb +) 再 + Verb (+ quantity)` · `还 + (Modal verb +) Verb`

Both words can come out in English as "again", "more" or "also", but they do different work. 再 is about doing an action once more or carrying it on, and in that sense it points to the future: 再上一门课, 再来一次. 还 is about adding something, widening the scope ("also, in addition, still"), and it works for the past as well as the future: 上个学期他还上了化学. The two also differ with modal verbs such as 想, 要, 能, 会: the modal comes before 再 but after 还 (想再去, but 还想去). The two can even be combined: 还要再上一门 means "will still have to take one more". Putting the modal on the wrong side is the classic error.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这门课太难了，我下个学期不想再上了。 | Zhè mén kè tài nán le, wǒ xià ge xuéqī bù xiǎng zài shàng le. | This course is too hard; I don't want to take it again next semester. |
| 除了中文以外，我还想选一门文学课。 | Chúle Zhōngwén yǐwài, wǒ hái xiǎng xuǎn yì mén wénxué kè. | Besides Chinese, I'd also like to take a literature course. |
| 你明天能再来一次吗？ | Nǐ míngtiān néng zài lái yí cì ma? | Could you come once more tomorrow? |
| 上个学期他上了化学，还上了世界历史。 | Shàng ge xuéqī tā shàngle huàxué, hái shàngle shìjiè lìshǐ. | Last semester he took chemistry, and world history as well. |

**Watch out:** the modal goes before 再 but after 还: ✗ 我再想去一次 should be 我想再去一次, and ✗ 我想还选一门 should be 我还想选一门.

### G5 · 要么…，要么… (either… or…)
**Pattern:** `要么 + option A，要么 + option B (，要么 + option C)`

要么…，要么… lays out the possibilities from which one will, or must, be chosen: "either this or that". Each 要么 is followed by a verb phrase or a whole clause, and you can add a third 要么 if there are more options. The pattern carries a sense that the choice is limited to what you have listed and nothing else is on the table, which makes it firmer than 或者. It is used in statements and in answers that set out choices, never to ask a question; for an "or" question you need 还是. If the two options have different subjects, 要么 goes before each subject. A typical mistake is to turn the pattern into a question.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 周末你要找我，要么去图书馆，要么去我宿舍。 | Zhōumò nǐ yào zhǎo wǒ, yàome qù túshūguǎn, yàome qù wǒ sùshè. | If you're looking for me at the weekend, it's either the library or my dorm. |
| 晚饭我要么自己做，要么去学校餐厅吃。 | Wǎnfàn wǒ yàome zìjǐ zuò, yàome qù xuéxiào cāntīng chī. | For dinner I either cook myself or eat in the campus dining hall. |
| 你要么少选一门课，要么周末少玩一点儿。 | Nǐ yàome shǎo xuǎn yì mén kè, yàome zhōumò shǎo wán yìdiǎnr. | Either you take one course fewer, or you play a bit less at the weekend. |
| 要么学经济，要么学金融，他爸爸说别的专业都不行。 | Yàome xué jīngjì, yàome xué jīnróng, tā bàba shuō biéde zhuānyè dōu bù xíng. | It's either economics or finance; his father says no other major will do. |

**Watch out:** 要么 cannot ask a question: ✗ 你要么学历史，要么学文学？ should be 你学历史还是学文学？

## Words & Phrases

### W1 · 只是 / 就是 (it's just that)
只是 and 就是 both bring in a mild "but", softer than 但是 or 可是 and close to 不过. They stand at the head of the last clause. The clause before them is mostly positive, and the 只是/就是 clause adds the one flaw that keeps the thing from being perfect: the apartment is great, it's just a bit far. Because of that, they are not used for a strong, general contrast between two equal ideas. 就是 sounds a little more colloquial than 只是. Both are handy for criticising something politely, since the praise comes first.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这门课很有意思，只是要写的文章太多了。 | Zhè mén kè hěn yǒu yìsi, zhǐshì yào xiě de wénzhāng tài duō le. | This course is really interesting; it's just that there are too many essays to write. |
| 那个公寓又大又安静，就是离学校远了点儿。 | Nàge gōngyù yòu dà yòu ānjìng, jiùshì lí xuéxiào yuǎnle diǎnr. | That apartment is big and quiet; it's just a bit far from campus. |
| 我的电脑很好用，只是有点儿旧了。 | Wǒ de diànnǎo hěn hǎoyòng, zhǐshì yǒudiǎnr jiù le. | My computer works well; it's just getting a bit old. |

**Watch out:** the reservation comes second: ✗ 只是太贵了，这件衣服很漂亮 should be 这件衣服很漂亮，只是太贵了.

### W2 · 受不了 (unable to bear)
受不了 is a negative potential complement: 受 means "to bear", and 不了 says it cannot be done. It works as a predicate, "can't stand it", about heat, noise, a person's habits or too much work. The cause usually comes in the clause before it, though it can also take an object directly (我受不了他). It is often strengthened with 真 or 真的, and 快受不了了 means "I'm about to reach my limit". The positive form 受得了 appears mostly in questions (你受得了吗？). It is everyday spoken Chinese.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这儿的夏天太热了，我真受不了。 | Zhèr de xiàtiān tài rè le, wǒ zhēn shòu bu liǎo. | Summers here are so hot, I really can't stand it. |
| 一个学期写八篇文章，谁都受不了。 | Yí ge xuéqī xiě bā piān wénzhāng, shéi dōu shòu bu liǎo. | Eight essays in one semester: nobody could take that. |
| 楼下的人每天晚上听音乐听到两点，我快受不了了。 | Lóuxià de rén měitiān wǎnshang tīng yīnyuè tīng dào liǎng diǎn, wǒ kuài shòu bu liǎo le. | The people downstairs play music until two every night; I'm about at the end of my rope. |

**Watch out:** "can't bear" is not 不能受 or 不受: ✗ 这么热，我不能受 should be 这么热，我受不了.

### W3 · 跟…打交道 (to deal with…)
打交道 is a verb-object phrase meaning "to have dealings with". The people or things you deal with are introduced by 跟 in front of it; they can never follow 打交道 as an object. It typically describes what a job or a situation puts you in contact with day after day, so it often comes with 整天, 天天 or 每天. Both people (跟病人, 跟客人) and things (跟数字, 跟电脑) can be the partner. To say you are good at dealing with people, use 很会跟人打交道.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 学金融的人整天跟数字打交道。 | Xué jīnróng de rén zhěngtiān gēn shùzì dǎ jiāodào. | People who study finance deal with numbers all day long. |
| 我喜欢跟人打交道，不喜欢一个人整天坐在电脑前面。 | Wǒ xǐhuan gēn rén dǎ jiāodào, bù xǐhuan yí ge rén zhěngtiān zuò zài diànnǎo qiánmiàn. | I like dealing with people; I don't like sitting alone in front of a computer all day. |
| 当医生每天都得跟病人打交道。 | Dāng yīshēng měitiān dōu děi gēn bìngrén dǎ jiāodào. | As a doctor you have to deal with patients every day. |

**Watch out:** the partner goes before the verb with 跟: ✗ 他每天打交道数字 should be 他每天跟数字打交道.

### W4 · 这样 (in this way)
Used as a connective, 这样 opens the second clause and points back to what was just said: "if you do that", "that way". What follows is the result or the purpose of that step. It very often pairs with 就 for an easy consequence (这样就…) or with 才 when the step is the necessary condition (这样才能…). The first clause usually describes a plan or a decision, and the 这样 clause explains why it is a good idea. It can also be set off with a comma: 这样，…. Compare 所以, which simply gives a consequence and does not suggest that the first clause was a chosen step.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你下个学期少选一门课，这样就轻松多了。 | Nǐ xià ge xuéqī shǎo xuǎn yì mén kè, zhèyàng jiù qīngsōng duō le. | Take one course fewer next semester; that way things will be a lot easier. |
| 我们三个人一起住，这样每个人的房租就少多了。 | Wǒmen sān ge rén yìqǐ zhù, zhèyàng měi ge rén de fángzū jiù shǎo duō le. | The three of us live together; that way everyone pays a lot less rent. |
| 你每天听半个小时中文，这样你的中文才能越来越好。 | Nǐ měitiān tīng bàn ge xiǎoshí Zhōngwén, zhèyàng nǐ de Zhōngwén cái néng yuè lái yuè hǎo. | Listen to Chinese for half an hour every day; only that way will your Chinese keep getting better. |

## Dialogues

### Dialogue 1 · Four courses and too many essays
*Setting: Halfway through the semester, 安娜 runs into 林月 and 马克 in the library, where 马克 is struggling with yet another essay.*

## Exercises

### E1 · Choose the right word {type=choice}
1. ___我来说，选课是一件很麻烦的事。 [给 | 对 | 跟] → 对
2. 这个电影太好看了，我想___看一次。 [还 | 又 | 再] → 再
3. 我这个学期选了中文课，___还选了两门历史课。 [另外 | 别的 | 其他] → 另外
4. 我___去工学院，要么去管理学院，还没决定。 [还是 | 要么 | 或者] → 要么
5. 这家饭馆的菜很好吃，___贵了一点儿。 [就是 | 要么 | 对] → 就是
6. 天天写文章，我真的快___了。 [轻松 | 受不了 | 解决] → 受不了
7. 服务员每天都要___很多人打交道。 [对 | 给 | 跟] → 跟
8. 老师说得太快，我没听___。 [累 | 走 | 懂] → 懂

### E2 · Put the words in order {type=order}
1. 把 / 洗干净了 / 衣服 / 他 → 他把衣服洗干净了。
2. 很会 / 跟人 / 打交道 / 我姐姐 → 我姐姐很会跟人打交道。
3. 再 / 想 / 我 / 上一门化学课 → 我想再上一门化学课。
4. 就是 / 很有意思 / 文章太多 / 这门课 → 这门课很有意思，就是文章太多。
5. 省下来 / 把钱 / 我想 / 买电脑 → 我想把钱省下来买电脑。
6. 研究生 / 他打算 / 管理学院的 / 申请 → 他打算申请管理学院的研究生。
7. 快 / 我 / 了 / 受不了 → 我快受不了了。

### E3 · Translate into Chinese {type=translate}
1. For me, history is more interesting than economics. → 对我来说，历史比经济有意思。
2. I want to take one more Chinese course next semester. → 下个学期我想再上一门中文课。
3. After graduating, I'll either apply to the school of engineering or look for a job. → 毕业以后，我要么申请工学院，要么找工作。
4. I still haven't decided what to major in. → 我还没决定学什么专业。
5. The professor is very nice; it's just that he gives too much homework. → 那位教授人很好，就是给的功课太多了。
6. My dad deals with numbers every day. → 我爸爸每天跟数字打交道。
7. I'll finish the essay today; that way I can relax a bit at the weekend. → 我今天把文章写完，这样周末就可以轻松一点儿了。

### E4 · Match the word {type=match}
1. 学位 → academic degree
2. 化学 → chemistry
3. 学分 → academic credit
4. 教授 → professor
5. 哲学 → philosophy
6. 申请 → to apply (to a school or job)
7. 碰见 → to run into (a person by chance)
8. 挣钱 → to earn money
