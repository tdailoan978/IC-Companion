# Lesson 3 · 在饭馆儿 · At a Restaurant

## Objectives
- After this lesson you can order dishes and drinks in a restaurant and ask the waiter for things politely with 麻烦你.
- After this lesson you can describe how food tastes and say which flavours you like, using 又…又… and 特别是.
- After this lesson you can explain how you want your food cooked: not too salty, less oil, something light.
- After this lesson you can name four regional styles of Chinese cooking and give an "it depends" answer with 这要看…了 and 比方说.
- After this lesson you can put information you already share with the listener at the front of the sentence, and agree with a point before adding a "but" using the reaffirmative 是.

## Grammar

### G1 · Topic-comment sentences
**Pattern:** `Topic (known information) + (Subj) + V / comment`

English almost always keeps subject, verb, object in that order. Chinese often starts instead with what both speakers already have in mind, the topic, and then says something about it, the comment. The topic can be something just mentioned (the menu, the fish), or something taken for granted in the situation (your homework, tonight's dinner). Very often the topic is the logical object of the verb, so the object ends up in front of both subject and verb: 功课我做完了. This is not a special emphatic form; it is simply how Chinese conversation flows from old information to new. Learners who keep strict English order are not wrong, but they sound stiff. The one real mistake is to make a new, unknown thing the topic.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 功课我做完了，我们去吃饭吧。 | Gōngkè wǒ zuòwán le, wǒmen qù chīfàn ba. | I've finished my homework, so let's go and eat. |
| 晚饭我们去哪儿吃？ | Wǎnfàn wǒmen qù nǎr chī? | Where shall we go for dinner? |
| 饭钱我已经付了，我们走吧。 | Fànqián wǒ yǐjīng fù le, wǒmen zǒu ba. | I've already paid for the meal. Let's go. |
| 筷子你会用吗？ | Kuàizi nǐ huì yòng ma? | Can you use chopsticks? |

**Watch out:** a new, unspecified thing cannot be the topic: ✗ 一本书我买了 should be 我买了一本书.

### G2 · 一 + verb and 一…就…
**Pattern:** `(Subj) + 一 + V, + second clause` · `(Subj1) + 一 + V1 + (Subj2) + 就 + V2`

You met 一…就… ("as soon as") in Level 2. Two things are new here. First, 一 before a verb, usually a one-syllable verb, can mark a quick, brief action: 一看, 一问, 一尝. Such a clause can never end the sentence; a second clause must follow and tell what you found or what happened as a result. Second, 一…就… can link a condition with what regularly follows from it: whenever A happens, B happens. 就 is needed when the two clauses have different subjects, when there is no pause, and when the first part is a condition; it may only be left out when a pause and another adverb already lead into the second verb. The usual learner error is dropping 就 between two different subjects.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我一问，才知道鱼已经卖完了。 | Wǒ yí wèn, cái zhīdào yú yǐjīng màiwán le. | Only when I asked did I find out the fish was sold out. |
| 我们一到饭馆儿，服务员就给了我们菜单。 | Wǒmen yí dào fànguǎnr, fúwùyuán jiù gěile wǒmen càidān. | As soon as we got to the restaurant, the waiter gave us menus. |
| 马克一吃辣的就要喝很多水。 | Mǎkè yì chī là de jiù yào hē hěn duō shuǐ. | Whenever Mark eats something spicy he has to drink lots of water. |
| 他一回家就开始做饭。 | Tā yì huí jiā jiù kāishǐ zuòfàn. | As soon as he gets home he starts cooking. |

**Watch out:** with two different subjects 就 is required: ✗ 我一到门口，他走了 should be 我一到门口，他就走了.

### G3 · 又…又… (both . . . and . . .) revisited
**Pattern:** `Subj + 又 + Adj1/V1 + 又 + Adj2/V2`

In Level 2 you used 又…又… to join two adjectives that are true at the same time: 又便宜又好吃. Two adjectives joined this way must point in the same direction: both good or both bad. What is new is that 又…又… can also join two verbs, describing two actions going on together, such as talking and laughing or running and jumping. The verbs must happen at the same time, not one after the other, so "first eat, then go" is not 又…又…. Each 又 goes right before its adjective or verb, and no 很 is added. The classic error is joining a good quality with a bad one; for that you need 可是.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这家饭馆儿的菜又便宜又好吃。 | Zhè jiā fànguǎnr de cài yòu piányi yòu hǎochī. | The food at this restaurant is both cheap and good. |
| 这个鸡做得又香又嫩。 | Zhège jī zuò de yòu xiāng yòu nèn. | This chicken is fragrant and tender. |
| 孩子们在公园里又跑又跳。 | Háizimen zài gōngyuán li yòu pǎo yòu tiào. | The children are running and jumping around in the park. |
| 大家又说又笑，吃得很高兴。 | Dàjiā yòu shuō yòu xiào, chī de hěn gāoxìng. | Everyone talked and laughed and enjoyed the meal. |

**Watch out:** a good and a bad quality cannot share 又…又…: ✗ 这家饭馆儿的菜又便宜又不好吃 should be 这家饭馆儿的菜很便宜，可是不好吃.

### G4 · The reaffirmative 是
**Pattern:** `Subj + 是 (stressed) + Adj / V, (可是 / 但是 + …)`

You know that an adjective predicate normally takes no 是: 这个菜很咸, not 这个菜是咸. There is one special use. When someone has just said something, and you want to confirm that it is indeed true, you can put a stressed 是 before the adjective or verb: "it IS salty", "yes, it really is". The adjective or verb must already be in the conversation, so this 是 always responds to something. Very often a "but" clause follows, adding a reservation: yes, it is expensive, but it's worth it. It can also be followed by more evidence. Keep this apart from 是…的 and from "A 是 A，可是…", which you know from Level 2. Used out of the blue, the stressed 是 sounds odd.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这件衣服是很漂亮，可是太贵了。 | Zhè jiàn yīfu shì hěn piàoliang, kěshì tài guì le. | The dress is very pretty, it's true, but it's too expensive. |
| 四川菜是辣，可是辣得很香。 | Sìchuān cài shì là, kěshì là de hěn xiāng. | Sichuan food is spicy, yes, but it's a delicious kind of spicy. |
| 你说得对，自己做饭是省钱。 | Nǐ shuō de duì, zìjǐ zuòfàn shì shěngqián. | You're right, cooking for yourself really does save money. |
| 这个办法是不错，可是太麻烦了。 | Zhège bànfǎ shì búcuò, kěshì tài máfan le. | That method is good, I admit, but it's too much trouble. |

**Watch out:** to simply describe something, leave 是 out: ✗ 今天的鱼是新鲜 should be 今天的鱼很新鲜 (unless you are confirming what someone just said).

## Words & Phrases

### W1 · 正好 (coincidentally; as it happens)
正好 is an adverb. It sits after the subject or a time word and before the verb phrase, and it says that something happens to fit the situation conveniently: you happen to be free just when a friend asks, or happen to be at the door just when someone arrives. It often appears with 也 (正好也想…), with 没 (正好没课), or with 在 (正好在家). It can also open the clause in speech (正好我也要去). It describes a lucky coincidence, not an action in progress.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我明天正好没课，可以跟你一起去。 | Wǒ míngtiān zhènghǎo méi kè, kěyǐ gēn nǐ yìqǐ qù. | As it happens I have no class tomorrow, so I can go with you. |
| 我到宿舍门口的时候，她正好出来。 | Wǒ dào sùshè ménkǒu de shíhou, tā zhènghǎo chūlai. | Just as I got to the dorm entrance, she happened to come out. |
| 你想吃饺子？我正好也想吃。 | Nǐ xiǎng chī jiǎozi? Wǒ zhènghǎo yě xiǎng chī. | You want dumplings? As it happens, so do I. |

**Watch out:** 正好 is not "in the middle of doing": ✗ 他正好吃饭，你等一下再打 should be 他正在吃饭，你等一下再打.

### W2 · 特别是 (especially)
特别是 singles out one member of a group you have just talked about. First comes a general statement, then usually a comma, then 特别是 and a noun or noun phrase: I like Chinese food, especially Sichuan food. The general statement can be positive or negative. 特别是 does not start a conversation and does not come before the general statement. Compare 比方说 (W6): 比方说 gives examples of a statement, while 特别是 picks out the one case where it is most true.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我爱吃中国菜，特别是四川菜。 | Wǒ ài chī Zhōngguó cài, tèbié shì Sìchuān cài. | I love Chinese food, especially Sichuan food. |
| 这儿的菜都很新鲜，特别是鱼。 | Zhèr de cài dōu hěn xīnxian, tèbié shì yú. | All the dishes here are fresh, especially the fish. |
| 我们班的同学都不喜欢考试，特别是马克。 | Wǒmen bān de tóngxué dōu bù xǐhuan kǎoshì, tèbié shì Mǎkè. | Nobody in our class likes exams, Mark least of all. |

**Watch out:** the general statement comes first: ✗ 特别是鱼，这儿的菜都很新鲜 should be 这儿的菜都很新鲜，特别是鱼.

### W3 · 有机 (organic)
有机 is an adjective with a limited job: it can only stand in front of a noun, directly (有机水果) or with 的 when the noun is left out because it is understood (我只买有机的). Unlike most adjectives, it cannot be the predicate on its own, and it cannot take 很. To say that something is organic, wrap it in 是…的: 这些菜是有机的. In questions the same frame is used: …是有机的吗？ It is a common word on shop signs and menus.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这家店卖的都是有机水果。 | Zhè jiā diàn mài de dōu shì yǒujī shuǐguǒ. | All the fruit this shop sells is organic. |
| 有机的太贵了，我不买。 | Yǒujī de tài guì le, wǒ bù mǎi. | The organic ones are too expensive; I'm not buying them. |
| 请问，这个菠菜是有机的吗？ | Qǐngwèn, zhège bōcài shì yǒujī de ma? | Excuse me, is this spinach organic? |

**Watch out:** 有机 needs 是…的 as a predicate: ✗ 这些鸡很有机 should be 这些鸡是有机的.

### W4 · 麻烦 (to trouble; troublesome)
麻烦 works as a verb and as an adjective. As a verb it opens a polite request, a bit like "Could I trouble you to…": 麻烦你 (or 麻烦您) + the thing you want done. It is very common with waiters, shop assistants and strangers. 不麻烦你了 politely turns down an offer of help. As an adjective it means "troublesome, a hassle" and is usually the predicate, after 很, 太 or 真: a task, a procedure or even a person can be 麻烦. Compare 请, which is also polite but sounds more like an instruction; 麻烦你 admits that you are putting the other person out.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 麻烦你帮我们拿一下菜单。 | Máfan nǐ bāng wǒmen ná yíxià càidān. | Could I trouble you to bring us the menu? |
| 不麻烦你了，我自己去拿。 | Bù máfan nǐ le, wǒ zìjǐ qù ná. | Don't trouble yourself, I'll go and get it myself. |
| 自己做饭太麻烦了，我常常在外边吃。 | Zìjǐ zuòfàn tài máfan le, wǒ chángcháng zài wàibian chī. | Cooking for myself is too much hassle, so I often eat out. |

### W5 · 这（就）要看…（了） (it depends on . . .)
This frame gives a flexible answer: the answer to the question hangs on some factor, and you name that factor after 看. What follows 看 can be a noun phrase (你的口味), a whole clause, or a question form such as 你想吃什么 or 天气好不好. 就 and the final 了 are optional; with both, the answer sounds a little more relaxed. The question itself is often repeated in front as a topic: 好不好吃，这要看…. It answers "which is best?" or "will you go?" without committing.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 哪家饭馆儿最好？这要看你想吃什么了。 | Nǎ jiā fànguǎnr zuì hǎo? Zhè yào kàn nǐ xiǎng chī shénme le. | Which restaurant is best? That depends on what you want to eat. |
| 我们明天去不去公园，这就要看天气了。 | Wǒmen míngtiān qù bu qù gōngyuán, zhè jiù yào kàn tiānqì le. | Whether we go to the park tomorrow depends on the weather. |
| 菜辣不辣，这要看你怎么做。 | Cài là bu là, zhè yào kàn nǐ zěnme zuò. | Whether the dish is spicy depends on how you cook it. |

**Watch out:** the verb is 要看, not 是看: ✗ 这是看你的口味 should be 这要看你的口味.

### W6 · 比方说 (for example)
比方说 introduces examples. It comes after a general statement, usually after a comma, and the examples follow it. They can be a short list of nouns, one example with a comment on it, or a series of phrases. In speech it is also used on its own to buy a moment before giving an example. It is a little more colloquial than 比如 (bǐrú), which you may hear too. Unlike 特别是, it does not claim that the example is the strongest case; it just illustrates.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我喜欢吃清淡的菜，比方说清蒸鱼。 | Wǒ xǐhuan chī qīngdàn de cài, bǐfāng shuō qīngzhēng yú. | I like light food, steamed fish for example. |
| 学校附近有很多好饭馆儿，比方说门口那家。 | Xuéxiào fùjìn yǒu hěn duō hǎo fànguǎnr, bǐfāng shuō ménkǒu nà jiā. | There are lots of good restaurants near campus, the one by the gate for instance. |
| 学中文有很多好办法，比方说多听、多说、多跟中国朋友聊天。 | Xué Zhōngwén yǒu hěn duō hǎo bànfǎ, bǐfāng shuō duō tīng, duō shuō, duō gēn Zhōngguó péngyou liáotiān. | There are lots of good ways to learn Chinese: listening a lot, speaking a lot, chatting with Chinese friends. |

**Watch out:** the examples follow 比方说, not the other way round: ✗ 清蒸鱼比方说，我喜欢清淡的菜 should be 我喜欢清淡的菜，比方说清蒸鱼.

## Dialogues

### Dialogue 1 · Too much for three
*Setting: On Friday evening 林月, 马克 and 安娜 go to the new Chinese restaurant across the road from 马克's apartment, and 马克 starts ordering.*

| Speaker | Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 服务员 | 三位晚上好！这是菜单，你们先看看。 | Sān wèi wǎnshang hǎo! Zhè shì càidān, nǐmen xiān kànkan. | Good evening, all three of you! Here's the menu, have a look first. |
| 马克 | 这么多菜！我一看菜单就饿了。请问，你们这儿什么菜做得最好？ | Zhème duō cài! Wǒ yí kàn càidān jiù è le. Qǐngwèn, nǐmen zhèr shénme cài zuò de zuì hǎo? | So many dishes! Just looking at the menu makes me hungry. Excuse me, what's your best dish? |
| 服务员 | 我们的饺子和鱼都很好吃，特别是清蒸鱼。鱼是今天早上刚送来的，又新鲜又嫩。 | Wǒmen de jiǎozi hé yú dōu hěn hǎochī, tèbié shì qīngzhēng yú. Yú shì jīntiān zǎoshang gāng sònglai de, yòu xīnxian yòu nèn. | Our dumplings and our fish are both very good, especially the steamed fish. The fish only arrived this morning; it's fresh and tender. |
| 马克 | 好，清蒸鱼来一个，饺子来两盘，芥兰来一盘，还有…… | Hǎo, qīngzhēng yú lái yí ge, jiǎozi lái liǎng pán, jièlán lái yì pán, háiyǒu… | OK, one steamed fish, two plates of dumplings, a plate of Chinese broccoli, and… |
| 林月 | 等一下，你开玩笑吧？我们只有三个人，你叫这么多菜，吃得完吗？ | Děng yíxià, nǐ kāi wánxiào ba? Wǒmen zhǐ yǒu sān ge rén, nǐ jiào zhème duō cài, chī de wán ma? | Hold on, you're joking, right? There are only three of us. Can we finish all that? |
| 马克 | 吃不完可以带回去啊，明天的午饭就有了。 | Chī bu wán kěyǐ dài huiqu a, míngtiān de wǔfàn jiù yǒu le. | What we can't finish we can take home. Then tomorrow's lunch is sorted. |
| 安娜 | 饺子就别叫了，我们要一个菠菜汤吧。麻烦你，菜别做得太咸，油也少放一点儿。 | Jiǎozi jiù bié jiào le, wǒmen yào yí ge bōcài tāng ba. Máfan nǐ, cài bié zuò de tài xián, yóu yě shǎo fàng yìdiǎnr. | Forget the dumplings, let's get a spinach soup. And could you please not make the food too salty, and go easy on the oil? |
| 服务员 | 没问题。我们的菠菜是有机的，汤做得很清淡。还要别的吗？ | Méi wèntí. Wǒmen de bōcài shì yǒujī de, tāng zuò de hěn qīngdàn. Hái yào biéde ma? | No problem. Our spinach is organic, and we make the soup nice and light. Anything else? |
| 林月 | 麻烦你先给我们三杯热茶，再多拿几张餐巾。 | Máfan nǐ xiān gěi wǒmen sān bēi rè chá, zài duō ná jǐ zhāng cānjīn. | Could we have three cups of hot tea first, please, and a few extra napkins? |
| 服务员 | 好的，马上来。真对不起，我刚才问了一下，清蒸鱼今天已经卖完了。要不要换一个香辣鸡？我们的鸡做得也很香。 | Hǎo de, mǎshàng lái. Zhēn duìbuqǐ, wǒ gāngcái wènle yíxià, qīngzhēng yú jīntiān yǐjīng màiwán le. Yào bu yào huàn yí ge xiāng là jī? Wǒmen de jī zuò de yě hěn xiāng. | Sure, coming right up. I'm so sorry, I've just checked and the steamed fish is sold out today. Would you like the spicy chicken instead? Our chicken is delicious too. |
| 马克 | 啊？那好吧，就换香辣鸡。下次来，我一进门就先叫清蒸鱼！ | Á? Nà hǎo ba, jiù huàn xiāng là jī. Xià cì lái, wǒ yí jìn mén jiù xiān jiào qīngzhēng yú! | What? Oh well, the spicy chicken then. Next time, the moment I walk in I'm ordering the steamed fish first! |

### Dialogue 2 · Which food is best?
*Setting: The next evening 林月 and 马克 video-call 陈乐 on WeChat while 马克 eats the leftovers from the restaurant.*

| Speaker | Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 陈乐 | 林月，马克，你们好！马克，你在吃什么呢？ | Lín Yuè, Mǎkè, nǐmen hǎo! Mǎkè, nǐ zài chī shénme ne? | Hi, Lin Yue, Mark! Mark, what are you eating? |

## Exercises

### E1 · Choose the right word {type=choice}
1. 你也想去？我___也想去看看那家饭馆儿。 [特别 | 正好 | 比较] → 正好
2. 这儿的水果都很新鲜，___苹果。 [一般 | 特别是 | 正好] → 特别是
3. 这些水果都___有机的。 [很 | 太 | 是] → 是
4. 我一下课___去饭馆儿找你。 [才 | 就 | 再] → 就
5. 这个汤又清淡___好喝。 [也 | 又 | 还] → 又
6. 哪儿的菜最好吃？这___要看你的口味了。 [才 | 再 | 就] → 就
7. ___你帮我拿几张餐巾。 [考虑 | 麻烦 | 比方说] → 麻烦
8. 你说得对，这家饭馆儿的菜___很地道，可是太贵了。 [是 | 才 | 再] → 是

### E2 · Put the words in order {type=order}
1. 他一吃辣的 / 就 / 喝 / 很多水 → 他一吃辣的就喝很多水。
2. 做得 / 又香又嫩 / 这家饭馆儿的 / 鸡 → 这家饭馆儿的鸡做得又香又嫩。
3. 是 / 有机的 / 这些菠菜 / 都 → 这些菠菜都是有机的。
4. 特别是 / 马克 / 都爱吃辣的 / 我们 → 我们都爱吃辣的，特别是马克。
5. 口味了 / 好不好吃 / 你的 / 这就要看 → 好不好吃，这就要看你的口味了。
6. 麻烦 / 你 / 帮我 / 换一双筷子 → 麻烦你帮我换一双筷子。
7. 门口 / 我出去的时候 / 在 / 他正好 → 我出去的时候，他正好在门口。

### E3 · Translate into Chinese {type=translate}
1. As it happens, I don't have class tomorrow. → 我明天正好没有课。
2. This fish is both fresh and tender. → 这条鱼又新鲜又嫩。
3. Could I trouble you to bring us two more pairs of chopsticks? → 麻烦你再给我们拿两双筷子。
4. Please don't make the food too salty, and use less oil. → 菜别做得太咸，少放一点儿油。
5. Which one is better depends on your personal taste. → 哪个好，这要看你的口味了。
6. As soon as he gets to the restaurant, he looks at the menu. → 他一到饭馆儿就看菜单。
7. (Agreeing with a friend) Yes, the vegetables here really are fresh, but they're a bit expensive. → 这儿的菜是很新鲜，可是有点儿贵。
8. Is this chicken organic? → 这个鸡是有机的吗？

### E4 · Match the word {type=match}
1. 菜单 → menu
2. 筷子 → chopsticks
3. 餐巾 → napkin
4. 清淡 → light in flavor
5. 咸 → salty
6. 辣 → spicy
7. 新鲜 → fresh
8. 考虑 → to consider
