# Lesson 7 · 电脑和网络 · Computers and the Internet

## Objectives
- After this lesson you can name what you use the Internet for (news, reference material, games, blogs, takeout, shopping) and weigh its advantages and disadvantages.
- After this lesson you can say whether circumstances allow you to do something with V得了 / V不了 and keep it apart from 不能 ("not allowed").
- After this lesson you can complain that someone's carelessness caused you trouble with 害得 and 结果, ask whether a friend is angry with you, and apologize.
- After this lesson you can say which way something moves in relation to a point of view with 过来 and 过去.
- After this lesson you can tell a short story whose sentences hang together, and react to what you see and hear with 看起来 and 听起来.

## Grammar

### G1 · Potential complements (III): V得了 / V不了
**Pattern:** `V + 得了 / 不了` · compare: `(不)能 + V`

You already know potential complements such as 听不懂 or 回得来, which say whether a particular result can be reached. 得了 and 不了 (read liǎo here, not le) do not name a result; they say whether the action can happen at all, because time, health, money or other circumstances allow it or not. The form is mostly negative: 我明天去不了 means "I won't be able to go tomorrow". It is close to 不能去 but more conversational. Be careful: 不能 can also mean "not allowed" or "shouldn't", while V不了 simply says it is impossible. In questions, 你来得了吗？ is open, 你来不了吗？ expects a "no", and 来得了来不了 asks neutrally. An object follows the whole verb: 吃不了这么多.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我发烧了，明天的考试可能考不了了。 | Wǒ fāshāo le, míngtiān de kǎoshì kěnéng kǎo bu liǎo le. | I've got a fever, so I may not be able to take tomorrow's exam. |
| 这么多菜，我们三个人吃得了吗？ | Zhème duō cài, wǒmen sān ge rén chī de liǎo ma? | Can the three of us really eat this many dishes? |
| 宿舍的网络有问题，这两天上不了网。 | Sùshè de wǎngluò yǒu wèntí, zhè liǎng tiān shàng bu liǎo wǎng. | Something is wrong with the dorm network, and we haven't been able to get online for two days. |
| 你星期六来得了来不了？ | Nǐ xīngqīliù lái de liǎo lái bu liǎo? | Will you be able to come on Saturday or not? |

**Watch out:** 不 goes between the verb and 了, never in front: ✗ 我不去得了 should be 我去不了.

### G2 · Connecting sentences (II): linking with time, place and nouns
**Pattern:** `…。 + time word / place word / noun or pronoun + (Subj) + …` (the linking word opens the new clause)

In Lesson 1 you tied reasons together; now the job is telling a story. A string of correct sentences that all start with 我 or 他 reads like a list. Chinese links them by opening the next clause with a time word (那时候, 第二天, 后来), a place word (饭馆里, 宿舍门口) or a noun or pronoun that picks up something just mentioned (那个网站, 这本杂志). In first position the word works as a bridge: "and at that point…", "and in there…", "and that magazine…". The subject follows it, or is left out when it is obvious. Put the same phrase after the subject and it only dates or places the action; the link is lost and the story sounds choppy. That misplacement is the usual learner error.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我们走进了一家小饭馆。饭馆里人很多，我们等了半个小时。 | Wǒmen zǒujìnle yì jiā xiǎo fànguǎn. Fànguǎn li rén hěn duō, wǒmen děngle bàn ge xiǎoshí. | We went into a small restaurant. It was packed, and we waited half an hour. |
| 昨天我十二点才离开图书馆。那时候，外面一个人也没有。 | Zuótiān wǒ shí'èr diǎn cái líkāi túshūguǎn. Nà shíhou, wàimian yí ge rén yě méiyǒu. | Yesterday I didn't leave the library until midnight. By then there wasn't a soul outside. |
| 安娜买了一本中文杂志。这本杂志很有意思，她一个晚上就看完了。 | Ānnà mǎile yì běn Zhōngwén zázhì. Zhè běn zázhì hěn yǒu yìsi, tā yí ge wǎnshang jiù kànwán le. | Anna bought a Chinese magazine. It was so interesting that she finished it in one evening. |

**Watch out:** after the subject, a time word only dates the action and does not link: 他到了北京。他第二天去了长城 is correct but sounds choppy in a story; better: 他到了北京。第二天，他去了长城.

### G3 · 过来 and 过去 (to come over / to go over)
**Pattern:** `V + 过来` (toward the standpoint) · `V + 过去` (away from the standpoint)

过 means crossing a space. 过来 is movement toward the point the speaker takes as the standpoint; 过去 is movement away from it. Usually the standpoint is where the speaker is, but it can also be a place the sentence focuses on or the person being talked to. Both can stand alone (你过来！我过去) or follow a verb of motion or handling: 走, 跑, 拿, 送, 寄. With 把 the thing moved comes before the verb (把书拿过来). The choice depends on the reference point, not on who is moving, so ask "toward here or away from here?" Learners who think only about the mover often pick the wrong one.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 林月在门口叫我，我马上走了过去。 | Lín Yuè zài ménkǒu jiào wǒ, wǒ mǎshàng zǒule guoqu. | Lin Yue called me from the door, and I went straight over. |
| 你过来一下，我给你看一个有意思的网站。 | Nǐ guòlai yíxià, wǒ gěi nǐ kàn yí ge yǒu yìsi de wǎngzhàn. | Come over here a moment, I want to show you an interesting website. |
| 他一看见我们，就急忙跑了过来。 | Tā yí kànjiàn wǒmen, jiù jímáng pǎole guolai. | As soon as he saw us, he hurried over. |
| 这本杂志你看完了吗？看完了请给安娜送过去。 | Zhè běn zázhì nǐ kànwán le ma? Kànwán le qǐng gěi Ānnà sòng guoqu. | Have you finished this magazine? If so, please take it over to Anna. |

**Watch out:** something coming to you comes "over here": ✗ 你把杂志拿过去给我看看 should be 你把杂志拿过来给我看看.

## Words & Phrases

### W1 · 从…到… (from ... to ...)
从…到… marks a range: the starting point follows 从 and the end point follows 到. The range can be time (从早到晚), place (从宿舍到图书馆), stage of life (从小到大) or a list of things (从衣服到外卖). The whole phrase comes before the verb, often followed by 都 to say that everything in the range is included. With places, the sentence often goes on to give the time or distance between them (从这儿到那儿要走十分钟). Some pairs are fixed and short: 从早到晚, not 从早上到晚上 in that set phrase.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 安娜从小到大都喜欢踢足球。 | Ānnà cóng xiǎo dào dà dōu xǐhuan tī zúqiú. | Anna has loved playing soccer ever since she was little. |
| 从宿舍到图书馆走路只要五分钟。 | Cóng sùshè dào túshūguǎn zǒulù zhǐ yào wǔ fēnzhōng. | It's only a five-minute walk from the dorm to the library. |
| 这个网站上的东西，从衣服到外卖，什么都有。 | Zhège wǎngzhàn shang de dōngxi, cóng yīfu dào wàimài, shénme dōu yǒu. | This website has everything, from clothes to takeout. |

**Watch out:** the range goes before the verb: ✗ 他上网从早到晚 should be 他从早到晚都在上网.

### W2 · 结果 (as a result)
As a conjunction, 结果 opens the second clause and reports what actually came of the first: often an outcome nobody planned, and often an unwelcome one. It belongs in storytelling ("I meant to…, and in the end…"). That makes it different from 所以, which states a logical consequence and pairs with 因为; 结果 does not pair with 因为. 结果 is also a noun, "result" or "outcome", as in 考试的结果. The conjunction usually sits at the head of its clause, before the subject if there is one.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我想在网上找点儿资料，结果看了两个小时新闻。 | Wǒ xiǎng zài wǎngshàng zhǎo diǎnr zīliào, jiéguǒ kànle liǎng ge xiǎoshí xīnwén. | I meant to look up some material online and ended up reading the news for two hours. |
| 我们急忙跑到车站，结果车已经开走了。 | Wǒmen jímáng pǎo dào chēzhàn, jiéguǒ chē yǐjīng kāizǒu le. | We rushed to the bus stop, but the bus had already left. |
| 这次考试的结果明天才知道。 | Zhè cì kǎoshì de jiéguǒ míngtiān cái zhīdào. | We won't know the results of this exam until tomorrow. |

**Watch out:** do not pair 结果 with 因为: ✗ 因为他睡得太晚，结果迟到了 should be 他睡得太晚，结果迟到了.

### W3 · 害得 (to cause trouble so that, to do harm so that)
害 means "to harm", and 害得 links a cause to the bad consequence it had for someone. The cause comes first: a person, an action or an event. Then 害得 introduces the person who suffered and what happened to them. The sentence carries blame or complaint, so it often comes with 老是 or 又. The consequence must be unwelcome; for neutral or good outcomes use 让 or 使. In casual speech 害 alone can do the same job: 你害我等了一个小时.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你昨天晚上一直玩游戏，害得我也没睡好。 | Nǐ zuótiān wǎnshang yìzhí wán yóuxì, hài de wǒ yě méi shuìhǎo. | You were playing games all last night, so I couldn't sleep properly either. |
| 我的电脑坏了，害得我的功课都没做完。 | Wǒ de diànnǎo huài le, hài de wǒ de gōngkè dōu méi zuòwán. | My computer broke down, and because of that I couldn't finish my homework. |
| 下雨了，害得我们去不了公园。 | Xiàyǔ le, hài de wǒmen qù bu liǎo gōngyuán. | It started raining, which meant we couldn't go to the park. |

**Watch out:** the result after 害得 must be bad: ✗ 你的帮助害得我很高兴 should be 你的帮助让我很高兴.

### W4 · 看起来 (it seems)
看起来 means "judging by how it looks". It introduces an impression or a guess based on what the speaker sees: a person's state, the look of a place, the quality of a thing. It can stand before the subject (看起来他很累) or after it (他看起来很累) with almost no difference. What follows is usually an adjective phrase or a clause, often softened with 好像 or 不太. Since it rests on appearance, it often sets up a contrast with 其实 (qíshí, "actually").

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你看起来很累，昨天晚上又玩游戏了吧？ | Nǐ kàn qǐlai hěn lèi, zuótiān wǎnshang yòu wán yóuxì le ba? | You look tired. Were you playing games again last night? |
| 看起来这个网站不太可靠。 | Kàn qǐlai zhège wǎngzhàn bú tài kěkào. | This website doesn't look very reliable. |
| 这件衣服看起来很贵，其实不贵。 | Zhè jiàn yīfu kàn qǐlai hěn guì, qíshí bú guì. | This piece of clothing looks expensive, but actually it isn't. |

**Watch out:** if you only hear the person, use 听起来: on the phone, ✗ 你看起来很累 should be 你听起来很累.

### W5 · 听起来 (it sounds)
听起来 works like 看起来, but the impression comes from what the speaker hears: someone's voice, a plan, a story, a piece of news. It is very common as a quick reaction to what someone has just said, often with no subject at all: 听起来不错！ It can also come after a subject (你的办法听起来…). What follows is the speaker's own interpretation, so it sounds more tentative than a flat statement. Do not put 我 in front when you mean "it sounds to me": 我听起来 means "I sound".

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 听起来不错，我们周末就去吧！ | Tīng qǐlai búcuò, wǒmen zhōumò jiù qù ba! | Sounds good, let's go this weekend! |
| 你说的那个软件听起来很有用。 | Nǐ shuō de nàge ruǎnjiàn tīng qǐlai hěn yǒuyòng. | The software you mentioned sounds really useful. |
| 他在电话里听起来不太高兴。 | Tā zài diànhuà li tīng qǐlai bú tài gāoxìng. | He didn't sound very happy on the phone. |

**Watch out:** ✗ 我听起来很好 (I sound fine) should be 听起来很好 (that sounds good).

## Dialogues

### Dialogue 1 · Late again
*Setting: On Saturday afternoon, 林月 and 安娜 are waiting outside a karaoke place near campus for 马克, who is forty minutes late and not answering his phone.*

| Speaker | Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 安娜 | 都三点四十了，马克怎么还不来？我给他打了三次电话，他都不接。 | Dōu sān diǎn sìshí le, Mǎkè zěnme hái bù lái? Wǒ gěi tā dǎle sān cì diànhuà, tā dōu bù jiē. | It's already three-forty. Why isn't Mark here yet? I've called him three times and he hasn't picked up once. |
| 林月 | 你看，半个小时以前，他还在博客上写东西呢。看起来他一直在家上网。 | Nǐ kàn, bàn ge xiǎoshí yǐqián, tā hái zài bókè shang xiě dōngxi ne. Kàn qǐlai tā yìzhí zài jiā shàngwǎng. | Look, half an hour ago he was still posting on his blog. It looks like he's been online at home the whole time. |
| 安娜 | 在家上网？那他为什么不接电话？ | Zài jiā shàngwǎng? Nà tā wèishénme bù jiē diànhuà? | Online at home? Then why isn't he answering his phone? |
| 林月 | 他一玩游戏，就什么都听不见了。哎，那不是他吗？马克，我们在这儿，快过来！ | Tā yì wán yóuxì, jiù shénme dōu tīng bu jiàn le. Āi, nà bú shì tā ma? Mǎkè, wǒmen zài zhèr, kuài guòlai! | Once he starts playing a game, he doesn't hear a thing. Hey, isn't that him? Mark, we're over here, hurry up! |
| 马克 | 对不起，对不起！我在家下载一个软件，想下载完了再走，结果等了一个多小时。 | Duìbuqǐ, duìbuqǐ! Wǒ zài jiā xiàzài yí ge ruǎnjiàn, xiǎng xiàzàiwán le zài zǒu, jiéguǒ děngle yí ge duō xiǎoshí. | Sorry, sorry! I was downloading some software at home and wanted to leave once it was done, and it ended up taking over an hour. |
| 安娜 | 你老是迟到！上次看电影也是这样，害得我们在门口站了半个小时。 | Nǐ lǎoshì chídào! Shàngcì kàn diànyǐng yě shì zhèyàng, hài de wǒmen zài ménkǒu zhànle bàn ge xiǎoshí. | You're always late! It was the same last time we went to a movie: you had us standing at the entrance for half an hour. |
| 马克 | 是我不好。林月，你怎么不说话？你是不是生我的气了？ | Shì wǒ bù hǎo. Lín Yuè, nǐ zěnme bù shuōhuà? Nǐ shì bu shì shēng wǒ de qì le? | It's my fault. Lin Yue, why aren't you saying anything? Are you angry with me? |
| 林月 | 我没生气。我只是觉得，你现在一天也离不开电脑了。 | Wǒ méi shēngqì. Wǒ zhǐshì juéde, nǐ xiànzài yì tiān yě lí bu kāi diànnǎo le. | I'm not angry. I just think you can't go a single day without your computer any more. |
| 马克 | 听起来你还是生气了。今天唱卡拉OK我请客，你们想唱什么就唱什么，好不好？ | Tīng qǐlai nǐ háishi shēngqì le. Jīntiān chàng kǎlā ōukēi wǒ qǐngkè, nǐmen xiǎng chàng shénme jiù chàng shénme, hǎo bu hǎo? | Sounds like you're angry after all. Karaoke is on me today, and you can sing whatever you like. OK? |
| 安娜 | 这还差不多。快进去吧，再不进去，就唱不了多长时间了。 | Zhè hái chàbuduō. Kuài jìnqu ba, zài bú jìnqu, jiù chàng bu liǎo duō cháng shíjiān le. | That's more like it. Let's go in. If we don't go in now, we won't get to sing for long. |
| 林月 | 等一下，马克，你的手机先给我。今天唱歌的时候，你不能上网。 | Děng yíxià, Mǎkè, nǐ de shǒujī xiān gěi wǒ. Jīntiān chànggē de shíhou, nǐ bù néng shàngwǎng. | Hold on, Mark. Give me your phone first. No going online while we're singing today. |
| 马克 | 好，给你！以后我不敢再迟到了。 | Hǎo, gěi nǐ! Yǐhòu wǒ bù gǎn zài chídào le. | OK, here you go! I won't dare be late again. |

## Exercises

### E1 · Choose the right word {type=choice}
1. 我明天有考试，晚上的卡拉OK我去___了。 [不了 | 得了 | 不能] → 不了
2. 你看，林月在那儿！我们快走___吧。 [过来 | 过去 | 起来] → 过去
3. 他说五分钟就到，___过了一个小时才来。 [所以 | 因为 | 结果] → 结果
4. 你昨天晚上玩游戏玩到两点，___我也没睡好。 [帮助 | 害得 | 听起来] → 害得
5. 你在电话里___很累，昨天晚上又没睡觉吧？ [看起来 | 结果 | 听起来] → 听起来
6. 你看，这家饭馆的门口那么漂亮，___很贵。 [看起来 | 听起来 | 害得] → 看起来
7. 我从小___大都喜欢玩电脑游戏。 [在 | 到 | 跟] → 到
8. 他玩游戏玩___了，一天不玩就不舒服。 [严重 | 可靠 | 上瘾] → 上瘾

### E2 · Put the words in order {type=order}
1. 离不开 / 他 / 电脑 / 一天也 → 他一天也离不开电脑。
2. 到晚 / 都在 / 他从早 / 玩游戏 → 他从早到晚都在玩游戏。
3. 害得 / 等了半个小时 / 你老是迟到 / 我们 → 你老是迟到，害得我们等了半个小时。
4. 跑了 / 他看见我们 / 过来 / 急忙 → 他看见我们，急忙跑了过来。
5. 结果 / 玩到了两点 / 我想早点儿睡觉 / 玩游戏 → 我想早点儿睡觉，结果玩游戏玩到了两点。
6. 有 / 网络 / 很大的帮助 / 对学习 → 网络对学习有很大的帮助。
7. 拿过来 / 请你 / 杂志 / 把那本 → 请你把那本杂志拿过来。

### E3 · Translate into Chinese {type=translate}
1. I have a lot of homework tonight, so I won't be able to go to the movie. → 今天晚上我功课很多，看不了电影了。
2. He played games all night, and as a result he was late for class this morning. → 他玩了一晚上游戏，结果今天早上上课迟到了。
3. Please take this magazine over to Lin Yue. → 请你把这本杂志给林月送过去。
4. From Monday to Friday I have Chinese class every day. → 从星期一到星期五，我每天都有中文课。
5. Your idea sounds good; let's do it that way. → 你的办法听起来不错，我们就这么做吧。
6. Is the material on the Internet reliable? → 网上的资料可靠吗？
7. In the Internet age, a lot of magazines have fallen behind. → 在网络时代，很多杂志都落伍了。

### E4 · Match the word {type=match}
1. 资料 → reference material
2. 博客 → blog
3. 下载 → to download
4. 软件 → software
5. 外卖 → takeout
6. 上瘾 → to become addicted
7. 免费 → free of charge
8. 落伍 → to be outdated
