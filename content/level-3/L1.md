# Lesson 1 · 开学 · Starting a New Semester

## Objectives
- After this lesson you can talk about the first days of a new semester and use 了 correctly with and without a numbered object.
- After this lesson you can ask and answer when, where, how and with whom something happened with 是…的, including where you were born.
- After this lesson you can single out an exception with 除了…以外，都… and keep it apart from 除了…以外，还….
- After this lesson you can weigh living on and off campus and line up your reasons with 第一、第二, 除了…以外，还因为… and 再说.
- After this lesson you can disagree politely with 不见得.

## Grammar

### G1 · The particle 了 (IV): completed actions, new situations, one action after another
**Pattern:** `Subj + V + 了 + number/measure + Obj` · `Subj + V + Obj + 了` · `V1 + 了 (+ Obj) + V2`

了 does not mean "past tense"; it says that an action has actually taken place, or that a situation is now new. Where the 了 goes depends on the object. If the object carries a number or another description, 了 follows the verb (上了四节课). If the object is bare, 了 usually moves to the end of the sentence (我买书了), unless the object is a specific, known person or thing, or another clause follows. When two actions are chained, 了 after the first one means "once this is done, the next thing happens", and that works for the future too. Sentence-final 了 can also announce a change, as in Level 2. The classic error is a bare object right after 了 with nothing following, which leaves the listener waiting.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 开学第一天，我上了四节课。 | Kāixué dì yī tiān, wǒ shàngle sì jié kè. | On the first day of the semester I had four classes. |
| 我在图书馆看见了我们的中文老师。 | Wǒ zài túshūguǎn kànjiànle wǒmen de Zhōngwén lǎoshī. | I saw our Chinese teacher in the library. |
| 你到了北京就给我打个电话。 | Nǐ dàole Běijīng jiù gěi wǒ dǎ ge diànhuà. | Give me a call as soon as you get to Beijing. |
| 他今年是研究生了。 | Tā jīnnián shì yánjiūshēng le. | He's a graduate student now, as of this year. |

**Watch out:** a bare object right after 了 sounds unfinished: ✗ 我昨天买了书 should be 我昨天买了两本书 or 我昨天买书了.

### G2 · Sentences with 是…的 (I) revisited
**Pattern:** `(是) + time / place / manner / person + V + 的` · negative: `不是 + … + V + 的`

You met 是…的 in Level 2: once both speakers know that something has happened, 是…的 puts the spotlight on a detail, such as when, where, how, with whom, or who did it. A conversation often starts with a 了 question that establishes the event (你回来了？), and then switches to 是…的 to ask about the details (你是什么时候回来的？). What is new at this level: in a positive sentence 是 is often dropped, but in a negative sentence 不是 must stay. If the verb has an object, 的 may come either at the very end or between the verb and the object. Learners tend to use 了 instead, or to leave out 是 in the negative.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 你是什么时候到学校的？ | Nǐ shì shénme shíhou dào xuéxiào de? | When did you get to campus? |
| 他们不是开车来的，是坐飞机来的。 | Tāmen bú shì kāichē lái de, shì zuò fēijī lái de. | They didn't drive here, they flew. |
| 我妈妈是在上海出生的。 | Wǒ māma shì zài Shànghǎi chūshēng de. | My mother was born in Shanghai. |
| 我是在网上买的机票。 | Wǒ shì zài wǎngshàng mǎi de jīpiào. | I bought the plane ticket online. |

**Watch out:** 是 cannot be left out in the negative: ✗ 我不坐飞机来的 should be 我不是坐飞机来的.

### G3 · 除了…以外，都… (except for)
**Pattern:** `除了 + X + 以外，Subj + 都 + …` · compare: `除了 + X + 以外，(Subj +) 还 / 也 + …`

除了…以外 means "not counting X", and the adverb in the second half decides what happens to X. With 都, X is the exception: everyone or everything else shares the property, and X does not ("except for X"). With 还 or 也, which you already know, X is included and something more is added ("besides X, also…"). So the same opening can lead to opposite meanings. A quick test: if you can rephrase the sentence as "X is the only one who doesn't…", you need 都. 以外 can be dropped in speech, and the subject of the second half goes before 都. Learners often choose 还 when they mean an exception.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 除了马克以外，我们都住在校内。 | Chúle Mǎkè yǐwài, wǒmen dōu zhù zài xiàonèi. | Except for Mark, we all live on campus. |
| 除了星期五以外，我每天都有中文课。 | Chúle xīngqīwǔ yǐwài, wǒ měitiān dōu yǒu Zhōngwén kè. | I have Chinese class every day except Friday. |
| 除了这个箱子以外，别的行李都搬进宿舍了。 | Chúle zhège xiāngzi yǐwài, biéde xíngli dōu bānjìn sùshè le. | Apart from this suitcase, all the other luggage has been moved into the dorm. |
| 除了中文以外，林月还会说日文。 | Chúle Zhōngwén yǐwài, Lín Yuè hái huì shuō Rìwén. | Besides Chinese, Lin Yue can also speak Japanese. |

**Watch out:** 都 leaves X out, 还 adds X in: 除了马克以外，我们都去了 (Mark did not go) is not the same as 除了马克以外，安娜也去了 (Mark went too).

### G4 · Connecting sentences (I): giving several reasons
**Pattern:** `因为…，除了…以外，还因为…，再说…` · `第一…，第二…，第三…`

When someone asks "why?", a string of short separate sentences sounds childish. Chinese speakers tie their reasons together. One way is to give the first reason with 因为 (or with 除了因为…以外), add the next with 还因为, and throw in a final point with 再说 ("and besides"), which you know from Level 2. Another way is to number the reasons with 第一, 第二, 第三, which sounds organised and is common when weighing pros and cons. Both ways can be mixed with patterns you already know. The typical slip is to stack reasons with 和, which in Chinese joins nouns, not clauses.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我喜欢住在校内，除了因为上课方便以外，还因为宿舍比较安全。 | Wǒ xǐhuan zhù zài xiàonèi, chúle yīnwèi shàngkè fāngbiàn yǐwài, hái yīnwèi sùshè bǐjiào ānquán. | I like living on campus, not only because it's convenient for classes but also because the dorms are relatively safe. |
| 我不想搬家，因为找公寓太麻烦了，再说现在的房租也不贵。 | Wǒ bù xiǎng bānjiā, yīnwèi zhǎo gōngyù tài máfan le, zàishuō xiànzài de fángzū yě bú guì. | I don't want to move, because looking for a place is too much trouble, and besides, my rent now isn't expensive. |
| 住在校外有三个好处：第一，比较自由；第二，比较省钱；第三，可以自己做饭。 | Zhù zài xiàowài yǒu sān ge hǎochu: dì yī, bǐjiào zìyóu; dì èr, bǐjiào shěngqián; dì sān, kěyǐ zìjǐ zuòfàn. | Living off campus has three advantages: first, you're freer; second, it saves money; third, you can cook for yourself. |

**Watch out:** do not join two reasons with 和: ✗ 因为房租便宜和离学校很近 should be 因为房租便宜，再说离学校也很近.

## Words & Phrases

### W1 · 觉得 (to feel, to think)
觉得 is a verb that introduces either a feeling (cold, tired, happy) or a personal opinion. What follows is an adjective phrase or a whole clause, never a bare noun. For opinions it is the everyday word; 认为 (rènwéi) says much the same but sounds more formal and more decided. To disagree softly, Chinese often negates 觉得 itself: 我不觉得… The question 你觉得…怎么样？ is the standard way to ask someone's view.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 今天上了五节课，我觉得有点儿累。 | Jīntiān shàngle wǔ jié kè, wǒ juéde yǒudiǎnr lèi. | I had five classes today, and I feel a bit tired. |
| 你觉得住在校外怎么样？ | Nǐ juéde zhù zài xiàowài zěnmeyàng? | What do you think about living off campus? |
| 我不觉得这个学期的课很难。 | Wǒ bù juéde zhège xuéqī de kè hěn nán. | I don't think this semester's classes are very hard. |

**Watch out:** an adjective after 觉得 takes 很, not 是: ✗ 我觉得他的中文是好 should be 我觉得他的中文很好.

### W2 · 方便 (convenient)
方便 is an adjective. As a predicate it says that a place, a time or an arrangement is convenient (交通很方便), and with 的 it describes a noun (方便的时间). Asking 你…方便吗？ checks whether something suits the other person, a polite way to make a request. To say that something is convenient for someone, use 对 or put the person first: 这个时间对我很方便, 我明天不方便. Its opposite is 不方便, which can also hint politely that something is awkward.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我家附近有很多商店，买东西很方便。 | Wǒ jiā fùjìn yǒu hěn duō shāngdiàn, mǎi dōngxi hěn fāngbiàn. | There are lots of shops near my home, so shopping is very convenient. |
| 你明天下午方便吗？我想请你帮个忙。 | Nǐ míngtiān xiàwǔ fāngbiàn ma? Wǒ xiǎng qǐng nǐ bāng ge máng. | Are you free tomorrow afternoon? I'd like to ask you a favour. |
| 你方便的时候给我打个电话吧。 | Nǐ fāngbiàn de shíhou gěi wǒ dǎ ge diànhuà ba. | Give me a call when it's convenient for you. |

**Watch out:** the person does not go after 方便: ✗ 这个时间很方便我 should be 这个时间对我很方便.

### W3 · 安全 (safe)
安全 is an adjective. As a predicate it says that a place, a person or an activity is safe (这儿很安全), and it often appears in questions such as …安全吗？ It can also stand directly before a noun without 的 in fixed combinations such as 安全问题 (safety problem), or with 的 before nouns like 办法 or 地方. 安全 can also work as a noun, "safety", as in 注意安全 (be careful). It is a frequent concern when students choose where to live.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 晚上一个人走回宿舍安全吗？ | Wǎnshang yí ge rén zǒuhuí sùshè ānquán ma? | Is it safe to walk back to the dorm alone at night? |
| 学校附近很安全，你别担心。 | Xuéxiào fùjìn hěn ānquán, nǐ bié dānxīn. | The area around the school is very safe, don't worry. |
| 我们得想一个安全的办法。 | Wǒmen děi xiǎng yí ge ānquán de bànfǎ. | We have to think of a safe way to do it. |

### W4 · 省钱 (to save money, to economize)
省钱 is a verb-object compound: 省 means "to save, to spare" and 钱 is its object. Because of that, other words can come between the two parts: 了, a complement, or an amount (省了不少钱, 省了两百块钱). It covers money saved through low prices, cheap living or careful spending, and it often appears after 比较 or 很 to describe a choice (自己做饭比较省钱). It does not mean putting money in the bank.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 自己做饭比在饭馆吃省钱。 | Zìjǐ zuòfàn bǐ zài fànguǎn chī shěngqián. | Cooking for yourself saves money compared with eating in restaurants. |
| 坐公共汽车比开车省不少钱。 | Zuò gōnggòng qìchē bǐ kāichē shěng bù shǎo qián. | Taking the bus instead of driving can save you quite a bit of money. |
| 这个月我省了两百块钱。 | Zhège yuè wǒ shěngle liǎngbǎi kuài qián. | I saved two hundred dollars this month. |

**Watch out:** the amount goes between 省 and 钱: ✗ 我省钱了两百块 should be 我省了两百块钱.

### W5 · 自由 (free, unconstrained)
自由 is mostly an adjective: 住在校外很自由 (living off campus is very free), and with 不 it means restricted (在家里住不太自由). It is also a noun, "freedom", as in 我想要多一点儿自由. Add 地 and it becomes an adverb before a verb, "freely" (自由地安排). It describes having no rules or limits; it does not mean "free of charge", which is 免费 (miǎnfèi).

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 跟爸爸妈妈住在一起，有时候不太自由。 | Gēn bàba māma zhù zài yìqǐ, yǒu shíhou bú tài zìyóu. | Living with your parents, you sometimes don't feel very free. |
| 放假的时候，我想自由地去旅行。 | Fàngjià de shíhou, wǒ xiǎng zìyóu de qù lǚxíng. | During the holidays I want to travel wherever I like. |
| 上大学以后，我觉得自由多了。 | Shàng dàxué yǐhòu, wǒ juéde zìyóu duō le. | Since starting university I feel much freer. |

**Watch out:** 自由 is not "free of charge": ✗ 这张电影票是自由的 should be 这张电影票是免费的.

### W6 · 不见得 (not necessarily)
不见得 works like an adverb: it usually goes after the subject and before the verb or adjective, and it questions a claim without flatly rejecting it. That makes it a polite way to disagree: instead of "that's wrong", you say "that's not necessarily so". It is especially useful against generalisations (all expensive things are good, everyone from a certain place is like this). It can also stand alone as a reply: 那不见得。 Compare 不一定, which is very close in meaning; 不见得 sounds a little more like doubting what the other person said.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 这家饭馆人很多，可是菜不见得好吃。 | Zhè jiā fànguǎn rén hěn duō, kěshì cài bújiànde hǎochī. | This restaurant is crowded, but the food isn't necessarily good. |
| 美国人不见得都喜欢喝咖啡。 | Měiguórén bújiànde dōu xǐhuan hē kāfēi. | Not all Americans necessarily like coffee. |
| 大公寓不见得住得舒服。 | Dà gōngyù bújiànde zhù de shūfu. | A big apartment isn't necessarily comfortable to live in. |

**Watch out:** 不见得 means "not necessarily", not "not": 他不见得会来 leaves open that he may come, but 他不会来 says he won't.

### W7 · 好处 (advantage, benefit)
好处 is a noun meaning "advantage" or "benefit". It can be the subject (这样做的好处很多) or the object (有什么好处？). Its most common frame is X 对 Y 有好处, "X is good for Y", and the negative is X 对 Y 没有好处. The 对 phrase must come before 有, never after 好处. When comparing options, 好处 often pairs with 坏处 (huàichu, disadvantage).

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 住在宿舍有什么好处？ | Zhù zài sùshè yǒu shénme hǎochu? | What are the advantages of living in a dorm? |
| 多走路对身体有好处。 | Duō zǒulù duì shēntǐ yǒu hǎochu. | Walking more is good for your health. |
| 晚上睡得太晚对学习没有好处。 | Wǎnshang shuì de tài wǎn duì xuéxí méiyǒu hǎochu. | Going to bed too late does your studies no good. |

**Watch out:** ✗ 多运动有好处对身体 should be 多运动对身体有好处.

### W8 · 适应 (to adapt, to become accustomed to)
适应 is a verb that needs to say what you are getting used to: a climate, a new life, a different schedule. That object can follow directly (适应这儿的天气) or be moved in front with 对 (对这儿的生活还不太适应). Because adapting takes time, 适应 often appears with 还不, 还没 or 慢慢 to say that it has not happened yet or is happening slowly, and with 了 when it is done. It is close to 习惯 (to be used to), but 适应 stresses the process of adjusting to something new.

| Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|
| 我还不适应这儿的天气。 | Wǒ hái bú shìyìng zhèr de tiānqì. | I'm still not used to the weather here. |
| 他刚来美国，对这儿的生活还不太适应。 | Tā gāng lái Měiguó, duì zhèr de shēnghuó hái bú tài shìyìng. | He has just come to America and hasn't quite adjusted to life here yet. |
| 新生要多长时间才能适应大学生活？ | Xīnshēng yào duō cháng shíjiān cái néng shìyìng dàxué shēnghuó? | How long does it take new students to adjust to university life? |

**Watch out:** an object cannot follow a 得 complement: ✗ 我适应得很好这儿的生活 should be 我对这儿的生活适应得很好 or 我已经适应这儿的生活了.

## Dialogues

### Dialogue 1 · Back from Beijing
*Setting: On the first day of the new semester, 安娜 runs into 林月 and 马克 outside the library and wants to hear all about their summer.*

| 林月 | 回来了。我是上个星期六回来的。 | Huílai le. Wǒ shì shàng ge xīngqīliù huílai de. | I am. I got back last Saturday. |

| 林月 | 玩得很高兴！陈乐有一辆车，常常开车带我们出去玩。我们去了长城，还去了好几个公园。 | Wán de hěn gāoxìng! Chén Lè yǒu yí liàng chē, chángcháng kāichē dài wǒmen chūqu wán. Wǒmen qùle Chángchéng, hái qùle hǎo jǐ ge gōngyuán. | We had a great time! Chen Le has a car and often drove us around. We went to the Great Wall and to quite a few parks. |

| 马克 | 刚到的时候不太适应，天气太热了。过了一个星期就适应了。除了天气以外，别的都很好，特别是吃的！ | Gāng dào de shíhou bú tài shìyìng, tiānqì tài rè le. Guòle yí ge xīngqī jiù shìyìng le. Chúle tiānqì yǐwài, biéde dōu hěn hǎo, tèbié shì chī de! | Not really at first, it was too hot. But after a week I was fine. Apart from the weather, everything was great, especially the food! |
| 林月 | 他每天吃了早饭就问我中午去哪儿吃。 | Tā měitiān chīle zǎofàn jiù wèn wǒ zhōngwǔ qù nǎr chī. | Every day, as soon as he'd finished breakfast, he'd ask me where we were going for lunch. |
| 安娜 | 你们去了那么长时间，我觉得马克好像胖了一点儿！ | Nǐmen qùle nàme cháng shíjiān, wǒ juéde Mǎkè hǎoxiàng pàngle yìdiǎnr! | You were gone so long. I think Mark has put on a bit of weight! |
| 马克 | 那不见得！哎，糟糕，我把给你买的礼物拉在北京了！ | Nà bújiànde! Āi, zāogāo, wǒ bǎ gěi nǐ mǎi de lǐwù là zài Běijīng le! | I wouldn't say that! Oh no, I left the present I bought you in Beijing! |
| 林月 | 别担心，是我帮你带回来的，现在在我的宿舍里。 | Bié dānxīn, shì wǒ bāng nǐ dài huílai de, xiànzài zài wǒ de sùshè li. | Don't worry, I'm the one who brought it back for you. It's in my dorm room. |

### Dialogue 2 · Dorm or apartment?
*Setting: A week into the semester, 林月 finds her dorm too noisy and asks 马克 and 安娜 over lunch whether she should move off campus.*

| Speaker | Chinese | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|---|

## Exercises

### E1 · Choose the right word {type=choice}
1. 除了马克以外，我们___住在校内，只有他住在校外。 [还 | 都 | 也] → 都
2. 除了中文课以外，我这个学期___上历史课。 [都 | 再 | 还] → 还
3. 你是什么时候到北京___？ [的 | 了 | 过] → 的
4. 我不___坐飞机来的，我是开车来的。 [没 | 是 | 要] → 是
5. 明天下___课，我们一起去吃饭吧。 [着 | 的 | 了] → 了
6. 住在校外___省钱，你还得花钱坐车。 [一定 | 不见得 | 比较] → 不见得
7. 常常吃水果___身体有好处。 [对 | 给 | 跟] → 对
8. 安娜刚搬进新宿舍，还不太___那儿的生活。 [方便 | 安全 | 适应] → 适应

### E2 · Put the words in order {type=order}
1. 去过 / 除了我弟弟以外 / 长城 / 我们都 → 除了我弟弟以外，我们都去过长城。
2. 马克 / 出生的 / 是 / 在加州 → 马克是在加州出生的。
3. 吃了 / 他每天 / 去上课 / 早饭 / 就 → 他每天吃了早饭就去上课。
4. 不见得 / 贵的 / 好 / 东西 → 贵的东西不见得好。
5. 对 / 身体 / 早点儿睡觉 / 有好处 → 早点儿睡觉对身体有好处。
6. 一辆 / 楼下 / 出租车 / 有 / 宿舍 → 宿舍楼下有一辆出租车。
7. 方便 / 住在 / 校内 / 上课 / 很 → 住在校内上课很方便。

### E3 · Translate into Chinese {type=translate}
1. When did you get back? → 你是什么时候回来的？
2. My surname is Zhang: the Zhang made of "bow" and "long". → 我姓张，弓长张。
3. Except for Anna, we all went to China this summer. → 除了安娜以外，我们这个夏天都去中国了。
4. Living off campus is not necessarily more convenient. → 住在校外不见得更方便。
5. I came by taxi, not by bus. → 我是坐出租车来的，不是坐公共汽车来的。
6. Oh no, I left my phone in the classroom! → 哎，糟糕，我把手机拉在教室里了！
7. Tomorrow after class I'll come and help you move. → 明天下了课，我来帮你搬家。

### E4 · Match the word {type=match}
1. 开学 → to begin a new semester
2. 新生 → new student
3. 研究生 → graduate student
4. 出生 → to be born
5. 弓 → bow (for archery)
6. 省钱 → to save money
7. 自由 → free, unconstrained
8. 搬家 → to move (one's residence)
